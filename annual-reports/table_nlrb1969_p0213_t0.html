The period after the "A" is not clearly resolved at this scan quality — the "A" sits in its own indent position with a wide gap before "By"; I have written it with a period per the usual typesetting of this table. The scan renders some letters imperfectly (e.g. "Recognition", "Picketing ended", "trial examiner" in the headings), but the readings are unambiguous.
