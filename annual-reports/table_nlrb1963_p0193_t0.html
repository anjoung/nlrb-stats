All five are transcribed with the comma, and each is confirmed by the printed arithmetic (e.g. Indiana 5,553 + 1,114 + 7,287 = 13,954; New England's six states' last column sums to 11,741). Every row and column total printed on this crop was checked and is internally consistent: the three election-outcome columns sum to the Total column in every row, the three "Valid votes cast for—" columns sum to "Total valid votes cast" in every row, and each division line equals the sum of its states in all columns. No misprints were found. Section/division lines (New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic) carry values here and are transcribed as ordinary data rows, as printed.
