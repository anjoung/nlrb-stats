No column headings are printed on this page, so header_rows is empty. The centered line "C. Cases Certified or Dismissed on the Basis of the Record" is transcribed as a section row (label only, second cell empty), following the rule for section lines such as "A. By number of cases involved"; it is a lettered section heading within the appendix list, not a "Table N.—..." caption. The printed list is separated into alphabetical groups (A, B, C, D, E, F) by blank lines; those blank lines are not transcribed as rows. The hyphens in the case numbers are printed as short en-dashes (e.g. "10–R–1861"); they are transcribed as plain hyphens. The list is cut off by the bottom of the page and continues on the following page (the last transcribed entry is 13-R-3918, Field Enterprises, Inc.). Company names are transcribed exactly as printed, including the terminal periods that some entries carry and some do not, the inverted-name style ("Arthur, W., & Co., Inc."; "Banta, George, Publishing Co."), the lowercase start of "du Pont, E. I. de Nemours & Co.", and the spelling "Specialities" in "Danly Machine Specialities Inc.". Doubtful characters: the case numbers are set in a bold face with ink spread, so a few digits are slightly uncertain — in particular 13-R-4095 (Banta, George, Publishing Co.), 16-R-2096 (East Texas Electric Steel Co.), 6-R-1496 (Buckeye Coal Co.) and 10-R-1861 (Acme Boot Mfg.
