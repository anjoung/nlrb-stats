The crop ends after California, below a closing rule, and no footnote texts appear on this page; markers ^1 (on the stub heading) and ^2 (on the CA/CB/CC/CD/RC/RM/RD headings) are printed but their definitions are not on this image. The table is almost certainly continued on the following page: the printed Total row exceeds the sum of the nine divisions shown here by 452 in "All cases" (16,659 printed vs 16,207 summed), and the shortfall decomposes consistently across every column (All C 9,260 vs 8,977; All R 7,399 vs 7,230; CA 208, CB 67, CC 6, CD 2, RC 152, RM 10, RD 7), which is what would be expected if rows for outlying areas (e.g. Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico) follow. Doubtful cell: "New Mexico", column "All cases". I read the printed figure as 213, transcribed as printed. Arithmetic indicates it should be 243: New Mexico's All C (177) + All R (66) = 243, and the Mountain division total of 757 reconciles exactly only with 243 (58+64+25+216+243+89+47+15 = 757). Either the source is a misprint or the middle digit is a 4 degraded in the scan; it is the only cell in the body that fails an internal check. Every other state row and every division subtotal was checked and reconciles exactly (component columns sum to "All C cases" and "All R cases", and those two sum to "All cases"), which confirms the column assignment throughout. Minor scan artifacts, read without doubt: a stray ink speck sits just left of the "1" in Delaware/RM^2; the "1" in South Dakota/RD^2 prints with a broken serif resembling a "J".
