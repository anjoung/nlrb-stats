p. 207. This is not a numeric table but an appendix list of cases, set in two side-by-side panels separated by a vertical rule, each panel holding a case-number column (treated as the stub) and a company-name column. The two panels are merged here: all 24 left-panel entries first (16-C-1530 through 14-C-1296), then the 23 right-panel entries (10-C-2259 through 14-C-1273). No column headings are printed, so header_rows is empty. The two centered lines at the top, "I. Unfair Labor Practice Cases" and "A. NLRA: C cases—charges of unfair labor practices committed by an employer under section 8, prior to amendment", are transcribed as label-only section rows; if the project treats them as the table caption instead, they can be dropped. The second was printed in italic with the em dash after "C cases". Blank spacer lines separating the alphabetical groups (A/B/C/D/E/F/G, H/I/J/L/M/N/O) carry no text and are not transcribed as rows. Row labels broken by end-of-line hyphens were rejoined ("Manu-facturing", "Manufactur-ing", "Weld-ing", "Mar-ket"). Case-number dashes are printed as en dashes; written here as plain hyphens. Uncertain/notable cells: the regional prefixes "16E-C-0001" (Hicks Hayward Co.), "15M-C-0021" (Lingle Refrigerator Co., Inc.) and "23-C-0068" (Metropolitan Meat Market) carry leading zeros and letter suffixes as printed. "Eastman Cotton Mills" (10-C-2215) ends without the terminal period most other entries carry. Small stray ink marks appear in the right margin near the top, beside "Bentley, J. A., Lumber" and to the left of the Illinois Bell entry; these are scan artifacts, not footnote markers. The crop ends mid-list at "14-C-1273 Opelika Textile Mills, Inc.", so the list continues on the following page; the left panel's final entry (14-C-1296 Granite City Steel Co.) also sits at the very bottom edge and may be followed by further lines cut off by the crop.
