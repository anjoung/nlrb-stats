Reader’s notes

p. 274. Eight printed columns: stub, Total, and six columns under the spanner "Identification of filing party". The stub column has no printed heading; "Total" is a tall heading spanning both heading levels. The seven centred section lines ("CA cases" … "CP cases") carry no values. Every "Pending October 1, 1981" row carries a raised asterisk printed immediately before the Total figure (e.g. *17,693); transcribed as a trailing marker, 17,693^*. In all seven sections the six filing-party columns sum exactly to the printed Total for all five rows, which independently confirms the column placement. Punctuation: no sentence-ending periods are visible in either footnote in this scan (after "terms", after "Revised", after "Annual Report"); the source almost certainly printed them but they have dropped out of the 1-bit image, so the footnotes are given as rendered. Minor scan artifacts, none affecting a value: a stray speck sits between the "Other national unions" and "Other local unions" columns on the CD "On docket fiscal 1982" row; a stray mark appears in the left margin beside the caption; the dot of the letter "i" is frequently lost in the row labels ("Pendıng", "Receıved", "fılıng"), rendered here with normal spelling.
