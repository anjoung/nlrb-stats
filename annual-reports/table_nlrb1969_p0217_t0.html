Suspected misprint: Ohio, "All C cases" is printed as 1,162, but it is inconsistent with the rest of the table in two independent ways — the Ohio row components (761 + 236 + 65 + 72 + 1 + 17) sum to 1,152, and the East North Central subtotal of 4,528 requires 1,152 for Ohio (480 + 1,533 + 1,033 + 330 = 3,376). Ohio's "All cases" of 1,924 also only works with 1,152. The glyph read as 6 is most likely a scanned 5, but it is transcribed as printed per instruction. Illinois, "All C cases" is typeset as "1 533" with a space where the comma belongs (comma faint or dropped in the scan); written as 1,533. Kansas, "All cases": the leading digit is broken/lowered in the scan and could be read as a subscript, but it is a 2 — 272 is confirmed by the West North Central subtotal (2,468) and by the row components (164 + 106 + 2 = 272). Row labels "East North Central" and "West North Central" are each printed on two lines and joined here into one row each. Leader dots after row labels dropped. Every other row and column in the table cross-foots exactly against its printed subtotal.

Two readings worth flagging, both confirmed by those checks: West Virginia column 8 is 77 (far larger than its neighbours, but 137+62+37+7+0+77 = 320 = its column 2), and Oklahoma column 14 is 0, preceded on the scan by a small stray speck that is dirt, not a digit or footnote marker (the West South Central column 14 subtotal of 9 = 3+3+0+3 requires Oklahoma to be 0). In footnote 2 the abbreviation is transcribed as printed, "U S"; any periods are lost in the scan.
