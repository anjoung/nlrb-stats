It continues a table begun on the previous page, so no column headings are printed on this image and header_rows is empty. The table also continues past the bottom of this page (Region 12 is the last entry shown; Regions 1-5 are on the preceding page and later regions follow). Column rules are not printed; the three columns are set off by white space only, but the column assignment of every entry is unambiguous.
