AFL-CIO: 122 + 38 = 160 and 18,751 + 7,142 = 25,893), and the Total line sums to 4,946 and 462,712. No printed figure appears to be a misprint. "4-union elections" shows no such mark. These marks are punctuation, not footnote references. CIO", are run together on one line that carries values; it is kept as one row label.
