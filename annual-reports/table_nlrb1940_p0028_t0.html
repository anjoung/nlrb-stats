Reader’s notes

p. 28. 22 columns: stub, the single "Total number of unfair labor-practice cases pending, June 30, 1939" column, 15 columns under "Number of cases alleging specific unfair labor practices (subsections of sec. 8 of the act)", and 5 columns under "Total number of cases". The 15 specific-practice columns are heavily sparse, with most empty cells printed as dash rules; all of these are transcribed as "". The whole grid was cross-checked arithmetically and is internally consistent: every region's 15 specific-practice cells sum to its printed total; each of the five "Total number of cases" figures equals the sum of the specific-practice columns that contain that subsection; and all 21 numeric columns foot exactly to the printed Total line (e.g. 8 (1) and 8 (3) = 1,004; 8 (2) total = 708; 8 (3) total = 2,131; 8 (4) total = 70; 8 (5) total = 1,233; the Total column = 2,902). No printed figure appears to be in error. Doubtful glyphs, all resolved by those checks: "17. Kansas City", Total column and 8 (1) column — the middle digit is broken and could be read as 0, giving "108"; it is 168 (the Total column foots to 2,902 only with 168, and 30+30+71+22+8+1+2+3+1 = 168). "8. Cleveland", column "8 (1)" — the degraded glyph reads 5 (confirmed by the row sum of 110). "16. Fort Worth", "Total number of cases" columns 8 (3) and 8 (4) — printed faintly as 56 and 6 (not 50 and 0); both confirmed by the column totals. Because several lone 1s and 2s sit in the narrow sparse columns where visual alignment is tight, the exact column of each was fixed by the row and column sums; notable cases: "9. Cincinnati" has 2 under "8 (1), 8 (3), and 8 (4)" and 2 under "8 (1), 8 (2), 8 (3), and 8 (4)" (its 8 (5) total of 67 leaves no room for a value under any 8 (5) combination); "15. New Orleans" has 1 under "8 (1), 8 (3), and 8 (4)" and 1 under "8 (1), 8 (2), and 8 (4)". The top right corner of the header box, above "8 (5)" in the "Total number of cases" block, carries a dark scan artifact (an ink smudge or stray handwritten mark) overlaying the blank heading space; it is not printed table text and is not transcribed.
