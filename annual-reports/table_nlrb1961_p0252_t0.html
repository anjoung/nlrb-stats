p. 252. Six printed columns: stub (Case No), Name of complainant, Name of union, and the three sub-columns Granted / Denied / Pending under the spanner "Disposition of injunctions". Section lines: the two statutory headings "8(b)(4)(A)" and "8(b)(4)(B)" are printed in italic, centred over the "Name of complainant" column rather than in the stub; I placed each in cell 0 as a label-only row per the grid convention. Continuation: the table is not finished on this page — the body ends mid-list at "3-CC-130 Watson Warehouse, Inc" and the line "See footnote at end of table" appears below the last rule, so the single asterisk footnote itself is printed on a later page and is not visible in this crop. I recorded that reference line in `footnotes`; it carries no marker of its own. Raised asterisks: a small raised * follows a number of union names (Locals 348, 505, 83, 901, 630, 886, 107, 516, the Construction Trades Council entry, and "Plant Guard Workers U of America"). I treated all of them as footnote markers and wrote them as ^*. Punctuation: this is a 1-bit scan in which the periods of abbreviations have largely dropped out, leaving a space before the following comma ("I B E W , Local 59", "Houston Armored Car Co , Inc"). I transcribed the letters as legible and closed up the stray space before the comma; the original almost certainly reads "I. B. E. W., Local 59" etc. Doubtful cells: - Row "4-CC-163" (Leonard Shaffer Co), Name of union: the local number in "Hotel & Restaurant Employees, Local 568" is blurred; the last digit could be 8 or 9. - Row "23-CC-72-73", Name of complainant: printed across a line break as "Pig" / "gley Wiggly", which joins to "Piggley Wiggly". This is very likely a misprint for the grocery chain "Piggly Wiggly"; transcribed as printed. - Row "12-CC-112" (Tampa Sand & Material Co), Name of union: "I B E W, Local 2017" — the four digits are faint and the leading 2 is the least certain. - Row "17-CC-122" (Siebler Heating), Name of union: reads "Sheetmetal Workers, Local 3"; a trailing digit, if any, is lost at the column rule. - Row "12-CC-123" (Martin Company): "I B E W, Local 756" — the final digit sits against leader dots and could be 6 or 8. Dash rules ("-------") appear in many Granted/Denied cells and are transcribed as empty strings; the only marks of substance in those three columns are the X's.
