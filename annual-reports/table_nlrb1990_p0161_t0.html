p. 161. 17 columns: stub, "All cases", 8 unfair-labor-practice columns (All C cases, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), 4 representation columns (All R cases, RC, RM, RD), and UD, AC, UC. The stub heading carries a raised "2" ("Division and State^2"); the caption's footnote marker and any footnotes are outside this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. The crop is cut off at the bottom: the table continues past the Maryland row (the South Atlantic division and the remaining divisions are on the rest of the page/next page), and the Delaware/Maryland rows are the start of an unlabeled division block whose subtotal is not shown. Doubtful cell: Nebraska, UC column. It reads as a printed 0, and that is what is transcribed, but two arithmetic checks suggest the printed digit may be 2: (a) Nebraska's components sum to 99 (65 C + 34 R + 0 UD + 0 AC + 0 UC) against a printed "All cases" of 101; (b) the UC column for the seven West North Central states sums to 22 against the printed division total of 24. Every other row and every other division subtotal on this crop adds up exactly, so this single cell is the only inconsistency. Division subtotal rows (New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central) are transcribed in place as ordinary rows with their values.

p. 162. This is a continuation page of Table 6A; the table runs on from the previous page and continues past the bottom of the crop (the Mountain division subtotal is the last printed row, and the body is cut off below it). No footnote texts are printed on this crop, so `footnotes` is empty, even though footnote markers 1 (in the excluded caption) and 2 (on the stub heading "Division and State") appear. The "South Atlantic" subtotal does not equal the sum of the seven states shown here (e.g. All cases 4,514 vs. 3,729 for DC through Florida; All C cases 3,758 vs. 3,116). This is expected rather than a misprint: the South Atlantic division also includes Delaware and Maryland, which are printed on the preceding page. Every other subtotal on this crop (East South Central, West South Central, Mountain) adds up exactly across all 16 numeric columns, and each state's row is internally consistent (CA+CB+CC+CD+CE+CG+CP = All C; All C + All R + UD + AC + UC = All cases). Header structure: three heading levels. The spanner level carries "Unfair labor practice cases" over the eight C columns and "Representation cases" over the four R columns; the stub, "All cases", and the three right-hand headings are tall and repeated down the levels, with the two-letter codes UD, AC and UC printed on the bottom level only. The left-hand C and R column headings ("All C cases", "CA" ... "RD") occupy the middle level and are repeated into the bottom level because they span it. Print quality is good and all digits are legible; the dotted leaders after state names and the stray leader marks in the stub column (e.g. after "East South Central") were dropped.
