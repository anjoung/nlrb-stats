p. 198. Eight printed columns: stub, "Objections filed", "Objections withdrawn", "Objections ruled upon", then four columns under the spanner "Disposition of objections ruled upon" (Overruled: Number, Percent; Sustained^2: Number, Percent). Decimal points in several percent cells are very faint or dropped in the scan, printing as a gap: "65 2", "34 8" (All elections), "64 6", "35 4" (RC elections), "17 6" (RM elections). These are decimal fractions in the same style as the clearly printed "82.4", "61.9" and "38.1" in the same columns, so they are transcribed as 65.2, 34.8, 64.6, 35.4 and 17.6. The digits themselves are legible; only the points are doubtful. Footnote 1 is printed without a terminal period. The scan renders many dotless "i" glyphs (e.g. "Objectıons", "electıons"); these are reproduced as ordinary "i". The caption carries footnote marker 1, which is not reproduced here per instructions.
