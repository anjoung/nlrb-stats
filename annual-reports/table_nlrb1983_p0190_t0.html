p. 190. 17 columns: stub, "All cases", 8 unfair-labor-practice columns (All C cases, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), 4 representation columns (All R cases, RC, RM, RD), then UD, AC, UC. The three rightmost headings ("Union deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases", "Unit clarification cases") are tall boxes sitting above a third heading level that holds only UD, AC and UC; they are repeated into the levels they span, as are "All cases" and the other single-level column labels. Suspected misprint: Minnesota, column CB reads "34" as printed, but it does not cross-foot. Minnesota's own row sums to All C cases = 406 rather than the printed 426 (which is itself consistent with the printed All cases of 639), and the West North Central CB column sums to 464 rather than the printed 484. Both discrepancies are exactly 20, so the printed value was most likely 54 with a broken or filled-in "5". Transcribed as printed ("34"); every other cell in the crop cross-foots in both directions (each state row's C components to All C cases, each row's components to All cases, and each state column to its division total). The crop is cut off below the "West North Central" line: the column rules continue past the bottom edge, so the table continues (further divisions and the national total) on this or a following page, and any footnote text for markers 1 and 2 falls outside the crop. The caption carries footnote marker 1 and the stub heading carries marker 2; no footnote text is printed on this image, so `footnotes` is empty. Scan artifacts: the typeface renders many dotted i's faintly (e.g. "Division", "Maine", "Pennsylvania", "Michigan", "Wisconsin", "Missouri", "practice", "Representation", "deauthorization", "certification", "clarification"); these are rendered with normal spelling. Row labels are followed by leader dots that stop just left of the "All cases" column; these have been dropped.
