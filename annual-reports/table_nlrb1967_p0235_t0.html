All printed row sums are internally consistent (each party's five detail columns sum to that party's Total, and the two party totals sum to "Total all"), which supports the digit readings; no misprints detected.

p. 236. Continuation page: no column headings are printed on this crop, so header_rows is empty. The grid has 14 columns (stub + 13 values): a left block of 7 columns and a right block of 6 columns, separated by a double vertical rule but sharing the single stub on the left — this is one panel, not two side-by-side panels. Arithmetic cross-checks confirm the column assignment: in every row with values, columns 3-7 sum to column 2, columns 9-13 sum to column 8, and columns 2 + 8 sum to column 1 (e.g. 3,383+113+168+374+236 = 4,274; 31,920+4,810+7,050+22,420+49,040 = 115,240; 3,171,220+115,240 = 3,286,460). The rows "Employees receiving backpay:" and "Employees reimbursed for fees, dues, and fines:" are label-only lines with no values printed. Dashed rules (which I have transcribed as empty cells) fill the right block for the reinstatement/Accepted/Declined/preferential-hiring-list rows and the left block (columns 2-7) for "Hiring hall rights restored" and "Objections to employment withdrawn". Doubtful readings: (1) the row label at "From either employee or union" (the backpay row) appears to read "employee", not "employer", while the parallel fees row below clearly reads "From either employer or union"; this looks like a misprint in the original, and I have transcribed it as printed. (2) The marker on the second footnote is a small raised glyph I could not resolve between 2 and 3 at this scan resolution; since footnote 1 is the only other note printed, I rendered it as 2. A stray speck of ink sits just above the "115,730" cell in the Backpay row (column 4); it is not a footnote marker or digit.
