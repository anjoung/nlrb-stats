Doubtful cell: Indiana, "Valid votes cast for unions / Other local unions". I read the printed digits as 883 and transcribed them as seen, but there is a small stray mark beside this cell in the scan and two independent arithmetic checks both point to 783 instead: (a) the row's own union subtotal, 8,922 + 1,336 + 1,344 + 883 = 12,485, does not match the printed 12,385 (it matches with 783); (b) the East North Central column total of 3,151 less the other four states (1,388 + 461 + 402 + 117 = 2,368) leaves 783. Indiana's printed row total 12,385 is itself consistent with the East North Central total of 67,801, so the discrepancy is isolated to this one component cell. Treat it as either a misread 7 or a misprint. Every other cell reconciles: in all four divisions shown, the union-win components sum to the win total, wins plus "no representative chosen" equal total elections, the union vote components sum to the union vote total, union votes plus "no union" votes equal total valid votes cast, and each state column sums to its printed division line. Minor rendering points: "Teamsters" and several headings are hyphenated across lines in the original and have been rejoined; the headings over columns 3 and 11 are printed "AFL–CIO unions" with a long hyphen and are written "AFL-CIO unions".
