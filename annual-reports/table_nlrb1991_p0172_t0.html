p. 172. Seven columns: stub, "Cases in which formal actions taken", and five columns under the spanner "Formal actions taken by type of case" (Total, RC, RM, RD, UD). The rule between RD and UD is printed as a double rule; it is a column rule only, not an extra column. Apparent misprints, transcribed as printed: in the first block the "Total formal actions taken" figure is 4 short of the sum of its type-of-case cells — "Hearings completed, total" 874 vs 763+30+81+4=878, and "Initial hearings" 731 vs 633+27+71+4=735 (in both rows the total equals the sum excluding the UD count of 4). Every other row's total agrees with its row sum, and all column sub-sums check out. "Review of Regional Directors' decisions:" and "Regional Directors' decisions" are printed as label-only section lines; "Requests for review received" (325 ...) is the data line printed beneath the first of them, not a continuation of it. The section line printed as "Outcome." ends with a period in the scan (likely a broken colon); transcribed as printed. "Transferred by Regional Directors for initial decision" is printed over two lines with the end-of-line hyphen in "de-cision"; joined here. The caption carries a superscript footnote marker 1, but no footnote text appears within this crop (it is presumably below the cropped area), so `footnotes` is empty. The scan's typeface renders dotted i's faintly (e.g. "Initial", "Hearings", "Decisions", "Directors"); spellings are given in standard form. No cell was illegible.

p. 173. Seven columns: stub, "Cases in which formal actions taken", then the five columns under the spanner "Formal actions taken by type of case" (Total formal actions taken, RC, RM, RD, UD). The rule between RD and UD is doubled, but it is a rule, not an extra column. Arithmetic from the parent "By Board" row confirms the intended row is 475 | 467 | 402 | 7 | 56 | 2: RC 432 = 402+25+5, RM 7 = 7+0+0, RD 58 = 56+2+0, UD 3 = 2+1+0, and Total 500 = 467+28+5, Cases 512 = 475+30+7. The stray leading "D" is scan damage, not a footnote marker. I left the cells as printed per instructions; a consumer will likely want to shift this row right by one.
