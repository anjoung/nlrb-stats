Also note "Dismissals on record" under "By Regional Director" shows a larger "Total formal actions taken" (37) than "Cases in which formal actions taken" (31), unlike the rest of the table. Label details: "Transferred by Regional Directors for initial decision" is followed by a single period where the leader dots are clipped by the column rule; the trailing dot was dropped as a leader. "In directed elections ( after transfer by Regional Director)" is printed with a doubled space and a space after the open parenthesis; normalized to single spacing here. The apostrophe in "Regional Directors'" is set as a typographic right quote in the two "…Exceptions to Regional Directors' reports" rows and as a straight apostrophe elsewhere; a straight apostrophe is used throughout.
