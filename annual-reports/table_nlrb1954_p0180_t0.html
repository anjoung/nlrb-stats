p. 180. Eight columns: stub plus 7 value columns, with "Valid votes cast for losing unions" spanning the three affiliate columns (A. F. L., C. I. O., Unaffiliated unions). Decimal points in the "Percent casting valid votes" column: only two cells (93.7 on "A. F. L.-C. I. O." and 50.0 on "Unaffiliated-unaffiliated") show a clearly inked period in this 1-bit scan. In the other eleven cells the period has dropped out and the digits read as e.g. "91 3", "87 3", "95 0". The column is a one-decimal percent throughout, so I transcribed all of them with a decimal point (91.3, 91.4, 91.1, 87.3, 91.3, 86.1, 83.0, 91.5, 92.9, 95.0, 79.7); a strictly literal reading of those cells would show a gap rather than a point.
