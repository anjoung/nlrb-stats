The stub heading "Division and State" carries a superscript 1 footnote marker, but no footnote text is printed within this crop; the footnote presumably appears at the foot of the full page or at the end of the table's continuation, outside the crop. Only four of the nine census divisions (New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central) appear here; the remaining divisions and any grand total continue on a following page. Suspected misprint, Connecticut row, "Number of elections in which representation rights were won by unions / Other national unions" column: transcribed as printed as 6, but the row does not reconcile — 50 (AFL-CIO) + 6 (other national) + 7 (other local) = 63, against the printed row Total of 57. The column also fails to reconcile at the division level: the six New England states as printed sum to 12 in that column (Massachusetts 6 + Connecticut 6), against the printed "New England" subtotal of 6. Both discrepancies would resolve if the Connecticut cell were 0 rather than 6. The glyph reads as a 6 on two passes of the scan, so it is transcribed as printed; a reader should treat this single cell as doubtful. Every other cell in the New England block reconciles, including Connecticut's valid-votes breakdown (2,176 + 31 + 738 = 2,945) and the total elections identity (57 + 34 = 91). All other division subtotals were checked against their member states across all 14 numeric columns and reconcile exactly: Middle Atlantic, East North Central, and West North Central agree in every column, as does New England in its other 13 columns. In the "New England" subtotal row there is a small stray ink mark between the "Total votes for no union" value (7,095) and the "Eligible employees in units choosing representation" value (8,620), sitting just inside the latter column's rule. It is a scanning artifact or a broken rule segment, not a digit or a footnote marker, and has not been transcribed. A similar isolated speck appears in the left margin beside the Michigan row, also ignored. The last column heading is printed broken across lines as "Eligible employ-/ees in/units/choos-/ing rep-/resentati/on"; the end-of-line hyphens have been repaired to "Eligible employees in units choosing representation". The scan's degraded dot accents make many "i" characters in the headings render faintly (e.g. "Division", "which", "unions"); these are ordinary letters and have been transcribed normally.
