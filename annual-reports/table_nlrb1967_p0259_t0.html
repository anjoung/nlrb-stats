Nevada, data column 6: I read the printed glyph as "1" and transcribed it as printed, but it is doubtful. Every other cell in the table satisfies the table's internal identities (col2 = col3+col4+col5+col6; col1 = col2+col7; col10 = col11+col12+col13+col14; col9 = col10+col15; and each region line equals the sum of its States). With Nevada col6 = 1, two of those fail: Nevada's own row gives 15+8+0+1 = 24 against the printed col2 of 23, and the Mountain column 6 total of 2 is one short of the States' sum of 3. Both would reconcile if the cell were 0. Nevada's other cells (43, 23, 15, 8, 0, 20) are each confirmed by the Mountain column totals, so the discrepancy is isolated to this one cell: either the scan glyph is degraded or the report misprinted it. 2. South Atlantic, last data column (16): printed as 45,699, but the nine South Atlantic States in that column sum to 45,799 (489 + 11,327 + 1,035 + 6,172 + 3,358 + 5,860 + 1,405 + 9,975 + 6,178), a difference of exactly 100. The regional totals in that column for East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, Pacific and Outlying Areas all equal their States' sums exactly, so this is the only column-16 mismatch. Transcribed as printed; the error is either a misprint in the regional total or a hundreds digit (6 vs 7, or one State's value) lost in this 1-bit scan. The whole-row and other-column checks for South Atlantic all tie out, so the suspect value lies in column 16 alone. Minor: stray ink specks sit beside Mississippi column 14 ("0"), Arkansas column 13 ("0") and Nevada column 2 ("23"); these are not footnote markers and were ignored.
