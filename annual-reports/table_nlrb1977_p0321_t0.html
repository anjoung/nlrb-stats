p. 321. Crop: the image ends immediately below the "3,000 to 9,999" row. Section "A." implies at least one further panel (and possibly further size classes such as "10,000 or more") below the crop; no footnotes are visible on this image. Decimal points: this 1-bit scan drops many decimal points, so a large share of the percent cells print as e.g. "22 9", "100 0", "0 3". Every value in the four percent columns ("Percent of total", "Cumulative percent of total", and each "Percent by size class") is a one-decimal figure, and the point is clearly present in other instances of the same values, so the decimal point has been written in each such cell. No digits were altered. "180 to 189", Cumulative percent of total: the separator prints as a comma, "93,5"; transcribed as 93.5 (it continues the monotone cumulative series 92.9 -> 93.5 -> 94.1 and equals the previous cumulative plus this row's 0.6). Heading of the first pair of "won by" columns: the scan reads like "AFO-CIO unions"; the third letter is a degraded "L". Transcribed as "AFL-CIO unions". Section line: the scan shows "A Certification elections (RC and RM)" with a wide gap after the "A"; the period is assumed lost with the other dropped points and has been written as "A. Certification elections (RC and RM)". Dash rules: wherever a count cell is 0, the matching "Percent by size class" cell carries a dash rule (------); those cells are "". The "Cumulative percent of total" cell on the "Total RC and RM elections" line is likewise a dash rule and is "". The dash rules sit low in their row and can appear to belong to the row below; each one here has been assigned to the row whose count is 0. Arithmetic checks (all passed, no misprints found in the counts): "Number eligible to vote" sums to 528,866; "Total elections" to 8,635; AFL-CIO 2,417; Teamsters 1,194; Other national 236; Other local 312; no representative 4,476; and 2,417+1,194+236+312+4,476 = 8,635. The "Percent of total" column sums to 100.0 and agrees cell-by-cell with the differences of the cumulative column. Percent-by-size-class columns: each sums to exactly 100.0 as printed, because in each column one cell is adjusted off its computed value to force the total. Printed vs. computed: "Under 10" AFL-CIO 24.4 (585/2,417 = 24.2); "Under 10" Teamsters 39.1 (469/1,194 = 39.3); "10 to 19" Other national 18.4 (43/236 = 18.2); "10 to 19" Other local 20.4 (63/312 = 20.2). All four are transcribed as printed; every other cell in those columns matches its computed value, which is what confirms these readings rather than casting doubt on them.
