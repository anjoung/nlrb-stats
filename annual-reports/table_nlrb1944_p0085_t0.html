p. 85. Seven columns: stub plus Number/Percent of total under each of the three case-type headings. The stub heading carries footnote marker 1, repeated across both heading levels along with the spanning case-type headings. Indentation (not preserved in the flat grid): "Manufacturing", "Agriculture and forestry", "Mining", "Construction", "Wholesale trade", "Retail trade", "Finance, insurance, and real estate", "Transportation, communication, and other public utilities" and "Services" are top-level industry divisions; the rows between them are components. "Transportation equipment" (1,120) is a manufacturing component set off by rules, and "Aircraft and parts", "Automotive", "Ship and boat building and repairing" and "Other" are its four sub-components (they sum to 1,120; they are indented one step deeper than the other manufacturing lines). Percent cells under 1 are printed without a leading zero (".3", ".9"); transcribed as printed. "Coal mining", Unfair labor practice cases Percent of total: the scan renders the value with a broken "1" that resembles "L6"; read as 1.6, consistent with 42/2,573 = 1.63 percent. Arithmetic check (not applied to any cell): every row's All-cases Number equals its Unfair labor practice Number plus Representation Number, and the two detail groups that have sub-lines (Transportation equipment) sum correctly, so no misprint is suspected in the Number columns.
