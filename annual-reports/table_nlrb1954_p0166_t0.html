The text of footnote 2 is not on this crop — only footnote 1 is printed here, and footnote 1 itself is cut off by the bottom of the crop in the middle of the RD definition ("...asserting that the union previously certified"). The remainder of footnote 1 and all of footnote 2 continue below the crop. In the Union-shop deauthorization cases block the Employers column shows a dash/leader rule on all five rows, transcribed as "". The C.I.O. heading prints faintly as "C. I O." in this scan (the period after the I is broken); rendered as "C. I. O. affiliates".
