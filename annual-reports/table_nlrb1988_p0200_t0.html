Reader’s notes

p. 200. Eight printed columns: stub, Total, then six columns under the spanner "Identification of filing party". The stub column carries no printed heading, so cell 0 of both heading levels is empty; "Total" spans both heading levels and is repeated. The asterisk footnote marker is printed immediately BEFORE the value in the Total column of every "Pending October 1, 1987" row (e.g. "*13,761"); per the convention it has been moved behind a caret ("13,761^*"). It appears only in the Total column, not in the six filing-party columns. The table is printed as seven stacked sections (CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP cases), each a centred label line with no values followed by the same five data rows; the case-type labels are transcribed as label-only rows. There is no CF section on this page. Every section is internally consistent: in all 35 data rows the six filing-party cells sum to the printed Total, and in each section Pending Oct 1 + Received = On docket and On docket - Closed = Pending Sept 30. No suspected misprints. Scan artifacts that do not affect the reading: a stray comma-like speck sits left of the "3" in the AFL-CIO cell of the CC "Pending September 30, 1988" row, a stray dot sits above the CB "Pending October 1, 1987" Other-local-unions cell ("31"), and the "Individuals" heading carries a small mark after it that is page noise, not a footnote marker. The Employers column of the CA section is narrow single digits (1, 0, 1, 1, 0) printed hard against the right rule; they are legible. The leader dots following the row labels, the caption line with its footnote 1 marker, and the running page number were omitted as instructed.
