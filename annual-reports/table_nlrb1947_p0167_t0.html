Reader’s notes

p. 167. This is a two-column appendix list (case number in the stub, company name in column 2), not a numeric table; no column headings are printed, so header_rows is empty. The bold centered line "C. Cases Certified or Dismissed on the Basis of the Record 1" is carried as a section row (label only), on the pattern of lettered section lines; if the project treats it as the table caption instead, that first row can be dropped. Blank lines separate the alphabetical letter groups in print; they are visual separators and are not transcribed as rows. The list is alphabetical and the page ends mid-alphabet after "Samsel Time Control, Inc.", so the table continues on the following page (the footnote rule and footnote are printed here because the page ends). Doubtful/odd cells: "Crysler Motors of Calif." (21-R-3832) is printed as shown but sits between "Cerf Bros." and "Clark Equipment" in alphabetical order, suggesting a misprint for "Chrysler"; "Gongdon, F. G., Co." (1-R-3539) is printed as shown and filed under G, though it may be a misprint for "Congdon"; "15M-R-1930" (Salant & Salant) is much longer than the other 15M numbers on the page (15M-R-81, 15M-R-46) but is printed as transcribed. Case-number digits throughout are from a 1-bit scan with some ink spread (e.g. the 6/8/9 and 3/8 shapes in 9-R-2687, 2-R-6944, 13-R-4392), so individual digits carry some risk. En dashes in the printed case numbers are written as hyphens. Trailing periods after company names are as printed.
