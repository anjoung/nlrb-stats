Reader’s notes

p. 129. 17 columns: stub plus four panels of four columns each (All R, RC, RM, RD elections). The second heading level, "Elections conducted", is centered over and spans all four columns of each panel. The raised footnote marker 1 is printed only on the All R elections panel's "Resulting in certification" heading; the same heading in the RC, RM and RD panels carries no marker. In every "Rerun required" / "Runoff required" sub-row only the third column of each panel ("Resulting in a rerun or runoff") carries a figure; all other cells in those rows hold dash rules (------) and are transcribed as empty. The scan has stray ink specks across the leader dots of the first "Rerun required" label (under "All types"); they obscure no data. A small stray tick appears in the left margin of the "Resulting in a rerun or runoff" cell of the All R panel in the "Rerun required" row under Regional Director-directed; the value there reads 14. All printed figures are internally consistent: panel rows sum across to the All R columns, the type-of-election rows sum down to "All types", and rerun plus runoff equals the "Resulting in a rerun or runoff" figure in each panel. No misprints detected.
