Decimal points in the Percent columns are nearly invisible in this scan (the period reproduces as a thin gap, e.g. "100 0", "65 1"), so they were restored: multi-digit percents are written with the point ("100.0", "65.1") and sub-1-percent values as printed in this report's style with a leading point and no zero (".3", ".8", ".1"). Exact zeros print as a bare "0" in both Number and Percent columns and are transcribed that way. The restoration is corroborated by arithmetic: in every one of the five case groups the five percent components sum exactly to the printed section subtotal, and the three section subtotals sum to 100.0; likewise every Number column's components sum exactly to its printed subtotal and grand total, and RC+RM+RD equals All R in every row.
