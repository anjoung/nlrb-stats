These are plainly one-decimal percentages (the cumulative column runs 23.3, 44.2, ... 100.0), so they are transcribed with a period. No other character is inferred. SECTION LINE: the panel heading reads "A Certification elections (RC and RM)" with the period after "A" lost to the same scan defect; transcribed as "A. Certification elections (RC and RM)". It is centred across the full table width and carries no values. CROP / CONTINUATION: the crop shows only panel A. A panel B (decertification elections) and the footnote keyed to the superscript 1 in the caption are not on this image, so `footnotes` is empty. Panel A is nevertheless complete: the "Total elections" column sums exactly to the printed 3,749 with the 27 size classes shown, so no size-class row is cut off at the bottom. "Total RC and RM elections", cumulative-percent column: the cell holds a dash rule ("——"), transcribed as "". HEADING WORDING: the sub-heading over "Other local unions" reads "Percent of size class", not "Percent by size class" as in the other four pairs. Transcribed as printed; likely a typesetting slip in the source. ARITHMETIC VERIFICATION (numbers not altered, only checked): every integer column sums exactly to its printed total — eligible to vote 217,331; total elections 3,749; AFL-CIO 1,048; Teamsters 481; other national 44; other local 172; no representative 2,004. In all 27 data rows the five outcome counts sum exactly to that row's "Total elections". This independently confirms the integer cells and their column placement. DOUBTFUL PERCENT CELLS: a few percent-by-size-class cells differ by 0.1 from a recomputation off the verified counts. The remaining ~20 cells in the no-representative column that sit 0.1 high (e.g. 2.4 for 47/2,004, 1.6 for 31/2,004) are consistent with the source rounding twice, so those are not in doubt. The glyphs in all listed cells were legible on both passes; the discrepancies look like source rounding/misprints rather than misreads.
