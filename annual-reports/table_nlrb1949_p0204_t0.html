The footnote texts presumably follow on the continuation page. Column assignment notes: in row 17-CC-2, "Nov. 6, 1948" is printed twice, once under "Temporary restraining order - Date lifted" and once under "Date injunction denied"; both are transcribed where printed. In row 30-CC-4 the only date after the petition date is "Sept. 22, 1948 2" and it sits under "Date injunction denied". Row 8-CC-4 and row 2-CC-64, 65, 66 carry only a filing date and petition type; all remaining cells are dash rules. Typography/legibility: the scan is a 1-bit halftone and the abbreviation periods are inconsistent and sometimes faint. I read "July 3, 1948", "Aug. 17, 1948", "Aug. 25, 1948", "Aug. 30, 1948" (8-CC-4), "Aug. 9, 1948" (petition date, row 2-CC-59) with periods, and "Aug 20, 1948", "Aug 30, 1948" (30-CC-4), "Aug 9, 1948" (Date issued, row 2-CC-59) and the four "Sept" filing dates without; a reader should treat the presence or absence of these periods as uncertain. In the first row's filing date there is a stray comma-like mark before the day ("July ,3,1948" as scanned); I transcribe it as "July 3, 1948". The petition type is rendered "10 (l)" (lowercase letter el) throughout except the one "10 (j)", following the caption's reference to secs. 10 (j) and 10 (l); in the scan the el is easily confused with the digit 1. In row 13-CC-5, 7 the company reads "Joilet Contractors Association" (apparently a misprint for "Joliet"); transcribed as printed. Trailing leader dots after case numbers, petition types and the 20-CC-30 company name were dropped, as were the trailing dots that may or may not be periods on the stub labels "2-CC-59, 2-CD-18" and "15-CC-10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15".
