  1. 1 Definitions of types of cases used in tables.—The following designations used by the Board in numbering cases are used in the tables in this appendix to designate the various types of cases CA A charge of unfair labor practices against an employer under sec. 8(a). CB A charge of unfair labor practices against a labor organization under sec. 8(b)(1), (2), (3), (5) (6) CC A charge of unfair labor practices against a labor organization under sec. 8(b)(4)(i) or (ii), (A), (B), (C) CD A charge of unfair labor practices against a labor organization under sec. 8(b)(4)(i) or (ii)(D). CE A charge of unfair labor practices against a labor organization and employer under sec 8(e) CP A charge of unfair labor practices against a labor organization under sec. 8(b)(7)(A), (B), (C) RC A petition by a labor organization or employees for certification of a representative for purposes of collective bargaining under sec 9(c)(1)(A)(i) RM A petition by employer for certification of a representative for purposes of collective bargaining under sec 9(c)(1)(B) RD A petition by employees under sec 9(c)(1)(A)(ii) asserting that the union previously certified or currently recognized by their employer as the bargaining representative no longer represents a majority of the employees in the appropriate unit UD A petition by employees under sec. 9(e)(1) asking for a referendum to rescind a bargaining agent's authority to make a union-shop contract under sec 8(a)(3)

Reader’s notes

p. 174. Six columns: stub, Total, and the four "Identification of complainant or petitioner" columns. The stub column has no printed heading, so cell 0 of every header row is empty. "All cases^1", "Unfair labor practice cases", "Representation cases" and "Union-shop deauthorization cases" are centered banner lines spanning the data columns; they are transcribed as label-only rows. In the Union-shop deauthorization block the AFL-CIO affiliates, Unaffiliated unions and Employers cells hold dash rules, transcribed as "". Typesetting leaves a space after the comma in several figures: "3 214" (All cases / Received fiscal 1963 / Employers), "5 969" (All cases / Closed fiscal 1963 / Individuals), "4 155" (Representation cases / On docket fiscal 1963 / Unaffiliated unions) and "1 052" (Representation cases / On docket fiscal 1963 / Employers); these are rendered 3,214, 5,969, 4,155 and 1,052. All 20 data rows cross-foot exactly (the four identification columns sum to Total, and the three case-type blocks sum to All cases), which supports the digit readings. A small stray ink mark sits just left of 588 in Representation cases / Pending July 1, 1962 / Unaffiliated unions; it is printer's dirt, not a footnote marker. In the footnote the abbreviation "sec" is sometimes followed by a period and sometimes not, and the scan is too coarse to resolve each instance reliably; the roman-numeral subsection references are printed as lowercase (i)/(ii) and appear as (1)/(11) in the scan. A stray dot after "RD" has been dropped. The footnote's final line sits flush with the bottom edge of the crop; the UD entry appears complete, but any further footnote text would be cut off.
