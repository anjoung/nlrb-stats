p. 121. 15 columns. Two heading levels: four columns sit under the spanner "Number of elections in which representation rights were won by unions" and four under "Valid votes cast for unions"; the remaining headings are tall single cells repeated in both levels. The stub heading carries a raised 1 ("Division and State^1"), but no footnote text appears on this crop — the caption says "Page 1 of 3", so the footnote is presumably printed on a later page. The crop is cut off after the Connecticut row: Connecticut is the first state of the next (New England) division group, whose remaining states and subtotal continue beyond this image. Connecticut's own figures are fully legible. "AFL-CIO" is printed with an en dash and broken across lines ("AFL–/CIO"); transcribed as "AFL-CIO unions" with a plain hyphen. Wrapped headings joined and end-of-line hyphens repaired ("represent-/ative" to "representative", "represent-/ation" to "representation"). Subtotal rows (East North Central, East South Central, Island Areas, Middle Atlantic, Mountain) are printed indented and set off by rules; indentation is not preserved in the labels. All five subtotals were checked against their component states in all 14 numeric columns and every one agrees with the printed total, so no misprints are suspected. No illegible or doubtful cells.

p. 122. 15 columns. The table is a one-panel continuation page ("Page 2 of 3"); the crop is cut off at both ends of the body. At the top, the New England block opens mid-section: only Maine through Vermont are printed, so the "New England" division total (124 elections) exceeds the sum of the states shown here (72) because Connecticut appears on the preceding page. At the bottom, the crop ends after Iowa and Kansas, which begin a division section (West North Central) whose remaining states and division-total line fall on the next page; those two rows therefore have no section total on this image. The stub heading carries a raised footnote marker, transcribed as "Division and State^1", but no footnote text is printed under the table on this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Arithmetic cross-checks pass everywhere they can be made within the crop: for every state row, AFL–CIO + other national + other local equals the union-votes total, and union votes + votes for no union equals total valid votes cast. The Pacific and South Atlantic division lines reconcile exactly with the sum of their printed member states in all 14 numeric columns, which confirms the column assignments and the small/zero-heavy rows (American Samoa, Federated States of Micronesia, Marshall Islands, Northern Mariana Islands, Palau all print 0 in every column; Guam prints 1, 1, 1, 0, 0, 0, 2, 2, 2, 2, 0, 0, 0, 2). Delaware's last column (74 eligible employees in units choosing representation, against 501 eligible to vote) looks low but is confirmed by the South Atlantic column total of 9,873. No illegible or doubtful cells; no apparent misprints. "AFL–CIO" is set with an en dash in the headings and is transcribed that way. Division-total labels (New England, Pacific, South Atlantic) are printed indented relative to the state names; the indentation is not reproduced in the cell text.
