p. 151. The crop is the first page of the table and ends mid-table: the last printed row is Maryland, and the South Atlantic division block (Delaware, Maryland, and the states after it) is cut off, with no division subtotal shown for it. No footnote texts appear on this crop, although footnote markers are printed (a raised 1 on the caption, which is excluded, and a raised 2 on the stub heading "Division and State"); their texts presumably appear on the continuation page. The table has 17 columns: stub, "All cases", eight unfair-labor-practice columns (All C cases, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), four representation columns (All R cases, RC, RM, RD), and three single columns UD, AC, UC. The division subtotal rows (New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central) are indented under their states and are transcribed in the stub column like other rows. Scan quality: the page is a 1-bit scan with light speckling. Two cells carry stray specks that I read as the digit alone: Missouri UD = 6 (a small mark sits just left of the 6) and Delaware CC = 0 (a speck precedes the 0). A stray dot also follows the "Maine" label and sits near "Iowa", "Minnesota" and "Illinois"; these are page speckles, not leader dots or markers. No digit was ambiguous. Arithmetic check (not used to alter any cell): every row's CA+CB+CC+CD+CE+CG+CP equals its "All C cases", RC+RM+RD equals "All R cases", and All C + All R + UD + AC + UC equals "All cases"; each division subtotal equals the sum of its listed states in every column. All checks are consistent with the figures as transcribed, so no misprint is suspected.

The residual is consistent across every column (All cases +545 = All C +407, All R +132, UD +3, UC +3), i.e. exactly two missing states (Delaware and Maryland); this is not a misprint. "All cases", and the three right-hand headings (Union deauthorization / Amendment of certification / Unit clarification cases), are tall headings spanning multiple header levels, so they are repeated down the levels they span; the UD / AC / UC abbreviations occupy the third level only. Doubtful/minor marks: in the "Mountain" row, a small stray speck is printed just left of the CG value; the value itself reads 0 and is confirmed by the column sum (all Mountain states have CG = 0). Faint stray specks also sit in the stub area of the Kentucky, Louisiana and "West South Central" rows (scanner dirt, not text). Several digits are printed with a broken "1" glyph typical of this scan (e.g. Georgia RM 1, Florida RM 0 / RD 15), but all were confirmed against the row and column totals.
