p. 295. 18 columns: stub + "All C cases" (Number, Percent of total closed, Percent of total method) + seven two-column groups (CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), each Number and Percent of total closed. Decimal points: in this scan the period in the percent figures is not resolved and the cells read as "100 0", "27 3", "0 8", etc. These are decimal percentages and are transcribed with the point restored ("100.0", "27.3", "0.8"). The restoration is confirmed arithmetically throughout: e.g. 11,359/41,544 = 27.3, 7,183/11,359 = 63.2, 5/576 = 0.9. "Total number of cases closed", All C cases / Percent of total method: the cell reads as two digit groups right-aligned, i.e. "0.0" under the convention above. This is almost certainly a misprint or a stray setting — that column otherwise carries 100.0 only on the method-heading rows (Agreement of the parties, Compliance with) and is blank on the total line in companion tables. Transcribed as printed; not corrected. "Before issuance of complaint", CD cases / Number: the cell holds a parenthesized raised 2 and no numeral, i.e. a footnote reference standing in for the value; written "(^2)". Its percent cell is a dash rule. The CD informal-settlement components (this cell + 4 + 0) sum to the printed 4, so the suppressed value is 0. The crop ends immediately below the "Board decision" row; its numbers are fully legible but the rule beneath it, any further body rows, and the footnote block (including notes 1 and 2, called out in the caption and in the CD cell) are cut off and are not on this image, hence the empty footnote list. Row labels are given as printed, including the bare heading "Compliance with", whose sub-rows complete the phrase. Two pairs of rows repeat the labels "Stipulated decision" and "Consent decree" (once under "After issuance of complaint, before opening of hearing" and once under "After hearing opened"); both pairs are kept in printed order. All dash-rule cells ("-------") are rendered as "".
