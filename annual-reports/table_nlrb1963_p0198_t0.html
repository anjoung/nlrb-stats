p. 198. Scope: the crop shows only the 10(l) part of Table 20 (section lines "10(l)", "8(b)(4)(A)", "8(b)(4)(B)", all printed in italic). No 10(j) section appears on this image, so the table evidently continues on a following page. The footnote line is clipped along the bottom edge of the crop but is legible. Asterisks: the asterisk that follows many union names is the marker for the footnote at the foot of the table, so it is written behind a caret (e.g. "Teamsters, Local 559^*"). These asterisks sit on the baseline rather than raised, but they are footnote references, not part of the union name. Punctuation: this is a 1-bit scan in which periods after abbreviations (Inc., Corp., Co., and personal initials) are frequently faint or absorbed into the leader dots that follow the entry. They have been transcribed in the standard printed form; individual periods, especially trailing ones before leaders, are not individually verifiable at this resolution. A few union entries do carry a visible sentence period inside the cell ("Bridge Structural Iron Local 167.", "Electrical Workers, IBEW, Local 861." for 15-CC-181, "Ladies Garment Workers, Local 102."), and those are kept. Doubtful cells: - Case No. column, row "Old Dutch Farms, Inc.": the leading pair of digits images as "92", giving "92-CC-800". 92 is not an NLRB regional number, the respondent is a New York dairy and the union is Teamsters Local 584 (New York), so this is almost certainly region 02 and has been transcribed "02-CC-800". Flagging it because the character as inked does read 9. - Case No. column, row "J. A. LaRocca Bros.": final digit of "22-CC-185" is partly broken; 185 is the best reading. - Case No. column, row "Evans Cooperage Co.": final digit of "15-CC-163" is partly broken; 163 is the best reading. - Name of union column, row "Standard Fruit & Steamship Co.": a stray mark sits between "ILA," and "Locals" and another possibly after "Locals"; transcribed as "ILA, Locals 1422 and 1771", but a comma after "Locals" cannot be ruled out. - Name of union column, row "Evans Cooperage Co.": printed with a comma as "Steelworkers, Union", transcribed as printed even though the comma looks like a compositor's slip. - Name of complainant column, row "06-CC-292": images as "Brvan II Spaite & Son"; read as "Bryan H. Spaite & Son". Disposition columns: every non-X cell in Granted/Denied/Pending is either blank or filled with a dash rule, and all such cells are given as "". No row carries more than one X.
