I have therefore written the decimal point in every percent cell. Footnote markers: in the CD "Number of cases" column the raised markers are printed to the LEFT of the figure (2 26, 3 86, 4 31); following the stated convention they are written after the value as 26^2, 86^3, 31^4. The cells reading "(^5)" are printed as a parenthesised raised 5, the usual NLRB device for a quantity too small to show at this rounding; the whole cell is that reference, so the marker is kept inside the parentheses.
