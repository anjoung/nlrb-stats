Reader’s notes

p. 293. Layout: the table is set as two side-by-side panels sharing one set of column headings, within each of three lettered sections (A, B, C). Following the panel rule, each section's left-panel rows are given first, then that section's right-panel rows, so the section structure stays intact; the headings are given once. The three lettered section titles are printed as centered cross-page headings between rules and are transcribed as label-only rows. Decimal points: in the "Percent of total cases" column the decimal point is broken or lost in this scan for many values, printing as a gap (e.g. "100 0", "9 4", "69 0", "28 5", "58 2", "58 8", "9 1", "26 3", "1 1", "97 4", "3 7", "8 6", "29 5"); it survives clearly in others on the same page ("1.4", "100.0", "62.6", "21.2"). These are treated as scan damage to a printed decimal point and written with the point, parallel to the "8, 044" -> "8,044" rule. Every such reading is consistent with the number/total ratio. Footnote markers: in section A the "Total cases" row carries two raised markers (1 and 2) before each value; they are written as "4,306^1^2" and "100.0^1^2". Raised markers elsewhere are single. As printed, component allegations exceed the totals (e.g. section C left: 651 + 25 = 676 against a total of 668; section C right: 189 + 64 + 26 + 89 = 368 against 302), which footnote 1 explains; nothing was adjusted. The closing NOTE line at the very bottom is clipped by the crop — only the upper half of its letters is visible — but it is legible and is included in the footnote list; the final words ("prior annual reports.") are the least certain part of it. The stub heading cell is blank in the original.
