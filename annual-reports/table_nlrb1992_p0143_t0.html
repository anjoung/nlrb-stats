p. 143. The crop is the first page of the table and is cut off at the bottom: the South Atlantic division is incomplete (Delaware, Maryland, District of Columbia and Virginia are printed, the remaining states and the division subtotal line continue on the following page), so no South Atlantic total appears here. Footnote markers are present (superscript 1 on the caption, superscript 2 on the stub heading "Division and State"), but the footnote texts themselves are not printed within the crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Column structure: 17 columns — stub, "All cases", eight unfair-labor-practice columns (All C cases, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), four representation columns (All R cases, RC, RM, RD), then UD, AC and UC, each under its own tall heading. The group headings "Union de-/authorization cases", "Amend-/ment of certifi-/cation cases" and "Unit clarifica-/tion cases" were de-hyphenated when joined. Arithmetic checks: every one of the four completed division subtotal rows (New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central) reconciles exactly with its member states in all 16 numeric columns, and the row-wise identities All C = CA+CB+CC+CD+CE+CG+CP and All cases = All C + All R + UD + AC + UC hold for every state row transcribed, including the four partial South Atlantic rows; no misprints detected. A few stray ink specks appear in the scan (e.g. a dot left of the "New York" CD value 63, a speck near the Pennsylvania/AC area and near Iowa's AC value 0); these are artifacts, not digits or footnote markers.

p. 144. The crop is a continuation page: it begins in the middle of the South Atlantic division (West Virginia), so the division's earlier states (Delaware, Maryland, D.C., Virginia on the preceding page) are not shown. The printed South Atlantic subtotal 4,022 therefore exceeds the sum of the five states visible here (2,598); this is expected, not a misprint. The crop ends after the Mountain subtotal row, so the Pacific division and the table's footnotes (including the note for the "Division and State^2" marker) fall outside the image; footnotes is accordingly empty. Doubtful cell: Nevada, "UC" (last) column — the glyph is smudged and could be read as a stray mark; transcribed as 0, which is confirmed by the Mountain UC subtotal of 16 (5+2+1+4+1+3+0+0 = 16). Column headings "Union deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases" and "Unit clarification cases" are printed hyphen-broken across lines ("Union de-/authoriza-/tion cases", etc.) and have been rejoined. "Division and State^2" and "All cases" are tall headings spanning both heading levels and are repeated in each. Verification: every state row's components sum to its "All C cases" and "All cases" totals, and the East South Central, West South Central and Mountain subtotals equal the sums of their states in all 16 value columns. No arithmetic inconsistencies were found among the fully visible sections.

Doubtful marks (none transcribed into cells): (a) Puerto Rico, column CP — a small apostrophe-like speck sits immediately before the printed 0; it appears to be a scan artifact, not a digit or footnote marker, so the cell is given as 0.
