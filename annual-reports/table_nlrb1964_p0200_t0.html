p. 200. 16 columns. Suspected misprint: in the "Teamsters" row of the 1-union block, "Percent won" is transcribed as printed, 53.6, but 987 won of 1,911 total elections is 51.6 percent; the 1,911 and 987 figures are confirmed by the column totals (4,275+1,911+343+130 = 6,659 and 2,300+987+201+87 = 3,575), so either the report misprinted the percentage or the scanned "1" reads as a "3". Every other percentage on the page agrees with its own row's counts. Decimal points in the "Percent won" column are frequently lost or faint in this scan (cells print as "53 7", "100 0"); they are transcribed as decimal points, which the surrounding values confirm. Likewise, the period after "A" in the section line "A. ALL REPRESENTATION ELECTIONS" is not clearly legible on the image but is implied by the report's section lettering. Row labels in the 2-union and 3-or-more blocks print abbreviation periods inconsistently ("Teamsters v Teamsters" vs "Teamsters v. Natl", "AFL-CIO v Natl" vs "Natl. v. Local"); a trailing period often merges with the leader dots that follow the label, so the periods are transcribed only where visible and should not be read as meaningful variation. Blank value cells are printed as dash rules ("-------") and are rendered as empty strings; printed zeros are kept as 0. Several cells in the 3-or-more block distinguish a printed 0 from a dash rule in adjacent columns (for example "AFL-CIO v Teamsters v Natl" has 0 under "Other national unions" and a dash under "Unaffiliated local unions"); these were read column by column against the vertical rules.
