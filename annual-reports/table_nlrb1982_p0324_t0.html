Decimal points: in this 1-bit scan the periods in the one-decimal percent figures are mostly dropped, so cells render as "100 0", "27 4", "15 2". They are decimals — the spacing is uniform and the CB percent for 5,000-9,999 still shows its period clearly as "2.0" — so I restored the decimal point in every percent cell. No other characters were added. Doubtful cell: row "600-699", CC "Percent by size class" shows only a right-aligned "7" with no visible leading zero, transcribed as ".7". Every other sub-1 value in the table is printed "0.x", so the leading "0" is probably present in the original and lost in this scan. Blank cells: the Total row has nothing printed under "Cumulative percent of all situations". Throughout the body, a count of 0 is normally printed with its percent cell left empty; those percent cells are "". Counts printed as 0 are kept as "0". Footnote marker: in the Total row the raised 2 is printed before the figure (²34,229); recorded as "34,229^2". Crop/scan artifact: a diagonal page-edge line crosses the lower right of the image, passing through the CA-CB and Other C combinations columns in the last several rows and clipping the bottom rule. The digits there are still legible — 5,000-9,999 Other C = 1 / 0.5 and Over 9,999 Other C = 2 / 1.0 — but those two percent values sit slightly low relative to their rows. Row-label ranges are printed with a short dash; transcribed as plain hyphens ("10-19", "1,000-1,999"). Neither footnote ends with a period in the print.
