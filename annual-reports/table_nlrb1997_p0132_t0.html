p. 132. Grid is 18 columns: stub + "All C cases" (Number, Percent of total closed, Percent of total method) + seven case-type panels (CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), each with Number and Percent of total closed. Doubtful cell: row "Before issuance of complaint", column "CD cases / Number", is printed as what reads "(2)" rather than a plain figure, and its adjacent percent cell is a dash rule. The row's components sum exactly to the All C total without any CD contribution (7,338 + 912 + 246 + 13 + 12 + 35 = 8,556), so the intended value is almost certainly 0; the characters are transcribed as they appear on the scan. Suspected misprint: row "Total number of cases closed", column "All C cases / Percent of total method", is printed as "0.0" where the other method-group header rows carry 100.0; transcribed as printed. Dash rules ("——") used for nil percents are rendered as empty cells throughout, per instruction. The CP percent cell on the "After hearing opened, before issuance of administrative law judge's decision" row carries only faint scan speckle plus a dash and is read as empty. The crop is cut off immediately below the "Compliance with" row (a double rule follows), so the sub-rows of that section and the rest of the table, including any footnotes, are not on this image. The caption's superscript 1 therefore has no corresponding footnote text in the crop. The row label "After hearing opened, before issuance of administrative law judge's decision" is printed in italics across two lines and joined here; "Compliance with" is followed by leader dots and may have been intended to end with a colon.

p. 133. Grid is 18 columns: stub + All C cases (Number / Percent of total closed / Percent of total method) + two columns (Number, Percent of total closed) for each of CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP. This is a "—Continued" page: the opening rows (Administrative law judge's decision, Board decision, Adopting..., Contested, Circuit court of appeals decree, Supreme Court action) are detail lines belonging to a section whose heading/total row is printed on the preceding page and is not in this crop. No footnote texts are printed below the table on this page; the marker "1" in the caption refers to a footnote carried on an earlier page of the table. Cells I want to flag: - CD cases, Number column, rows "Before issuance of complaint" under Withdrawal and under Dismissal: printed as "(2)" on the baseline. Given the row arithmetic (the other CD detail figures sum exactly to the CD totals of 0 and 2 without it), this parenthesized "(2)" appears to be a footnote reference rather than a count, but I transcribed it exactly as printed. - "By Board decision" / All C cases, Percent of total closed: the glyphs read "0 4" with no decimal point visible in this 1-bit scan (all neighbouring cells in that column carry one). Transcribed as "0.4"; read as "04" if literal glyphs are wanted. - "After hearing opened, before administrative law judge's decision" under Dismissal, All C Number: a stray leader dot sits just left of the figure; the value is 7. Possible misprint in the source (not corrected): under Dismissal, the five detail rows sum to 9,716 in the All C Number column and 6,005 in the CA Number column, three short of the printed totals 9,719 and 6,008. All other columns and the whole Withdrawal block reconcile exactly, as do the cross-case-type sums of every row. All dash rules ("——") and blank cells are given as "".

p. 134. This crop is the tail of a continued table (the caption reads "—Continued"), so it shows only the last four body rows; the earlier rows of the table are on the preceding page. The column headings are reprinted on this page, so they are transcribed here. 18 columns: stub plus Number/Percent-of-total-closed/Percent-of-total-method for All C cases, then Number/Percent-of-total-closed for each of CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG and CP cases. Scan specks: a stray dot sits just right of the "3" in the All C cases Number cell of "By circuit court of appeals decree", and a dot precedes the "0" in the CC cases Number cell of "By Supreme Court action" (reads ".0"); both are printing/scan artifacts, and the values are 3 and 0 respectively. A loose dot also appears under the CD cases Percent cell between the "10(k) actions" and "Otherwise" rows. Dash rules ("——") fill the percent cells opposite zero counts; these are transcribed as empty cells. In the "Otherwise" row the CD cases Percent cell is faint and may be blank rather than a dash rule, but either way it is recorded as empty. Footnote 2 is printed under the table but its marker does not appear on any row or heading in this crop; it presumably attaches to a row on the preceding page.
