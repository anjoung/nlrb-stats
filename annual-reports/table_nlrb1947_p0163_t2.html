Reader’s notes

p. 163. This page is not a statistical table: it is Appendix F, a two-column list of cases (case number in the stub, name of the party in column 2). No column headings are printed, so header_rows is empty. The appendix caption ("APPENDIX F" / "LIST OF CASES IN WHICH THE BOARD RENDERED DECISIONS DURING THE PERIOD JULY 1-AUGUST 21, 1947") and the page number 155 are omitted; the division line "I. Unfair Labor Practice Cases" and the two subsection lines "A. ..." and "B. ..." are kept as label-only rows. The list is cut off by the end of the page: subsection B breaks after "1-C-2962 Lathrop Engine Co., The." and continues on the following page. Doubtful cells (300-ppi bitonal scan; several leading digits are hard to separate): - Row "Geraldine Novelty Co., Inc.", case-number cell: the leading digit is ambiguous between 8 and 3; transcribed as "8-C-829" but "3-C-829" is equally consistent with the glyph. - Row "Pillsbury Flour Co.": read as "9I-C-1461" (region 9 plus a subregional letter I), but the character after the 9 could be a 1 ("91-C-1461") or a damaged hyphen. Letter-suffixed region codes do occur elsewhere on this page ("15M-C-...", "5W-C-..."), which supports reading it as a subregion letter. - Rows "Salant & Salant, Inc." and "Tishoming Country Electric Power Association." read as "15M-C-1222" and "15M-C-1002"; row "Gennett Oak Flooring Co." reads as "5W-C-2122". The letters M and W are legible but faint. - Row label "Tishoming Country Electric Power Association." is transcribed as printed; it is almost certainly a misprint for "Tishomingo County Electric Power Association" (the word reads "Country", clearly different from "County" in the "Gibson County" row above). - Row label "People Motor Express Inc." is transcribed as printed; a lost terminal "s" ("Peoples Motor Express") cannot be ruled out at this resolution. - "1-C-2953" (Brown & Sharpe), "1-C-2962" (Lathrop Engine), "10-C-1933" (Atlantic Co.) and "5-C-2198" (Richmond Coca-Cola) have slightly blurred final digits. Within each subsection the entries are in alphabetical order of the party name (in B, "Richmond Coca-Cola Bottling Works" is filed under "Coca-Cola"), and the blank lines between groups of entries are spacing only, not data rows.
