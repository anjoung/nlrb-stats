The page is a continuation ("—Continued") of a case-by-case listing; the italic centered line "Representation cases" immediately under the caption is a panel sub-caption, not a stub row, so it is not included in `rows`. The crop ends mid-alphabet at "Dickson-Jenkins Mfg. Co" and the table continues on the following page. The trailing line "See footnotes at end of table." is slightly clipped at the bottom edge of the crop but is legible; it is recorded in `footnotes` even though it carries no marker, and the numbered footnotes themselves are printed elsewhere (at the end of the table), not on this image. Small raised digits are hard to resolve at this scan resolution; several are doubtful: - "Bradley Lumber Co", Decision issued: parenthesized raised reference read as "(20)"; the second digit is indistinct and could be "(29)". - The two consecutive "Celanese Corporation of America" rows, Hearing Opened and Hearing Closed: both rows carry raised markers on "July 6, 1937". I read the first row as 13 and the second as 12, but the two digits are not cleanly separable and the pair may well be 12 then 13 (i.e. the order may be reversed), or 13/14. Treat these four cells' markers as low confidence. - "Armour Packing Co", Hearing Opened and Hearing Closed: marker read as 3; could be 5. - "Chrysler Corporation", Hearing Opened and Hearing Closed: marker read as 15 on both cells; it is possible the closed-date marker is 16. - "Chrysler Corporation" first "Do" row, Hearing Opened and Hearing Closed: ditto marker read as 17 on both; the printed form appears as "do." followed by the raised numeral, and I have transcribed the ditto word as "do" without the period. Parenthesized raised numerals standing alone in the Decision issued column — (1), (4), (6), (20) — are footnote references used in place of a date; they are transcribed as printed in the cell rather than as caret markers, since there is no value for them to attach to. Ditto conventions: stub ditto rows are printed as "Do" followed by leader dashes (no period is visible before the leaders, so transcribed "Do"); the date columns use a lowercase "do" set between leader dashes, transcribed "do"; the Decision issued column uses "Do." with a visible period. Leader dots/dashes have been dropped throughout per instruction.
