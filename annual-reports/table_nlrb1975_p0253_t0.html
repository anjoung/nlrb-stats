The blank bands between divisions are typographic spacing only, not label-only rows. Every value was cross-checked arithmetically and all checks pass: in each state row the four union-type columns sum to the "Total" won column, Total won + "no representative chosen" = Total elections, the four union vote columns sum to "Valid votes cast for unions: Total", and that total + "Total votes for no union" = "Total valid votes cast". Each division subtotal also equals the sum of its member states in all 16 numeric columns. No misprints detected.
