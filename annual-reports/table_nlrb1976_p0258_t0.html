p. 258. Single-panel table, 17 columns, two heading levels. The crop ends after Tennessee: the Region IV subtotal line and any further regions (and any footnote text, including the text for the raised "1" on the stub heading "Standard Federal regions") continue on the following page and are not on this image. One doubtful cell: Puerto Rico, column "Valid votes cast for unions — Teamsters". It is printed (and reads in the scan) as "1, 455", transcribed here as "1,455", but it is arithmetically inconsistent in two independent ways: the Puerto Rico row's union-vote components (1,004 + 1,455 + 9 + 2,041 = 4,509) do not match the printed row total 3,509, and the Region II Teamsters column (72 + 1,124 + 1,565 + 1,455 + 0 = 4,216) does not match the printed regional total 3,216. Both reconcile exactly if the value were 455, so the leading "1," is most likely a misprint or a scan artifact. Transcribed as printed per instructions. Every other value in the grid was cross-checked against its row and column totals and is internally consistent (Regions I, II and III all balance across all 16 numeric columns; each Region IV state row balances on elections won + no representative = total elections, on the four union-vote components summing to the union total, and on union total + no-union votes = total valid votes cast). Kentucky, "Valid votes cast for unions — AFL-CIO unions" prints slightly broken in the scan; read as 1,695, which is confirmed by the row's component sum (1,695 + 630 + 486 + 409 = 3,220). The scan's "AFL–CIO" uses an en-dash-like rule in the headings; normalized to a hyphen. Digit-grouping spaces after commas ("7, 231") were closed up as instructed.
