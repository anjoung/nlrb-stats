Every Percent value in this table is printed to one decimal place and neighbouring cells in the same columns show the dot clearly, so the dots have been restored (e.g. "100.0", "40.5"). No digits were changed. The "After" line under "Certification issued, total" is a label-only section line; its value cells are empty in the print. Dash rules ("------") in the Percent columns, which mark a zero or non-applicable percent beside a printed 0, are transcribed as "". There is a small stray ink mark (a scanning artifact, not a digit or footnote marker) in the UD Number column at the left edge of the row "After notice of hearing, before hearing closed" within the "By dismissal" block; the cell value there is 1. The caption carries footnote marker 1 on the year ("1978 1"); the caption itself is omitted per instructions. Not verified arithmetically; no printed total was altered.
