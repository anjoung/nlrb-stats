p. 326. Dropped periods: this bitonal scan has lost nearly every period on the page. The same loss affects the abbreviation points in the stub labels, which really read "Boston, Mass.", "New York, N.Y.", "Phila., Pa.", "St. Louis, Mo.", "Washington, D.C.", etc.; there I left the labels as the scan shows them rather than inserting characters. Doubtful cell — row "7 Chicago, Ill", column "Remanded in full / Cumulative fiscal years 1977-1981 / Number": the glyph reads as 3 and is transcribed as 3, but three independent checks say the intended figure is 7. With 3 the row's cumulative components sum to 173 against the printed row total of 177; the column sums to 91 against the printed "Total all circuits" value of 95; and the adjacent percent 3.9 corresponds to 7/177 (3/177 would be 1.7). Either a misprint or a 7 that this scan renders ambiguously — worth checking against the original page. Row "1 Boston, Mass", column "Modified / Fiscal Year 1982 / Number": the digit is broken up in the scan and prints as a blotted shape; read as 6, which is corroborated by the printed percent 31.6 (6/19) and by the column total of 45. Row "11 Atlanta, Ga" (footnote 2, commenced operations October 1, 1981) has no cumulative fiscal years 1977-1981 figures at all; its "Total fiscal years 1977-1981" cell and all five cumulative Number/Percent pairs are blank on the page and are left empty here. The last body row, "Washington, D C", carries no circuit number in the stub and is set flush left, unlike the numbered rows above it. Apart from the Chicago cell noted above, every row and every column cross-foots exactly against the printed totals, and every printed percent matches its number divided by its base.
