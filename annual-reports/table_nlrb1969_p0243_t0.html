Decimal points: in many percent cells the period is faint or has dropped out of the scan, printing as e.g. "100 0", "14 3", "20 8", "11 7", "13 8", "69 3", "3 0", "3 5", "83 1", "0 9", "2 3", "1 8", "2 0", "0 5", "88 9", "1 1", "1 5", "1 0", "0 2", "0 6", "91 8". All of these are one-decimal percentages in context (column alignment and the surrounding values confirm it), so they are transcribed with the decimal point restored, exactly parallel to the comma-with-space convention. Blank/dash cells transcribed as "": "Cumulative percent of total" in the "Total RC and RM elections" row (printed "- - - -"); "Other local unions / Percent by size class" in rows "110 to 119" and "190 to 199" (printed "- - - -" beside a printed 0 in the Number column, which is kept as "0"). CERTIFICATION ELECTIONS (RC & RM)" is carried as a label-only row. Dash rules ("----") are rendered as empty cells per the instructions. In the "50 to 59" row the column-11 zero is printed with a broken glyph that reads almost like "U"; it is a 0 (the column sums to the printed total of 18 with that reading).
