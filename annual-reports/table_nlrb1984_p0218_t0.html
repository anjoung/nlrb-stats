p. 218. 16 columns. Dash rules ("----") in the body are transcribed as empty cells; printed zeros are kept as 0. Suspected misprint: in the "1-union elections" row, the last column ("In elections where no representative chosen") is printed as 136,446, but the four member rows above it sum to 136,466 (94,109 + 29,469 + 4,615 + 8,273) and the grand total 143,593 minus the 2-union subtotal 7,127 also gives 136,466. Transcribed as printed. Every other cell in the table cross-foots exactly. "Percent won" column: this is a 1-bit scan in which the decimal points of that column did not reproduce (values render as "41 1", "100 0", etc.). They are transcribed with the decimal point ("41.1", "100.0"); each value is consistent with Total won / Total elections, so the reading is not in doubt, only the ink. Header grouping caveat: the rule printed under "In units won by" in the "Employees eligible to vote" block covers only the Teamsters, Other national unions and Other local unions columns, so the employees-block "AFL-CIO unions" column is transcribed as a level-2 heading standing beside "Total" and "In elections won". Arithmetically, though, that AFL-CIO column belongs to the same group (70,760 + 17,835 + 2,848 + 14,476 = 105,919 = "In elections won"), so a reader may wish to treat "In units won by" as spanning all four union columns. The exact left end of that short rule is the one layout feature I could not resolve with certainty at this resolution. Section line: the label reads "A All representation elections"; a period after the "A" is probable but is not legible in the scan, so none was added. The crop ends just below the "Total representation elections" rule, so any Part B of the table and the text of footnote markers ^1 (on the caption) and ^2 (on "Total elections") fall outside this image. Minor scan artifacts not carried into the cells: dotless "i" in several headings and labels ("elec-tıons", "1-unıon"), and a stray mark before the "Local v Local v Local" label.

Elections in RC cases" for the same reason, though no period is legible after the "B". Row labels show "v" between union names with no visible period; left as "v" since "v." cannot be confirmed. SUSPECT CELL — "2-union elections" row, "Employees eligible to vote / In elections won" column: I read this as 17,571 and have transcribed it as read, but it is almost certainly 17,671. Two checks fail at 17,571 and both pass at 17,671: (a) the row's four "in units won by" components 4,112 + 5,928 + 1,053 + 6,578 = 17,671, and 17,671 + 7,046 (no representative chosen) = 24,717, the printed row total; (b) the column total requires 53,066 + 17,671 + 19,449 = 90,186, the printed "Total RC elections" figure. So this is either a 6 that I have misread as 5 at this resolution, or a genuine misprint in the subtotal. Every other cell in the table reconciles both across rows and down columns. "Total RC elections" row, "Elections won by unions / Other national unions" column: printed with a stray speck before the figure ("· 62"); transcribed as 62, which is what the column sum (53 + 9 + 0) requires. HEADER STRUCTURE: the "In units won by" sub-band rule covers only the last three columns of the "Employees eligible to vote" group (Teamsters, Other national unions, Other local unions); the "AFL-CIO unions" column immediately to its left is set as a tall heading reaching up to the group rule, so it is repeated across levels 2 and 3 rather than placed under "In units won by". This is a typographic oddity — that column's values (e.g. 36,142 for AFL-CIO, 19,128 in the 3-union subtotal) are clearly employees in units won by AFL-CIO unions — but the grid follows the printed rules. Likewise, "In elections where no representative chosen" (last column) is printed as a separate tall column outside the "Employees eligible to vote" box, although arithmetically it belongs to that group (205,717 = 90,186 + 115,531). Dash rules ("——") and blank cells are both rendered as ""; printed zeros are kept as 0.
