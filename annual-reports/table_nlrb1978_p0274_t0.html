Reader’s notes

p. 274. Structure: 17 columns — stub plus four four-column panels (All R, RC, RM, RD elections), each headed "Elections conducted". The indented "Rerun required" / "Runoff required" lines carry a figure only in each panel's "Resulting in a rerun or runoff" column; all their other cells are printed as leader-dot rules and are transcribed as "". Suspected misprint (transcribed as printed): in the RC panel the row arithmetic does not close for "Regional director-directed" — 1,207 - 15 - 31 = 1,161, but 1,224 is printed under "Resulting in certification". The same 63-unit gap appears in the "All types" row (7,311 - 53 - 153 = 7,105 vs. 7,168 printed), and the All R total 8,464 exceeds RC 7,311 + RM 270 + RD 820 = 8,401 by the same 63. The RC "Total elections" column is internally consistent (538 + 5,521 + 1,207 + 42 + 3 = 7,311), so the error appears to originate in the single figure 1,207 (1,270 would reconcile every check). Every other row and column total in the table adds correctly. Minor legibility: in the "Rerun required" line under "Expedited—sec 8(b)(7)(C)", the RD "Resulting in a rerun or runoff" cell has an ink blemish on the glyph; read as 0, which is consistent with the column total of 8 for RD reruns.
