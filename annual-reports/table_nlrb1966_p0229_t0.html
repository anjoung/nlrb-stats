Reader’s notes

p. 229. The crop is the first page of Table 16; the body is cut off after "Ship and boat building and repairing" and the table continues on the following page. The stub heading carries footnote marker 1 ("Industrial group ^1"), but no footnote text is printed within this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Suspected misprint: in the "Fabricated metal products (except machinery and transportation equipment)" row, the "won by unions / Total" cell is printed as 244, but the four union components in that row (273 + 46 + 18 + 7) sum to 344, and 564 total elections minus 220 elections with no representative chosen also gives 344. Transcribed as printed (244); the leading digit is a plausible scan/print ambiguity between 2 and 3. Every other row in the table is internally consistent (union components sum to the won-by-unions total, and union votes plus no-union votes equal total valid votes cast), which supports the rest of the grid. Slightly doubtful cells from blurred type, both in the last column ("Employees in units choosing representation"): "Chemicals and allied products" read as 13,363, and "Products of petroleum and coal" read as 3,332. Neither is arithmetically checkable against other columns. Column structure: 17 columns. The five-column span "Number of elections in which representation rights were won by unions" (Total, AFL-CIO unions, Teamsters, Other national unions, Other local unions) and the five-column span "Valid votes cast for unions" (same five sub-headings) are the only spanning heads; all other headings are tall single columns and are repeated in both heading levels.

p. 230. This crop is a continuation page: no column headings are printed on the image, so header_rows is empty. The grid has 17 columns (stub + 16 numeric columns), confirmed by counting the vertical column rules and the 16 values in every full data row. The table is also cut off vertically by the crop at both ends: the first row ("Automotive and other transportation equipment") is a continuation of a manufacturing sub-list begun on the previous page, and the body ends at "Miscellaneous services" with a closing rule and no total row or footnote block on this image. Horizontal double rules separate the industry sections (after "Miscellaneous manufacturing", after "Nonmetallic mining and quarrying", after "Finance, insurance, and real estate", after "Heat, light, power, water, and sanitary services", and before/after "Services"); these are rules only and are not transcribed as rows. Doubtful cells from scan degradation, all read with reasonable confidence but worth flagging: "Construction" column 2 (reads 176, the 6 is partly filled in); "Transportation, communication, and other utilities" column 2 (reads 578, the 8 is broken); "Motor freight, warehousing, and transportation services" column 1 (reads 418, the 8 is broken); "Communications" column 1 (reads 103, the 3 is smudged and there is a stray ink speck under it); "Medical and other health services" column 1 (reads 6). Note the "Educational services" row: the printed value in the last column is 0 while column 15 is 15, which is unusual relative to the pattern of neighbouring rows but is transcribed exactly as printed.
