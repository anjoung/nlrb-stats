p. 33. The crop is a full page, not a conventional table: it shows "Chart 14" (a stacked bar chart titled "CASES CLOSED") with a data block printed beneath the chart's horizontal axis. That data block is what is transcribed here. Its column headings are the fiscal-year axis labels 1958-1967, preceded by the stub heading "Fiscal Year" (printed on two lines at the left of the year row); the chart's own title "CASES CLOSED", the "Cases" y-axis label, the axis scale values (0 to 33,000) and the "Chart 14" caption are excluded, as is the running head and the body text ("f. Other Developments" and the following paragraphs) printed below the chart. Each of the two data row labels is preceded in the original by a small legend swatch (a solid black bar for "C CASES", a hatched bar for "R, UD, AC, AND UC CASES"); those swatches are legend keys, not footnote markers, and are not transcribed. The second row label wraps onto three printed lines ("R, UD, AC," / "AND" / "UC CASES") and has been joined. The TOTALS row is set off by a rule and equals the sum of the two rows above it in every one of the ten year columns, which corroborates the digits read. No cell was illegible; the smaller figures (e.g. 16,360 and 29,494 for 1967, 20,355 for 1959) are slightly lighter in the scan but legible and confirmed by the column sums.
