Section line: the centred line reads "A All representation elections"; a period after the "A" is not legible in this scan (the gap suggests one may be present), so it is transcribed without one. This crop therefore shows only panel A of the table; panel B (and any later panels) continue on following pages. Row labels: the printed labels alternate between "v" and "v." for the versus abbreviation — e.g. "AFL-CIO v AFL-CIO v. national", "AFL-CIO v national v. local" show a period while most others do not. They are transcribed exactly as they appear; the inconsistency is almost certainly lost periods in the scan rather than real typesetting variation. Three labels wrap across printed lines and were joined with the end-of-line hyphen repaired: "AFL-CIO v AFL-CIO v AFL-CIO", "AFL-CIO v AFL-CIO v AFL-CIO v AFL-CIO", "AFL-CIO v AFL-CIO v Teamsters v local". Empty cells: the table fills unused cells with a dash rule (and a few appear fully blank); all are transcribed as "". Printed zeros are kept as "0". Arithmetic check: every cross-total verifies as printed — each detail row's four components sum to its "Total Valid votes cast"; the won/lost "Total" columns equal the sum of their four union columns; the "1-union elections", "2-union elections", "3(or more) union elections" and "Total representation elections" lines all equal the sums of their member rows. No misprints detected. The word "Appendix" set vertically in the right margin and the page number 269 are running head/folio and are omitted.
