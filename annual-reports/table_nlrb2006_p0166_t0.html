The crop is cut off at the bottom: the body ends mid-division with "New Mexico" and the table continues on the following page (the Mountain division subtotal and the remaining divisions are not on this image). The stub heading carries a superscript footnote marker 1, transcribed as "Division and State^1", but no footnote text is printed within the crop, so `footnotes` is empty — the note presumably appears at the foot of the continued table. Rows named after a group of states (East North Central, East South Central, Island Areas, Middle Atlantic) are indented division-subtotal lines set off by rules; the indentation is not reproduced in the labels. The last heading is printed as "Eligible employ-ees in units choos-ing rep-resentatio n", with the final "n" dropped to its own line; joined and de-hyphenated to "Eligible employees in units choosing representation". Digit-grouping commas are printed inconsistently in the source and are transcribed as printed: the "Number of employees eligible to vote" and "Eligible employees in units choosing representation" columns print four-digit values without commas (e.g. 2897, 5871, 1823, 4566, 2652, 4236, 1734), while "Total valid votes cast" and the vote columns use them (2,439; 4,873; 1,192). All internal arithmetic reconciles: every division subtotal equals the sum of its member states in all 14 numeric columns, the union sub-columns sum to their "Total", and union total plus "Total votes for no union" equals "Total valid votes cast" in every row. No misprints detected.
