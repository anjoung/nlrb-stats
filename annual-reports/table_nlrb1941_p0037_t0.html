DOUBTFUL MARKER. MARKER-ONLY CELLS. Two cells hold nothing but a small parenthesized raised digit in place of a figure: "Cases dismissed" under "Cases closed before hearing", employers Number-of-workers column, read as (5); and "Cases withdrawn" under "Cases closed after board decision", individuals Number-of-workers column, read as (6). The digits are at the limit of legibility and each could be 5 or 6; both are consistent with footnote references, since in each case the panel column totals above are already satisfied without them (e.g. employers before-hearing workers total 889 = the settled row alone). VALUE IN ONE COLUMN ONLY. Under "Cases closed after hearing", the rows "Cases dismissed" (3) and "Cases withdrawn" (2) carry a figure in the unaffiliated-unions Number column while that panel's Number-of-workers cell is a dash rule, left as "".
