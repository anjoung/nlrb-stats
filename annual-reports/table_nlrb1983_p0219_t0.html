The division lines (New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central) are indented subtotal rows set off by rules; they are transcribed in reading order after the states they cover. Blank spacing bands between the sections carry no label or values and are not transcribed as rows. Verification: every one of the four subtotal rows cross-foots exactly against its state rows in all 16 numeric columns, which confirms the column alignment and the digits; no arithmetic discrepancy was found anywhere on the page. Scan artifacts, none of which affect a value: a short stray horizontal mark sits just above the "Maine" stub label; a small dash/caret-shaped speck sits immediately left of "8,663" in the New Jersey row, "Number of employees eligible to vote" column (it is an ink artifact, not a footnote marker, and not part of the number); the scan renders many dotted i's faintly, so headings read as "Vahd votes" and "umons" in places — transcribed as the intended "Valid votes" and "unions". Rotated running text along the right edge is the page's running head and is excluded.
