Column placement was cross-checked by arithmetic on all 15 rows and every row is internally consistent: the AFL-CIO/Teamsters/other-national/other-local election counts sum to the "Total" won by unions; that total plus "no representative was chosen" equals "Total elections"; the four union vote columns sum to "Valid votes cast for unions: Total"; and that total plus "Total votes for no union" equals "Total valid votes cast". No printed value appeared to be a misprint. A vertical running head "Appendix A" is printed in the right margin outside the table rules and is excluded, as are the caption lines.
