The stub heading carries footnote marker 1 ("Industrial group 1"), but no footnote text is printed within this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Doubtful cells: in "Miscellaneous manufacturing industries", the last column ("Eligible employees in units choosing representation") is read as 5,877 — the leading digit is smudged and could conceivably be an 8 (8,877), though 5 fits the glyph and the column total better. In "Primary metal industries", the two adjacent valid-vote cells 1,373 (Teamsters) and 1,412 (Other national unions) are tightly set but legible. Checks on the "Manufacturing" subtotal: the five won-by-unions component columns (1,160 + 511 + 172 + 98 = 1,941) agree with the printed Total, and 1,941 + 2,396 = 4,337 agrees with total elections. The valid-votes components (87,650 + 23,636 + 19,479 + 10,197 = 140,962) also agree, and 140,962 + 153,879 = 294,841 agrees with total valid votes cast. Nothing was computed into the grid; all cells are as printed.

p. 320. Continuation page: no column headings are printed on this crop (the table's headings are on the preceding page), so header_rows is empty. The grid is 17 columns (stub + 16 numeric), confirmed from the column rules and from the arithmetic structure, which holds throughout: col1 = col2 + col7; col2 = col3+col4+col5+col6; col9 = col10 + col15; col10 = col11+col12+col13+col14. These identities were used to check every row and every subtotal, and all cells verify except the five noted below, which are transcribed exactly as printed. 1. "Membership organizations", column 14 (the fourth of the four columns that sum to col10, i.e. the one holding 1,796 in "Educational services" and 1,052 in "Business services"): the cell appears blank in the scan and is transcribed as "". It is almost certainly a dropped/failed impression rather than a true blank: the row needs 61 there (230+2+0+61 = 293), and the "Services" subtotal for that column (10,322) also requires exactly 61 from this row. No digits are legible, so nothing was entered. 2. "Communication", column 12 (the one totalling 43,081): reads 197 in the scan. Both checks fail by 90 with that value — the row gives 6,750+197+24+3,106 = 10,077 against a printed col10 of 9,987, and the "Transportation, communication, and other utilities" subtotal of 4,912 for this column requires 107. The printed value may be 107 with the 0 reading as 9, or 197 may be a misprint; 197 is transcribed as seen. 3. "Mining" subtotal, column 12: printed 643, but the four mining detail rows sum to 634 (478+6+29+121) and the row identity also requires 634 (1,495+634+970+22 = 3,121).
