p. 113. This is Appendix C, an alphabetical list of cases, not a numeric statistical table; the three printed columns are the case name (stub), "Volume" and "Page". Treated as caption and therefore excluded: "APPENDIX C"; "LIST OF CASES IN WHICH THE BOARD RENDERED DECISIONS DURING THE FISCAL YEAR 1942"; "I. Unfair Labor Practice Cases"; "A. Unfair Labor Practice Cases Decided After Contest". All four are centred display lines printed ABOVE the "Volume"/"Page" column headings, so they are title-block lines rather than body section lines. If the convention for this corpus is to keep the roman/letter subdivision lines as label-only body rows, "I. Unfair Labor Practice Cases" and "A. Unfair Labor Practice Cases Decided After Contest" would be inserted as label-only rows at the top. The stub column has no printed heading; cell 0 of the single heading row is therefore "". Blank lines separate the alphabetical groups (after "Atlanta Flour & Grain Co., Inc." and after the Butler Brothers entry). These are white space only — no label is printed — so no empty rows were emitted for them. Doubtful cells: - "Borg Warner Corp., Marvel-Schebler Division", Volume column: reads as "23", which is out of line with the 33-41 range of every other entry on the page and may be a misprint for "33" or a scanning artefact; transcribed as printed. - Row-label terminal periods: where a label ends in an abbreviation ("Corp", "Inc", "Co") the closing period sits at the baseline immediately before the leader rule and cannot be separated from the leaders in this scan. I have written the conventional period (e.g. "A. P. Parts Corp.", "Abbott Worsted Mills, Inc.", "Aluminum Ore Co.", "AAA Dental Laboratory ... et al."). Labels that do not end in an abbreviation ("Alco Feed Mills", "American Sheet Metal Works", "Botany Worsted Mills") were left without a period. - "Barrett Co. The": no comma is visible between "Co." and "The", unlike "Canyon Corp., The" and "Carborundum Co., The"; transcribed as printed. - "Carter Carbureter Corp.": spelled "Carbureter" (not "Carburetor") in the original. - "Blount, R. A., Hearst B., & Lonnie Flinn & Eunice Simpson": "Hearst B." is clear but unusual; transcribed as printed. Two labels wrap onto a second printed line and were joined into one row: the Bethlehem Steel entry, and the Butler Brothers entry (end-of-line hyphen in "Mainte-nance" repaired to "Maintenance"). Crop: the list continues past the bottom of this image; the printed folio at the foot of the page (partially visible, "112") is cut off and excluded.
