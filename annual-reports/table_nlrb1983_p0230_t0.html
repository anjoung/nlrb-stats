Reader’s notes

p. 230. The scan resolves small round marks very weakly: dotted i's print as dotless (e.g. "situations" appears as "sıtuatıons") and decimal points in the percent columns appear as gaps ("100 0", "29 4"). These are decimal points in the original typesetting — every percent column is a one-decimal figure, the "Percent of all situations" column totals 100.0, and the cumulative column runs 29.4 ... 100.0 — so they are transcribed with decimal points. Terminal periods in the two footnotes are likewise faint and have been restored. Total row, "Cumulative percent of all situations": the cell holds an em dash (—) and is transcribed as "". Total row, "Total number of situations": a raised 2 is printed immediately before the figure; transcribed as "36,263^2". Percent cells are left blank wherever the corresponding count is 0 (and in a few places where it is not, e.g. "80–89" / "90–99" Other C combinations); these blanks are transcribed as "" and are as printed. Minor doubtful readings: "170–179" CA–CB combinations percent reads "0 1" (0.1), noticeably smaller than its neighbours but legible; "2,000–2,999" CE number of situations is "0" followed by a small ink speck that is not a digit or marker.
