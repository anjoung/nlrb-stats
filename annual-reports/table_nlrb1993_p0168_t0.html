Reader’s notes

p. 168. 15 columns: stub plus 14 numeric columns. The stub heading carries a raised 1 ("Industrial group^1"), but no footnote text is printed within this crop, so `footnotes` is empty; the note for marker 1 presumably appears at the foot of the full table. The crop ends at the "Manufacturing" subtotal row (closed by a rule), so the table continues on a following page with the nonmanufacturing industry groups and the grand total; only the manufacturing panel is on this image. Two apparent misprints in the source, transcribed exactly as printed: 1. "Textile mill products", column "Valid votes cast for unions / AFL-CIO unions" reads 702, which exceeds that row's union total of 662 and is inconsistent with the row's own breakdown (702 + 0 + 0 should equal the 662 printed under "Valid votes cast for unions / Total"). The 662 figure is the consistent one: 662 + 736 (no union) = 1,398, the printed total valid votes cast, and the column of union totals sums exactly to the printed 37,364. Summing the printed AFL-CIO column gives 34,809 against the printed 34,769; the 40 gap is exactly 702 - 662, so 702 is most likely a typesetting error for 662. The digits were checked twice on the image and read 702 both times. 2. The last column, "Eligible employees in units choosing representation", sums over the 21 industry rows to 34,266, while the "Manufacturing" row prints 34,246, a difference of 20. I could not identify which cell is at fault; every other column checks out exactly against the Manufacturing subtotal, and all per-row internal checks pass (won + no representative = total elections; union subcategories = union total; union votes + no-union votes = total valid votes cast). Heading text was rejoined across the printed line breaks and end-of-line hyphens (e.g. "Num-/ber of elec-/tions in which no rep-/resenta-/tive was chosen" and "Eligible em-/ployees in units choos-/ing rep-/resenta-/tion"). "AFL-CIO" is printed with an en-dash in the heading and is written here with a plain hyphen. Row labels that wrapped onto two lines (Apparel, Fabricated metal products, Measuring/analyzing instruments) were joined and their broken words repaired ("mate-/rials", "equip-/ment)").

p. 169. The crop is a "—Continued" page of Table 16 and is cut off at the bottom: the last transcribed row, "Museums, art galleries, botanical and zoological gardens", sits on the bottom edge of the image (its values are fully legible, but the horizontal rule below it and any further rows, the services subtotal, and the footnote text are not on this crop). The stub heading carries a superscript 1 marker ("Industrial group^1"), but footnote 1 itself is not printed within the crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Fifteen columns, confirmed from the column rules: stub; Total elections; four columns under "Number of elections in which representation rights were won by unions" (Total / AFL-CIO unions / Other national unions / Other local unions); Number of elections in which no representative was chosen; Number of employees eligible to vote; Total valid votes cast; four columns under "Valid votes cast for unions" (same four sub-heads); Total votes for no union; Eligible employees in units choosing representation. In the "Transportation, communication, and other utilities" subtotal row there is a stray ink tick/apostrophe-like mark printed just left of 18,639 in the "Total votes for no union" column. It is a printing artifact, not a digit or footnote marker (18,639 is exactly the sum of the six member rows above it), so it has been dropped from the cell. Row indentation distinguishes two levels: "Mining" and "Transportation, communication, and other utilities" are division subtotal lines, while "Construction", "Wholesale trade", "Retail trade", "Finance, insurance, and real estate" and "U.S. Postal Service" are printed at that same subtotal indent (each is a one-group division with no separate subtotal line). Indentation is not encoded in the cells; labels are given as printed. No scanning ambiguity in any numeral: every row's union breakdown sums to its union total, won + no-representative sums to total elections, union votes + no-union votes sums to total valid votes, and both subtotal lines reconcile exactly against their member rows in all fourteen numeric columns. Nothing was computed or corrected; all values are as printed.
