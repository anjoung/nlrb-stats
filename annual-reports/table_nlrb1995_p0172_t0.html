p. 174. The crop is a continuation page: it opens with the "Mountain" division subtotal row, so the individual Mountain states appear on the preceding page. Column headings are printed on this page and are transcribed. Doubtful cell: California, "Valid votes cast for unions — Total". I read the printed figure as 6,630, but it is internally inconsistent: the row's own components (5,850 + 260 + 220) sum to 6,330, and the Pacific subtotal 11,407 in that column equals the sum of the state values only if California is 6,330 (the state totals as read sum to 11,707). The glyph in question (second digit, 3 vs 6) is not fully legible at this scan resolution; it is transcribed as read and should be verified. All other columns of the Pacific and Outlying Areas subtotals reconcile exactly with their component rows. Guam shows only 3 elections but 2,660 employees eligible to vote and 2,277 valid votes cast, with just 180 eligible employees in units choosing representation. These figures look anomalous but are confirmed by the Pacific subtotals (28,521 and 24,618), so they are transcribed as printed.
