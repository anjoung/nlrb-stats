p. 239. The caption marks this as "Page 1 of 3", and the body is cut off by the page break: the last printed row is Massachusetts, part of a New England division whose remaining States and divisional subtotal continue on the following page. There is no "Total" line on this image. The indented rows East North Central, East South Central, Middle Atlantic and Mountain are divisional subtotal lines (set off by double rules); their labels are transcribed without the printed indentation. Column 1 ("Total elections"), column 7 ("Number of employees eligible to vote") and column 14 ("Eligible employees in units choosing representation") are printed without digit-grouping commas throughout (e.g. 11840, 35071, 49432, 17693, 25028), unlike the vote columns, which do use commas; both styles are transcribed as printed. All printed row and column arithmetic I checked is internally consistent (won + no-representative = total elections; union votes + no-union votes = total valid votes cast; AFL–CIO + other national + other local = union totals; State rows sum to each divisional subtotal), so no misprints are suspected. All cells were legible; no doubtful digits.

The division subtotal rows "New England", "Outlying Areas" and "Pacific" are printed indented and set off by horizontal rules; they are transcribed with their own wording and no indentation marker. All cells are clearly legible; no doubtful digits. Every row's printed figures are internally consistent (component union columns sum to their "Total", and union total plus "no union" equals total valid votes), so no misprints are suspected. The last column's heading is printed hyphenated across lines as "Eligible employ-ees in units choos-ing rep-resentati on" and has been rejoined as "Eligible employees in units choosing representation". "AFL–CIO" is printed with an en dash.

Two columns are typeset without digit-grouping commas throughout — "Number of employees eligible to vote" (e.g. 1485, 2413, 22309, 13957, 192958) and "Eligible employees in units choosing representation" (e.g. 1670, 12993, 3604, 96150) — while the other numeric columns use commas. These are transcribed exactly as printed, with no commas added. Heading wording restored from line breaks: "Total elec-/tions" = "Total elections"; "Number of elec-/tions in which no represen-/ta-/tive was chosen" = "Number of elections in which no representative was chosen"; "Number of em-/ployees eligible to vote" = "Number of employees eligible to vote"; "Eligible employ-/ees in units choos-/ing rep-/resentati/on" = "Eligible employees in units choosing representation" (the last break, "representati"/"on", is printed without a hyphen).
