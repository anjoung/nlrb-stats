The printed glyphs look like "44", so "44" is transcribed as seen; the intended value is very likely 204 (either a misprint in the original or two leading digits lost in the scan). 2) "Number of elections ... won by unions / AFL-CIO unions" reads as "0" while the row's Total won is 1 and the other two union subcolumns are 0; the South Atlantic subtotal (12 Total, 12 AFL-CIO) implies this cell should be 1. Transcribed as the printed "0". The footnote marker 1 on the stub heading "Division and State" is printed here, but no footnote text appears on this crop (it is on the first page of the table). A stray ink mark resembling an apostrophe precedes the "South Atlantic" subtotal label in the scan; it is a scanning artifact and is not transcribed. Row labels are followed by leader dots, which have been dropped.

p. 167. This is a continuation page (caption reads "—Continued"), so the body begins mid-division: the Mountain division's earlier States (and all preceding divisions) are on the prior page, which is why the Mountain subtotal (29 elections) exceeds the sum of the three Mountain States shown here (Arizona 3 + Utah 1 + Nevada 6 = 10). The Pacific subtotal row and the "Total, all States and areas" row were checked and are internally consistent as printed: every Pacific column equals the sum of its six States, and in the total row 165 + 366 = 531, 158 + 1 + 6 = 165, 8,610 + 113 + 864 = 9,587, and 9,587 + 11,961 = 21,548. The subtotal rows "Mountain", "Pacific" and "Outlying Areas" are printed indented under their States, as is the final total row. The scan's typeface drops the dots on several lowercase i's (e.g. the stub labels for Arizona, California and Hawaii, and headings such as "elections", "unions"); these are read as ordinary i's. The table's 15 columns are all fully within the crop; no column is cut off.
