p. 209. 16 columns: stub + "All C cases" (Number, Percent of total closed, Percent of total method) + six two-column groups (CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CP), each Number and Percent of total closed. Decimal points: in this 1-bit scan the decimal point inside almost every percentage failed to print, so the cells read as e.g. "100 0", "26 0", "0 8". Two cells did retain a visible point ("1.5" at Board decision / CD percent, "0.5" at Consent decree / CP percent), confirming the format is one decimal place throughout. I have therefore written all percentages with the decimal point restored ("100.0", "26.0", "0.8"). No digits were changed, computed or rounded. Cut off by the crop: the table clearly continues past the bottom rule — the "Compliance with" block ends mid-list (Trial examiner's decision, Board decision, with no "Court decision" line), and the remaining disposition categories (withdrawals, dismissals, etc.) and the printed footnotes are not on this image. Footnote markers 1 (in the caption) and 2 (in the body) are referenced but their texts are below the crop, so `footnotes` is empty. "(2)" in the CD cases / Number column of "Before issuance of complaint" is printed as a parenthesized footnote reference standing in place of a figure; the matching CD percent cell is a dash rule. Transcribed as printed. Dash rules ("------") were read as "" in: Percent of total method for "Total number of cases closed"; and the CD / CE / CP percent cells wherever the count is 0 (or "(2)"). Row labels: "Informal settlement" and "Formal settlement" each carry a small smudged mark (reading like a raised dot and a comma respectively) between the word and the leader dots; I read these as leader-dot artifacts rather than punctuation or footnote markers and dropped them. The two "Stipulated decision" / "Consent decree" pairs are distinct printed rows (one pair under "After issuance of complaint, before opening of hearing", one under "After hearing opened"); the duplicate labels are as printed, not an error.
