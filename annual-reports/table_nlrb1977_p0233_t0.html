Reader’s notes

p. 233. Eight columns: stub, Total, and six columns under the spanner "Identification of filing party". The stub column has no printed heading, so cell 0 of both heading levels is empty; "Total" spans both heading levels and is repeated. The six centered section lines ("All cases", "Unfair labor practice cases", "Representation cases", "Union-shop deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases", "Unit clarification cases") are printed as spanners across the data columns, not in the stub; they are transcribed as label-only rows. Footnote markers on the two section lines: the raised glyphs on "Unfair labor practice cases" and "Representation cases" are small and hard to separate at this scan resolution — both could be read as 2 or 3. I assigned ^2 to "Unfair labor practice cases" and ^3 to "Representation cases" because footnote 2 points to table 1A (ULP totals) and footnote 3 to table 1B (representation totals), and the markers appear in that order down the page. In the "Union-shop deauthorization cases" block, every column except Total and Individuals holds a dashed leader rule, transcribed as "". Footnote 1: the mark between "terms" and "Advisory" is faint in the scan; I transcribed it as a period. The table caption also carries a raised 1 (caption omitted per instructions). Arithmetic check (not applied to the cells, only verified): all 30 data rows cross-foot — the six filing-party columns sum to the printed Total in every row — and the "All cases" block equals the sum of the five case-type blocks row by row. No misprints detected. "19TQ" is transcribed as printed (fiscal-year transition quarter, July-September 1976).
