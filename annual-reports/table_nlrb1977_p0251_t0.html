p. 251. Three printed columns; one heading level. Three of the row labels repeat verbatim under different categories, which is as printed. Decimal points: in the percent column the period is faint or dropped by the scan in several cells, which print as "46 1", "3 5" (twice), "35 6" and "11 3". I have transcribed these as 46.1, 3.5, 3.5, 35.6 and 11.3, matching the other cells in the column (100.0, 49.6, 33.9, 1.7, 11.3) and the column's arithmetic (46.1+3.5=49.6; 33.9+1.7=35.6; 49.6+3.5+35.6+11.3=100.0). Case counts and percents both sum to the printed totals (57+4+41+13=115). The caption's footnote marker 1 (on "(July–September 1976)") is part of the omitted caption. Scan artifacts make some "i" dots and periods drop out in the row labels (e.g. "Comphance", "decision"); labels are given with standard spelling.
