Reader’s notes

p. 110. Eleven columns: stub, "All cases", five unfair-labor-practice columns (NLRA C, CA, CB, CC, CD) and four representation columns (NLRA R, RC, RM, RD). "All cases" and the stub heading are tall single headings spanning both heading levels, so each is repeated in both. Arithmetic cross-check (used only to confirm doubtful glyphs, nothing was altered): the nine division rows (Manufacturing, Agriculture, Mining, Construction, Wholesale trade, Retail trade, Finance, Transportation/communication, Services) sum exactly to the printed Total row in all ten numeric columns; the 20 Manufacturing sub-rows sum exactly to the Manufacturing row in all ten columns; the four Mining sub-rows, the three Transportation-equipment sub-rows and the seven Transportation/communication sub-rows likewise match their own subtotals in every column. No printed figure appears to be a misprint. Doubtful glyphs resolved by that check: Manufacturing / NLRA R prints with a broken final digit that could read 463 or 465; the column sums confirm 463. Finance, insurance, and real estate / RM is a smudged digit with a stray ink blot printed just above it in the same cell; the RM column sum confirms the value is 6 and the blot is a scanner artifact, not a footnote marker. Other scan artifacts, not transcribed: a stray mark resembling an opening parenthesis after the "Unfair labor practice cases" spanner heading; a small tick inside the NLRA R column just below the heading rule and above the Total row; stray specks beside Apparel/CD (0), Products of petroleum and coal/RC (94), Stone clay and glass/RD (15), Primary metal industries/RC (184), Metal mining/RD (1), Water transportation/RD (2), Highway passenger transportation/RC (51) and Heat light power/CB (1). All footnote markers on the headings are raised 1 and 2 as recorded. The table body ends with "Services" at the foot of the page, immediately above the footnotes, so nothing is cut off by the crop.
