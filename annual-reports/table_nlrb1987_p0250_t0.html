p. 250. The crop cuts the table off after "Oil and gas extraction"; the table continues on the following page (further mining/non-manufacturing groups and the grand total are not on this image). The footnote marker 1 on the stub heading "Industrial group" refers to a footnote that is not printed within this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Column layout: 17 columns — stub, "Total elections", a five-column spanner "Number of elections in which representation rights were won by unions" (Total / AFL-CIO unions / Teamsters / Other national unions / Other local unions), three tall single columns, a five-column spanner "Valid votes cast for unions" with the same five sub-headings, then two more tall single columns. Arithmetic cross-checks all reconcile as transcribed (elections won + elections with no representative = total elections; the four union sub-columns sum to each "Total"; union votes + no-union votes = total valid votes; and the 22 manufacturing detail rows sum to the "Manufacturing" line in every numeric column, including 45,975 in the last column), so no misprints are suspected. Doubtful cell: in the "Automotive and other transportation equipment" row, the "AFL-CIO unions" cell under the elections-won spanner carries a stray ink mark (a small arrow-like scribble) immediately left of the digit; the digit itself reads 4, and 4+2+1+2 = 9 matches the row's "Total", so it is transcribed as 4 with the mark treated as page noise rather than a footnote marker. Scan artifacts: the page uses a dotless "i" in many words (e.g. "Prınting", "ındustrıes"); these have been transcribed with normal "i". Several row labels are followed by long leader-dot runs, which have been dropped.

No misprints detected; nothing was computed or corrected.
