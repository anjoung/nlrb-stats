The crop is cut off at the bottom: the "Compliance with" block ends at "Board decision" and the table continues on the following page (the listed methods account for only about 26 percent of total closed, and the usual Withdrawal/Dismissal sections are absent). No footnote text is visible in the crop, so `footnotes` is empty; the caption carries a raised 1 but captions are excluded.
