Division subtotal rows (East North Central, East South Central, Middle Atlantic, Mountain) are printed indented and set off by rules; they are transcribed as ordinary rows carrying their values. The last heading is printed hyphen-broken as "Eligible employ-ees in units choos-ing rep-resentati on" and has been rejoined to "Eligible employees in units choosing representation". All rows are internally consistent (valid votes for unions + votes for no union = total valid votes cast), with one oddity transcribed as printed: New York shows 7016 employees eligible to vote against only 1253 total valid votes cast, and the Middle Atlantic subtotal 8754 is consistent with that figure. Arizona's "Valid votes cast for unions — Total" of 49 splits as 10 AFL-CIO + 39 other national, as printed. No cell was illegible.
