Every percent column in this table is printed to one decimal place and the surviving periods elsewhere in the same column confirm the format, so these have been transcribed with the decimal point restored (e.g. "100.0", "85.7", "0.4", "1.0"). No digits were altered. (All, CA, CB); "After Board order adopting..." (All, CA, CB, CC); "After Board decision..." (CA, CP); "After circuit court decree..." (All, CA, CB, CC, CD); "After Supreme Court action" (All, CB).
