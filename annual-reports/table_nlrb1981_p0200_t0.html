p. 200. Crop shows 17 columns. The stub heading carries a raised footnote marker 2 ("Standard Federal Regions ^2"); the footnote text itself is not printed within this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. The headings "All cases" (col 1), "Union deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases" and "Unit clarification cases" are tall single headings printed across both heading levels, and the UD / AC / UC column letters sit on the second heading level beneath the last three of them. The crop is cut off at the bottom: the table ends after the Region IV subtotal and continues on the following page(s) (Regions V onward and the grand total are not in this image). Doubtful cell: Rhode Island, column CC — I read the glyph as 6, but the arithmetic disagrees in two independent ways. The Rhode Island row's C-case components (123+26+6+1+0+0+1) give 157 against a printed "All C cases" of 158, and the Region I CC column (3+0+57+4+6+0) gives 70 against a printed subtotal of 71. A value of 7 in that cell would reconcile both, and no other single-cell change reconciles either. Transcribed as seen (6) rather than corrected; the cell should be re-checked against a higher-resolution scan. Every other cell in the crop is internally consistent: for all 24 state rows, "All cases" equals All C + All R + UD + AC + UC and "All C cases" equals CA+CB+CC+CD+CE+CG+CP; and all four regional subtotal rows (Regions I-IV) equal the column sums of their member states in all 16 numeric columns, with the single CC exception in Region I noted above. Minor scan artifacts, not transcribed: a stray tick after "2,282" in the Region I "All C cases" cell, a stray mark left of "57" in the Region III "CD" cell, and a stray apostrophe-like mark left of "5" in the Connecticut "UD" cell. Leader dots and the trailing periods they produce were dropped from row labels (e.g. "Rhode Island.", "Virginia.", "Alabama..", "South Carolina." in the image).

p. 201. This crop continues a table from the preceding page: no column headings are printed on it, so header_rows is empty. The grid is a stub plus 16 numeric columns, counted from the vertical column rules; the stub's leader dots (and the stray leader-dot remnants beside New Mexico, Nebraska, Colorado, South Dakota, Region VI, Region IX, Washington, Region X and the total row) have been dropped. Column assignment was checked by adding each region's member States: Regions V, VI, VII, VIII and IX reconcile with their printed subtotals in all 16 columns, confirming the alignment. Region X reconciles in 15 of 16 columns; in column 7 the four States as transcribed (Alaska 9, Idaho 0, Oregon 3, Washington 2) sum to 14 against a printed subtotal of 12. One of the Alaska or Oregon digits in that column is either a misprint or a misreading of the scan (Alaska 9 vs. a possible 7, or Oregon 3 vs. a possible 1); the cells are transcribed as they appear and nothing was adjusted to force the total. Michigan, column 15: a stray ink speck sits just left of the digit, giving it the look of an apostrophe; the digit is read as 0, which the Region V subtotal of 10 confirms.
