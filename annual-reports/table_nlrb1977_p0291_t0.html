No other value was inferred. Verification: every one of the 16 numeric columns sums exactly to the printed "Manufacturing" figure, and every one of the 22 industry rows satisfies All cases = All C + All R + UD + AC + UC as well as the within-panel subtotals (All C = CA..CP, All R = RC + RM + RD). No misprints detected. A stray diagonal pen/pencil stroke crosses the "Union deauthorization cases (UD)" column beside the "Lumber and wood products" / "Furniture and fixtures" rows; it is a hand mark on the page, not type, and does not obscure the values (both read 6). The CG column is 0 for every industry and 0 in the total, as printed.

p. 292. Continuation page: no column headings are printed on this crop, so header_rows is empty. The grid has 17 columns (stub + 16 numeric), counted from the column rules and confirmed by the body rows and the "Total, all industrial groups" line. Column placement was verified arithmetically throughout: in every row columns 3-9 sum to column 2, columns 11-13 sum to column 10, and columns 2+10+14+15+16 sum to column 1; the three printed group subtotals (Mining, "Transportation, communication, and other utilities", Services) also reproduce the column-by-column sums of the rows above them. These checks fix the narrow right-hand columns (14, 15, 16), which are the hardest to place on this scan. One internal inconsistency as printed: in the "Retail trade" row the component cells 2,965 + 660 + 92 + 16 + 6 + 0 + 88 give 3,827, but column 2 is printed 3,828. The row's other checks pass (1,483+252+368 = 2,103, and 3,828+2,103+70+1+54 = 6,056), and the grand-total line is internally consistent, so this looks like a one-unit misprint (or an eroded digit) somewhere in the Retail trade component cells; all cells are transcribed exactly as read, nothing corrected. "Miscellaneous services" (second-to-last body row before the Services subtotal) is degraded in the 1-bit scan and renders roughly as "Miscellaneouls services"; transcribed as the intended "Miscellaneous services". Footnote 2's marker is a small eroded superscript glyph that could be read as an asterisk rather than a "2"; it is transcribed as "2" on the assumption that it follows footnote 1. The punctuation after "Source" in that line is a single faint mark that may be a colon rather than a period. "Electric, gas and sanitary services" is printed without a serial comma, as shown.
