Reader’s notes

p. 186. Eight printed columns: stub, Total, and six columns under the spanner "Identification of filing party". The stub has no printed heading; "Total" is a tall single heading spanning both heading levels, so it is repeated in each. The footnote marker 1 is attached to the caption (excluded by instruction), not to any row or column. Seven centered section lines ("CA cases", "CB cases", "CC cases", "CD cases", "CE cases", "CG cases", "CP cases") carry a label and no values; each precedes its five-row block. Scanning artifacts, transcribed as nothing: a stray ink dot sits just left of the Teamsters value 0 in "Pending September 30, 1981" of the CC block, and another after the row label "Received fiscal 1981" in the CP block. Neither is a footnote marker or a digit. The scan renders many lowercase "i" dots faintly (e.g. "Pendıng", "filıng"), but the wording is unambiguous and is given in standard spelling. No arithmetic was performed on the cells, but as a check every row's six party columns sum to the printed Total, and in each block Pending October 1 + Received = On docket = Closed + Pending September 30. No misprints found.
