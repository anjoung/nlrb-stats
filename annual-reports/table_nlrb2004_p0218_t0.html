Reader’s notes

p. 218. The crop is "Page 1 of 2" of Table 6B; the table continues on the following page with Regions VI-X, so there is no grand-total row here. Column structure: 17 columns. The stub, "All cases", and the eight ULP and four representation columns are headed on two levels (group banner + code); the last three columns carry a third printed level ("UD", "AC", "UC") on a short line of its own under their spelled-out headings, so three header levels are given and the tall headings are repeated down each level. "All C cases" and "All R cases" are each printed on two lines ("All C" / "cases", "All R" / "cases") and joined here. Typesetting inconsistency transcribed as printed: in the region subtotal rows the four-digit values in the "All C cases" column (1483, 4434, 2679, 2796, 6456) and in "All R cases" (1154) are printed without a digit-grouping comma, while the same rows' "All cases", "CA" and "CB" four-digit values do carry commas. No commas were added. Arithmetic check (not used to alter any cell): every region subtotal equals the sum of its member states in every one of the 16 value columns, and in each row All cases = All C cases + All R cases + UD + AC + UC, and All C cases = CA+CB+CC+CD+CE+CG+CP, All R cases = RC+RM+RD. No misprints detected. "District of Columbia" is set with a wide word space after "District"; transcribed with a single space. No cells were illegible.

p. 219. This is page 2 of 2 of Table 6B; Regions I-V appear on the preceding page, so the "Total, all States and areas" row covers rows not shown on this crop. Headings: the stub ("Standard Federal Regions^2") and the "All cases" column are tall single headings spanning all three heading levels; "Union deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases" and "Unit clarification cases" span the first two levels with their code (UD, AC, UC) on the third. "All C cases" and "All R cases" are printed on two lines ("All C" / "cases") and are joined here. Typesetting quirk, transcribed as printed: digit-grouping commas are used inconsistently. The "All C cases" and "All R cases" columns print four-digit values without a comma (1074, 1892, 1034, 3397, 4514, 26885, 4549), while the same magnitudes elsewhere carry commas (1,194; 1,442; 1,200; 3,583). No commas were added or removed. All regional subtotals and row cross-totals on this page were checked and are internally consistent with the state lines as transcribed, including the two values that look odd at first glance: Utah CC = 61 (large relative to its neighbours but required by Region VIII CC = 65) and Hawaii AC = 5 (the only nonzero AC in Region IX, matching the subtotal of 5). No cells were illegible or doubtful.
