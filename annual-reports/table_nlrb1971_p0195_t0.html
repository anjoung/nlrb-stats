The table is 17 columns wide with a two-level heading; the stub heading "Division and State" carries a raised footnote marker 1, but the footnote text itself is not printed within this crop (it presumably appears at the foot of the full table, on a later page). The crop is cut off at the bottom: the body ends with the "West North Central" subtotal row, and the table clearly continues with the remaining census divisions (South Atlantic onward) on the following page. Every arithmetic relation I could check is internally consistent, which supports the digit readings: for each division subtotal, each of the 16 numeric columns equals the sum of its member states; for each state row, Total elections = (union-won total) + (no representative chosen), union-won Total = AFL-CIO + Teamsters + Other national + Other local, Total valid votes cast = (votes for unions, Total) + (Total votes for no union), and votes-for-unions Total = its four components. No discrepancies were found, so no suspected misprints. Minor typographic details: the heading reads "Valid votes cast for unions" with extra letterspacing, transcribed as a single space; a stray comma-like ink mark sits just left of "391" in the West North Central row (Number of elections won by unions, Total) and was treated as a printing artifact, not part of the value. Leader dots after row labels were dropped as instructed.
