No footnote texts appear on this crop, although footnote markers are printed in the caption (Elections^1) and in the headings (Division and State^2, Total elections^3); the marker on the caption is omitted per instructions, and the heading markers are retained. Column structure: 15 columns. Two spanner headings each cover four columns — "Number of elections in which representation rights were won by unions" over Total / AFL-CIO unions / Other national unions / Other local unions, and "Valid votes cast for unions" over the second Total / AFL-CIO unions / Other national unions / Other local unions. Headings that wrap with end-of-line hyphens were rejoined ("elec- tions" = elections, "no rep- resenta- tive" = no representative, "employ- ees" = employees, "choos- ing rep- resentati on" = choosing representation). Apparent misprints, transcribed as printed: for Colorado the total valid votes cast (1,624) exceeds the number of employees eligible to vote (1,322); the same holds for Idaho (101 vs. 96) and Utah (135 vs. 107). Also as printed, Mississippi shows 1,186 eligible employees in units choosing representation against only 1,291 eligible to vote in all elections while only 12 of 15 elections were won, which may be inconsistent but is reproduced verbatim. Nothing was computed or corrected.
