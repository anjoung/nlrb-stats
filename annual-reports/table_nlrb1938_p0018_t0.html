REGIONAL OFFICES—LOCATION, TERRITORY, AND PERSONNEL" (1938 report, page 11). The three columns are (0) region number and office address, (1) territory covered, (2) personnel. No column headings are printed, so header_rows is empty. Excluded from the grid: the running head "II. THE NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD" and page number 11; the two body paragraphs above the list ("The field examiners aid the regional director..." and "The regional attorney is the legal officer..."); the section heading itself, treated as the caption; and the printer's signature line "108817—39——2" at the foot of the page. Every cell entry wraps over several printed lines with end-of-line hyphens (e.g. "Ver-mont", "Wool-worth", "Rens-selaer", "Her-rick", "di-rector", "ly-ing", "Dela-ware"); these have been rejoined. The list is cut off by the page: it continues with Region 6 and later regions on the following page. Doubtful characters: in the Region 5 stub the mark after "Baltimore" is worn and prints like a period, read here as the comma the sense requires; likewise the comma after "Blum" in the Region 5 personnel cell. Schauffler" is printed with a single "t" in "Bennet", as transcribed.
