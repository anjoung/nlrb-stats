O. alone" (including the stub heading and at least one data row) are on the preceding page. The body has 10 data columns, counted from the column rules; the 2nd, 3rd and 10th data columns hold only dash rules in every printed row, including the Total row, so they are transcribed as "". A thin rule separates the last data row from "Total"; "Total" is indented in the stub. The Total row likewise satisfies 3,108+2,917+423+686+1,334=8,468, and the 5th, 6th and 7th data columns' printed totals (2,917; 423; 686) exactly equal the sums of the rows shown here, with the residual in the 4th, 8th and 9th columns (649, 139, 788) internally consistent for the off-page rows. The final digits of the Total-row value 8,468 are thick and could be misread as "8,408"; the column arithmetic settles it as 8,468. The raised footnote markers on the row labels are small and partly broken in the scan. I read 1 on "C. I. O. alone", 5 on "A. F. L.-C. I. O.", 11 on "A. F. L.-Local unaffiliated union" and 7 on "C. I. O.-National unaffiliated union", with no marker on "A. F. L.-National unaffiliated union", "C. I. O.-Local unaffiliated union" or "A. F. L.-C. I. O.-Local unaffiliated union". That sequence is not monotonic down the stub (11 precedes 7), so at least one of these digits may be misread; the marker digits, not the data cells, are the weak point of this transcription. Footnote 3 as printed reads "24 elections", which cannot be a subset of any single row (the table totals 32 elections and the largest row shown is 14); it is transcribed as read and may be a misprint or a scan misreading of the digits. The last word of footnote 12 is faint at the right-hand edge of the crop; I read "employer." Footnote 13 is printed with a space before its closing period ("1 employer only ."), normalised here.
