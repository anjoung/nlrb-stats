The stub column has no printed heading, so cell 0 of both heading levels is empty; "Number of cases" and "Number of workers involved" are tall single headings and are repeated in both levels. Four cells contain only a parenthesized superscript 1 — the footnote reference standing in place of a value (footnote 1 = "Less than 0.05 percent."). They are transcribed as "(1)": row "(c) Pay-roll check" under "By settlement before hearing" in both the "Total cases closed" and "Total cases on docket" columns, and row "(c) Pay-roll check" under "By settlement after hearing" in the "Total number of workers involved in cases on docket" column. The row "By settlement after hearing" is a section line: its label is followed by leader dots and a dashed rule runs across all six value columns, so all its value cells are "". A stray ink/pen mark resembling a slash appears in the "Number of cases" column just left of the 72 on the row "By dismissal of petition by Board after election"; it is not part of the printed value. All printed totals are internally consistent (e.g. 454+257+241+264+480+5 = 1,701; 1,701+638 = 2,339; 3,550−2,339 = 1,211), so no misprints are suspected.
