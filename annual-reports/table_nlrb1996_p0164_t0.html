Crop is cut off at the bottom: the table ends mid-panel at "2,000 to 2,999" with the box rule running past it, so the remaining size classes of section A, any section B (decertification elections), and the footnote text are not on this image. The caption carries a superscript 1, but no footnote text is printed in the crop, so `footnotes` is empty. 13 columns, confirmed from the column rules: stub, Number eligible to vote, Total elections, Percent of total, Cumulative percent of total, then three two-column union panels under the spanner "Elections in which representation rights were won by", then two columns under "Elections in which no representative was chosen". Note the third panel's lower heading reads "Percent of size class" (not "by size class") as printed. Em-dash rules (------) are transcribed as "" throughout: the Cumulative percent cell of the "Total RC and RM elections" row, the "Other national unions - Percent by size class" cells opposite a printed 0, the "Other local unions - Percent of size class" cells opposite a printed 0, the AFL-CIO percent cell in "800 to 999", and the "no representative - Percent by size class" cell in "2,000 to 2,999". All five count columns were checked against the printed totals and every one reconciles exactly (eligible 194,062; total elections 2,792; AFL-CIO 1,154; other national 48; other local 118; no representative 1,472), so the count cells are solid. Cells re-checked on a second viewing because they disagree with the arithmetic implied by the rest of the table; each is transcribed exactly as printed and none is a reading error I can resolve further: - "800 to 999", Percent of total: printed 0.1, though 5/2,792 = 0.18 and the cumulative column jumps 99.6 -> 99.8 (the cumulative column elsewhere accumulates the rounded percents), both of which imply 0.2. Likely a misprint in the source. - "1,000 to 1,999", AFL-CIO Percent by size class: printed 0.0, though 1/1,154 = 0.09, which the table's rounding elsewhere would render 0.1. - "50 to 59" and "60 to 69", Other national unions Percent by size class: both printed 4.1, though 2/48 = 4.17 (-> 4.2) and 3/48 = 6.25 (-> 6.3, as printed in the other rows with a count of 3). The 60-to-69 value in particular looks like a typesetting error in the source, since its count of 3 is needed to make the column sum to 48. - "150 to 159" and "300 to 399", Other local unions Percent of size class: printed 2.6, though 3/118 = 2.54. - "1,000 to 1,999", Cumulative percent of total: printed 100.0, where accumulating the rounded percents gives 100.1. A stray ink mark sits in the stub under "Size of unit (number of employees)" in the heading block; it is scan noise, not a heading or a footnote marker, and is not transcribed.
