p. 273. 17 columns. The crop ends in mid-table: "Primary metal industries" is the last row on this image (its baseline touches the bottom edge) and the table continues on the page below the crop; the text of footnote 1, whose marker appears on the stub heading "Industrial group", is not visible in the crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Header wording: the second "Teamsters" column (under "Valid votes cast for unions") is printed "Team- stears", i.e. it reads "Teamstears" after repairing the line break, while the first one (under the elections panel) reads "Team- sters" = "Teamsters". I transcribed each as printed; the second may be a typesetting error in the original or a scan artifact, so verify before using the heading text. Arithmetic checks: 13 of the 15 rows reconcile exactly (won + no representative = total elections; the four union sub-columns = union total; union total + votes for no union = total valid votes cast). Two rows do not, and I transcribed them as read without correcting: - "Food and kindred products": the four union vote sub-columns (10,387 + 4,089 + 1,092 + 430) sum to 15,998, 20 more than the printed total 15,978 (which is consistent with 15,978 + 13,459 = 29,437). The most likely misread cells are AFL-CIO unions (10,387, could be 10,367) or Teamsters (4,089, could be 4,069) in the "Valid votes cast for unions" panel; the digits as printed look like 8s to me. - "Printing, publishing, and allied industries": the four union vote sub-columns (3,430 + 515 + 154 + 106) sum to 4,205, 600 less than the printed total 4,805 (which is consistent with 4,805 + 4,166 = 8,971). The shortfall of exactly 600 suggests a doubtful digit in one of AFL-CIO unions (3,430 vs 4,030), Other national unions (154 vs 754) or Other local unions (106 vs 706); I read them as 3,430, 515, 154 and 106. The scan is a 1-bit image with a serif face in which 5/6, 8/6 and 9/7 are the easiest confusions, so those two rows deserve a second look at higher magnification.
