Column structure: 17 columns — stub, "All cases", eight unfair-labor-practice columns (All C cases, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), four representation columns (All R cases, RC, RM, RD), then UD, AC and UC. The three right-hand headings ("Union deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases", "Unit clarification cases") span the top two heading levels with their code (UD/AC/UC) on the third level; "Division and State" and "All cases" likewise span levels, so they are repeated per the convention. Typesetting inconsistency, transcribed as printed: the "All C cases" column carries no digit-grouping commas anywhere (1616, 1544, 1855, 6368, 3143, 1735, 6074, 1453, 1603), unlike the neighbouring columns. In the "All R cases" column the East North Central subtotal prints as "1,135" with a comma while the Middle Atlantic subtotal prints as "1161" without one. Arithmetic cross-checks: every state row and every division subtotal reconciles both across the detail columns and down the column, which supports the digit and column assignments. For example Illinois 1,066+378+98+46+1+3+24 = 1616 and 1616+374+5+0+9 = 2,004; Idaho's unusually large UC value of 5 is confirmed by 64+9+0+1+5 = 79. No printed total appears to be in error.
