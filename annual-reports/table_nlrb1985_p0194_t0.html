No misprints detected.

The Mountain division continues (Nevada, division subtotal, etc.) past the crop, so there is no final total row and no footnote text here. The stub heading carries a raised 1 ("Division and State^1"), but the corresponding footnote is not printed on this page. The division lines (South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central) are subtotal rows set off by rules; they are transcribed as ordinary rows with their values. Arithmetic cross-checks all pass: in every row the four union breakdown columns sum to the adjacent "Total", won + no-representative elections equal "Total elections", and union votes + no-union votes equal "Total valid votes cast"; the three division subtotals also equal the sums of their member states. No digits were doubtful; the only typographic hazard was the frequent broken/faint "1" glyphs in the single-digit columns, which were resolved by these cross-checks.

p. 196. The crop is the final page of a continued table: the Mountain division's earlier States appear on the preceding page, so "Nevada" is the first printed body row here and the "Mountain" subtotal that follows covers States not all shown. Indented rows (Mountain, Pacific, Outlying Areas, Total, all States and areas) are division/area subtotals printed between horizontal rules; they carry values and are transcribed as ordinary rows. Arithmetic checks (performed only to validate the reading, nothing was altered): every one of the 16 numeric columns of the Pacific subtotal equals the sum of Washington through Guam, and every column of the Outlying Areas subtotal equals Puerto Rico plus Virgin Islands, so column alignment in those blocks is confirmed. Doubtful cells: in the Hawaii row, "Total votes for no union" has a small stray ink mark just left of the figure; I read the value as 118 with no leading digit. The Guam row's large eligible/valid-vote figures (1,701 and 1,543) against only 3 elections look anomalous but are clearly printed and are consistent with the Pacific subtotal, so they are transcribed as printed. In the footnote, the periods in "U.S." and the terminal period are faint/broken in the scan; they are rendered here in their conventional form.
