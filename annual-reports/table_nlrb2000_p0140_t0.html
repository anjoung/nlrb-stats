Typesetting inconsistency, transcribed as printed: in the "All C cases" column the four-digit figures are printed without a thousands comma ("2288", "3361", "1322"), while the same magnitudes carry commas elsewhere in the row ("2,740", "3,980", "1,566", "1,631", "2,249", "1,002"). Indentation is not preserved in the stub text. All row and column sums checked internally are consistent as printed.

This is a continuation page: no footnote text is printed on the crop, although footnote marker 1 appears in the caption and marker 2 on the stub heading "Industrial Group". The table is cut off after "Air Transportation", which is the first row of a new section continuing on the following page. Structure of the crop: the opening row "32-Manufacturing" is a subtotal line whose detail rows appear on the previous page; the eight detail rows that follow it sum to the "33-Manufacturing" subtotal printed beneath them (same pattern for "Wholesale Trade", "44-Retail Trade" and "45-Retail Trade", whose subtotals follow their detail rows). The horizontal rules in the image confirm this grouping. Digit grouping is inconsistent in the source and is transcribed as printed: e.g. "1928", "4267", "1313" and "1189" are printed without a thousands comma while "2,275", "1,560", "4,966", "3,198", "1,029", "1,469", "1,034" and "1,528" carry one. Every row's printed components add to its printed totals (C columns to "All C cases", R columns to "All R cases", and those plus UD/AC/UC to "All cases"), and each detail block sums to its subtotal, so no misprints are suspected.
