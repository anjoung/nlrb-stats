It is a figure legend keyed to the bar shading, not a column heading, and it does not align with the year columns, so it is not included in header_rows. The only printed column headings are the eleven years 1965-1975; the stub column has no heading. Decimal points: the scan is a coarse bitmap and the period in most values has dropped out, so many cells read as a space (e.g. "19 4", "16 1", "35 5") while a few retain a visible point ("36.2", "34.7", "32.3", "31.6", "30.2"). The first two row labels wrap over two printed lines and are joined here. In the "Total merit factor" row the 1970 cell shows a stray mark above the gap between the digits ("34 2"); it is scan noise over the decimal point, not a footnote marker.
