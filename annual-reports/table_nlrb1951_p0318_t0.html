Because the grid must be rectangular with one set of header rows, the grid is laid out on panel B's 13 columns (the wider panel), and header_rows reproduces panel B's three printed heading levels only. The two centered sub-captions are transcribed as label-only rows so the panel boundary is recoverable; they are printed centered above each heading block, not in the stub column. DECIMAL POINTS: all percent columns are printed with a decimal point that the 1-bit scan renders faint or drops, so the page reads e.g. "87 5", "20 4".
