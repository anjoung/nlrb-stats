Reader’s notes

p. 203. Ten printed columns; the "Temporary restraining order" spanner covers columns 4-5 ("Date issued", "Date lifted"). All other headings are tall single headings and are repeated in both heading levels. Empty cells are printed as dash rules (------) in most body rows and have been transcribed as "". Doubtful / noteworthy cells: - Row 15-CC-1,2,3,4, column "Date temporary injunction granted": a small raised mark follows "Feb. 17, 1948"; it reads as a superscript 1 and is transcribed as Feb. 17, 1948^1. Note that the only footnote 1 printed under the table is attached to the table caption and concerns the tabulation as a whole, so this body marker may be a different reference or a scan artifact. - Row 2-CC-40, column "Date issued": "June 29, 1948" carries a raised 2, matching footnote 2 ("Consent injunction or restraining order"). - Row 15-CC-1,2,3,4, column "Date injunction dissolved after Board order": printed as "July 1949" with a wide gap and no day; transcribed "July 1949" as printed. - Row 21-CB-8, column "Date injunction proceedings withdrawn or dismissed": printed as "May 1948" with a wide gap and no day; transcribed "May 1948" as printed. - Case numbers "15-CC-1,2,3,4" and "21-CC-25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 34" each end in a dot in the scan that could be either a terminal period or the first leader dot; the dot has been dropped in both. - Row 9-CC-2: "Dec. 2, 1947" sits in the "Date injunction proceedings withdrawn or dismissed" column (right of the column rule at the "Date injunction denied" boundary), not in the "denied" column; this was checked against the rules twice. - Case-number hyphens are printed as en-dashes and have been transcribed as plain hyphens. - The caption carries footnote marker 1 and is excluded per instructions, as are the running head ("Fourteenth Annual Report of the National Labor Relations Board") and the page number (186) printed vertically at the right edge. - Row 2-CC-23, 24, column "Type of petition": "10 (l)" appears with a stray overline above "10" in the scan; read as plain "10 (l)".
