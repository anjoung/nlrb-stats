I restored them, since the values are unambiguously decimals (e.g. 237/338 = 70.1) and the abbreviations are standard. Commas and other punctuation render normally, so only periods were affected. Doubtful cell: row "5 New Orleans, La.", Set aside / Cumulative fiscal years 1978-1982 / Percent reads "5 3" (transcribed 5.3). The arithmetic gives 38/248 = 15.3, and every other cell in that column is two digits before the decimal; the leading "1" appears to be missing from the print or dropped by the scan. Transcribed as printed, not corrected. All column sums were checked against the "Total all circuits" row and all twelve circuit rows against their own totals; everything reconciles exactly.
