Reader’s notes

p. 205. 23 columns: stub, two "Total" columns, then five disposition groups (Affirmed in full; Modified; Remanded in full; Affirmed in part and remanded in part; Set aside), each with a Fiscal Year 1990 Number/Percent pair and a Cumulative fiscal years 1985-1989 Number/Percent pair. The level-2 heading is printed "Fiscal year 1990" under "Affirmed in full" but "Fiscal Year 1990" under the other four groups; both are kept as printed. Decimal points: this 1-bit scan drops many periods in the percent columns, so cells print as e.g. "78 9", "4 4", "7 8", "5 5", "18 2", "4 8", "9 0", "76 0", "8 1", "3 1", "6 0", "40", "71", "53". All have been written with the decimal point ("78.9", "4.4", ...). This is confirmed arithmetically: every row of percents sums to 100.0 and every column of counts foots exactly to the "Total all circuits" line (e.g. FY1990 counts 127+7+6+9+12=161; cumulative 727+50+58+23+73=931; and each of the 12 circuit rows' counts sums to its own two totals). So the column placement and digits are verified, not merely read. Em dashes: every blank percent cell in the body is printed as an em dash "—" (the table's nil symbol, always paired with a printed 0 in the Number column to its left). Per the instruction to treat dash cells as empty, these are transcribed as "". A reader who needs the distinction should know the image shows "—" there, never a truly blank cell. Row labels: the period after the circuit number and in "Phila., PA" / "St. Louis, MO" is faint or lost in the scan ("1 Boston, MA", "3. Phila , PA", "8. St Louis, MO", "9 San Francisco, CA"); punctuation has been regularised to the table's evident style. The last row, the D.C. Circuit, carries no number as printed.
