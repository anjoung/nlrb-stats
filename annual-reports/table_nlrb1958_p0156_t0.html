  1. 1 Definitions of Types of Cases Used in Tables.—The following designations, used by the Board in numbering cases, are used in the tables in this appendix to designate the various types of cases CA: A charge of unfair labor practices against an employer under sec. 8 (a) CB: A charge of unfair labor practices against a union under sec 8 (b) (1), (2), (3), (5), (6) CC: A charge of unfair labor practices against a union under sec 8 (b) (4) (A), (B), (C). RC: A petition by a labor organization or employees for certification of a representative for purposes of collective bargaining under sec 9 (c) (1) (A) (i). RM: A petition by employer for certification of a representative for purposes of collective bargaining under sec. 9 (c) (1) (B). RD: A petition by employees under sec 9 (c) (1) (A) (ii) asserting that the union previously certified or currently recognized by their employer as the bargaining representative, no longer represents a majority of the employees in the appropriate unit UD A petition by employees under sec. 9 (e) (1) asking for a referendum to rescind a bargaining agent's authority to make a union-shop contract under sec 8 (a) (3)

Reader’s notes

p. 156. Six columns: stub plus Total, AFL-CIO affiliates, Unaffiliated unions, Individuals, Employers. The stub column has no printed heading, so the stub cells of the three heading levels are empty. "Number of cases" spans all five data columns; "Total" is a tall heading spanning heading levels 2 and 3; "Identification of complainant or petitioner" spans the last four columns. The four panel headings ("All cases 1", "Unfair labor practice cases", "Representation cases", "Union-shop deauthorization cases") are printed centered across the data columns rather than in the stub; they are transcribed as label-only section rows. The raised 1 on "All cases" is the footnote marker for the long note below the table. In the Union-shop deauthorization block the Employers column holds a dash rule (------------) in all five rows; these are transcribed as empty cells. In footnote 1 the roman numerals in "sec 9 (c) (1) (A) (i)" (RC) and "sec 9 (c) (1) (A) (ii)" (RD) are set in a degraded italic face that scans like "(1)" and "(11)"; they are lowercase roman i and ii. Several periods after "sec" are missing or faint in the original and are transcribed as printed; the item markers are inconsistent in the original ("CA:", "CB:", "CC:" with colons but "CD." with a period, "RD" with a raised dot, and "UD" with no punctuation) and are left as read. Terminal sentence periods are absent after "various types of cases", "sec. 8 (a)", "(5), (6)", "the appropriate unit", and "sec 8 (a) (3)" in the original. No arithmetic was altered. As a check only: every column adds correctly (pending July 1 + received = on docket; on docket − closed = pending June 30), the four identification columns sum to Total in every row, and the three case-type panels sum to the All cases panel in every row.
