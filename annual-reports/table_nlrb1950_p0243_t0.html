Column assignments were checked against the printed arithmetic and are internally consistent: "Decisions issued, total" = 39+276+102+4+2,479+51 = 2,951, and the four decision sub-rows likewise sum to 2,951 in the All-cases column and to each group's own total (e.g. LMRA R 1,627+561+291 = 2,479). The Formal-actions columns overshoot their totals by exactly 21 (All cases 2,384 vs 2,363; LMRA R 2,008 vs 1,987), matching footnote 3's "21 decisions", which confirms the readings 2,363, 1,987, 1,261, 1,256, 199 and 198. Doubtful cells and marks: - The "Formal actions" sub-heading over the NLRA R cases group carries a superscript that reads more like 3 than 2 in this bitonal scan; it is transcribed as ^2 to match the five parallel sub-headings, all of which take footnote 2 (footnote 3 concerns decisions, not formal-action counts). - "Cases heard" / Union-shop authorization cases, Number of cases: printed 5 followed by a stray tick-like speck; transcribed as 5. - "Dismissals on record" / LMRA R cases, Number of cases: a stray short dash precedes 291; it is a leader-rule artifact, not part of the figure. - "Notices of hearing issued" has no entries in the NLRA C and CA columns, which the total confirms (18+1+2,599+7 = 2,625). Blank cells in the right-hand groups are printed as dash rules in some rows and left empty in others; both are given as "". Either the 41 or one of the components is wrong in the original.
