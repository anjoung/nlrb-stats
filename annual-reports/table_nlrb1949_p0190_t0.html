I have written the decimal point in every Percent cell. No digit was changed, added or removed, and nothing was computed or rounded. Likewise, percents below 1 are set with a leading point and no leading zero; where that point did not survive I restored it, in these cells: Rubber products / Elections Percent (.8); Aircraft and parts / Elections Percent (.6); Professional, scientific, and controlling instruments / Elections Percent (.9) and Eligible voters Percent (.7); Printing, publishing, and allied industries / Eligible voters Percent (.9); Tobacco manufacturers / Eligible voters Percent (.5), Valid votes cast Percent (.5), Elections Percent (.1). The stub heading "Industrial group 1" carries a superscript footnote marker and spans all three heading levels, as do the Number/Percent pairs under Elections, Eligible voters and Valid votes cast (those headings sit at the second level and span down to the third).
