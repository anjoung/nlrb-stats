The third digit is a degraded glyph that could be a 9; every other cell in that row and column cross-foots exactly, so the discrepancy is isolated to this one cell. Many numbers are typeset with the thousands comma rendered as a bare space in this scan (e.g. "158 046", "13 656", "41 972", "15 020", "5 156", "26 835", "9 300", "3 778", "13 175", "14 953", "10 462", "3 407", "2 357"); these have been written with commas per instruction.
