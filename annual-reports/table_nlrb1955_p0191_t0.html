The two union-affiliation headings are set letterspaced in the original ("A F L", "C I O"); I have closed the letterspacing to "AFL affiliates" and "CIO affiliates". Arithmetic cross-checks all pass: every section detail block sums to its section line, and the nine section lines (Manufacturing, Forestry, Mining, Construction, Wholesale trade, Retail trade, Finance, Transportation, Services) sum to the printed Total row in all seven value columns.
