"do" is the printed ditto abbreviation: row 2-CC-12 under "Date injunction proceedings withdrawn or dismissed" and row 2-CC-14 under "Date petition for injunction filed". Row 21-CB-8 shows "Mar 8, 1948" with no visible period after "Mar", while the row above shows "Mar. 8, 1948"; transcribed as seen. Several abbreviation periods inside parentheses are faint or absent in this 1-bit scan ("Co )", "Corp )", "et al )", "Co ,"); they are transcribed as they appear rather than repaired. Row cells shown with a dash rule or blank are transcribed as empty.

p. 262. Crop shows only the last three body rows of a table continued from the preceding page; no column headings are printed on this image, so header_rows is empty. Column count (10) was taken from the dash rules: stub (case number), name of case, date filed, statutory section, then six further columns. In each row most of those six columns hold only a dash rule ("--------"), transcribed as "". The dated cells fall as follows: rows 1 and 2 have their date in column 6 (the third of the six), while row 3 has its date one column further right, in column 7. The final column (10) holds a date in all three rows. Doubtful cells / readings: - Column 4 ("10 (l)" in rows 1 and 2): the character in parentheses is a lowercase letter l that scans very much like the digit 1. Leader dots dropped per instruction. - Row 2, name column: printed as "(Di Georgio Wine & Fruit Cos )." — the period after "Cos" is faint/uncertain in the scan; transcribed as "Cos.)." The spelling "Di Georgio" is as printed (the company is usually spelled Di Giorgio); not corrected. - Row 1, column 6: a small raised 4 is printed immediately before "June 29, 1948"; recorded as the footnote marker "June 29, 1948^4".
