Given the preceding row is "2,000 to 2,999" and the class must close the series, it is almost certainly "3,000 to 9,999", but I transcribed only the characters that are legible. DROPPED DECIMAL POINTS. This is a 1-bit scan in which the period of a decimal number is sometimes lost, leaving a blank gap (e.g. "19 5", "0 7"). Every column affected is a one-decimal percent or cumulative-percent column where all other entries carry exactly one decimal place, so I read the gap as a decimal point. The cells where the point is not visible in the image are: "10 to 19" Percent of total (19.5); "20 to 29" Other local unions Percent (10.2); "80 to 89" Teamsters Percent (1.1); "100 to 109" Other national unions Percent (1.9); "120 to 129" Other local unions Percent (3.1); "130 to 139" Other national unions Percent (1.9); "140 to 149" Percent of total (0.7); "150 to 159" Other local unions Percent (0.8); "160 to 169" Cumulative percent (91.9); "180 to 189" Percent of total (0.7); "190 to 199" Other local unions Percent (1.6); "200 to 299" Percent of total (2.7) and Cumulative percent (96.5); "400 to 499" Teamsters Percent (0.4); "500 to 599" no-representative Percent (0.8); "600 to 799" Cumulative percent (99.3) and Other national unions Percent (1.9); "800 to 999" no-representative Percent (0.4); "2,000 to 2,999" no-representative Percent (0.1); last row Percent of total (0.1).
