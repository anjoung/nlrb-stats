p. 182. Seven columns: stub plus Total, Consent, Stipulated, Board-directed, Regional Director-directed, and Expedited elections under 8(b)(7)(C). The section lines (All types, total; RC cases; RM cases; RD cases; UD cases) carry no values; the three indented lines below each (Elections, Eligible voters, Valid votes) hold the figures. In the UD cases block, the Expedited elections under 8(b)(7)(C) column shows a dash rule on all three lines (Elections, Eligible voters, Valid votes), transcribed as "". The RM cases "Elections" line has a small stray ink mark in the gutter just right of the Stipulated value 151; it is a speck/stray comma from the printing, not a digit or footnote marker, and was not transcribed. The single footnote is clipped by the bottom edge of the crop — the line reads "See Glossary of terms for definitions" with the final punctuation cut off; a terminal period is assumed. The footnote marker 1 also appears on the caption (excluded per instructions). Arithmetic check (not applied to the cells, only verified): in every one of the six value columns, the RC + RM + RD + UD figures sum exactly to the "All types, total" figure for Elections, Eligible voters, and Valid votes.
