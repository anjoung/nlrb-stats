p. 312. The crop ends at the bottom of the last body row; no footnote texts are printed on this image, although markers 1, 2, 3 and 4 are referenced in the body, so the footnote block (and probably further rows) lies below the crop. The table may therefore continue on the following page. Footnote references that stand alone as a whole cell are printed as small raised parenthesized numerals; they are transcribed as "(1)" and "(2)" (column "Date temporary injunction granted" for 21-CC-92, 3-CC-19, Longshoremen Local 13, 14-CC-24, 2-CD-40, 10-CC-24,25 and 21-CC-114; column "Date of Board decision and/or order" for 17-CC-10). Two markers are printed as raised digits to the LEFT of a date and have been moved behind a caret: "3 Feb 11, 1950" (row 5-CB-43,44,45,46,47, "Date temporary injunction granted") and "4 Aug. 14, 1951" (row 2-CB-135,145,330,373,381, "Date of Board decision and/or order"). Cells holding only a dashed leader rule ("------") are transcribed as "". The two Longshoremen & Warehousemen rows (Local 48 and Local 13) have no printed case number of their own; their stub cells are left empty as printed, and they appear to belong with the preceding 20-CD-17, 18 entry. Case number "2-CA-1925, 1934 2-CB-628." is printed on two lines ("2-CA-1925, 1934" / "2-CB-628.") and has been joined into one label. Punctuation uncertainty from the 1-bit scan: several abbreviations show a gap where a period would sit but no period is legible — "(Jamestown Builders Exchange, Inc )", "Anheuser-Busch, Inc , and", "(Royden Ice Co )", "(Union Chevrolet Co )". These are transcribed without the period, as seen; the original very likely reads "Inc." and "Co.". Likewise the abbreviations in dates are inconsistent in the original and are transcribed as seen ("Aug 13, 1951", "Jan 30, 1951", "Aug 30, 1951", "Jan 28, 1952", "Dec 18, 1951", "Jan 4, 1952", "Feb 11, 1950" without a period; the rest with one). The filing dates of the last three rows are out of chronological order as printed (June 1 and June 14 follow June 12/June 18); transcribed as printed.

p. 313. This crop is a continuation page: no column headings are printed on it, so header_rows is empty. The grid has 10 columns, fixed by the printed vertical rules: (0) case number stub, (1) name of respondent/party, (2) date of charge/filing, (3) type of injunction "10 (j)" or "10 (l)", and six further date columns (indices 4-9), all of whose headings fall on the preceding page. Columns 4-9 are mostly dash rules, which are transcribed as "". Doubtful footnote markers (two raised glyphs I could not resolve at this resolution): - Row "20-CA-523,524, ...", stub: a small raised mark follows "567," on the second printed line. I read it as a footnote reference and wrote "567,^3", but the digit is indistinct and could be 4 (or, less likely, 2). Of the four printed footnotes, only 3 and 4 are otherwise unused on this page, and footnote 4's text names cases 2-CB-330, 373, which do not appear here. - Row "6-CC-59", column index 6: a thin raised mark sits immediately left of "(2)". I transcribed the cell as "(2)^3"; the mark may instead be a scan speck, in which case the cell is simply "(2)". Faint periods: this is a 1-bit scan in which many abbreviation periods have dropped out. Where a space before a closing parenthesis or comma makes the missing period unambiguous I restored it — "(South Porto Rico Sugar Co )." → "Co.)."; "(Mc Enery Co ," → "Co.,"; "Door Corp )", "Ferro-Co. Corp )", "Transfer Corp )", "Hammermill Paper Co )", "Henry Heide, Inc )", "National Cylinder Gas Co )", "Atlas Canning Co )", "Capital Service, Inc )", "Carriers of Ind )", "Nordan Plastics Corp )", "Cargill, Inc ", "(McDowell Company, Inc )". Likewise "Dec 17, 1951" in row "6-CC-60, 6-CD-14." is printed without a visible period after "Dec"; I wrote "Dec. 17, 1951". The injunction-type column is printed with a lowercase letter l, "10 (l)", which the scan sometimes renders like the digit 1; I used "10 (l)" throughout for the mandatory-injunction rows, consistent with the NOTE under the table. Row "20-CD-28" is one such case. End-of-line hyphens repaired in labels: "Ply-wood" → "Plywood"; "Living-ston" → "Livingston"; "Steam-ship" → "Steamship" (two rows); "Un-ion" → "Union" and "Individ-ual" → "Individual" in the first row. The hyphen in "Ferro-Co. Corp." is part of the company name, not a line break, and was kept. Minor stray specks in the dash-rule areas (row 1 far-right column; row "2-CC-215" column index 7; row "24-CB-40" column index 6, where a dot interrupts the dash rule) were treated as scan dirt, not content. The lowercase "association" in the "24-CC-14, 15, 16; 24-CD-2, 3, 4" row is as printed.
