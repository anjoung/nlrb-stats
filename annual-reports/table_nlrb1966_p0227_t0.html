The crop is the first page of the table: it ends after Kansas (a double rule follows), so the remaining divisions/States and any footnote text (including the note keyed to the superscript 1 on the stub heading "Division and State") are on the continuation page and are not visible here. Doubtful cell: Maine, "Number of employees eligible to vote". The glyph after "3," is damaged in the scan; I read it as 6 and transcribed "3,612" as printed, but every other column of the New England block foots exactly to the division line, while this one does not: the six State values sum to 31,589 against the printed division total of 30,989 — a difference of exactly 600, which would vanish if the digit were 0 (3,012). Either the digit is a 0 and my read is wrong, or the State line is a misprint. All other arithmetic in the crop checks out (each State's union-vote components sum to its union total, union + no-union equals total valid votes cast, and the State lines foot to their division lines for every other column and division). Header misprint: in the "Valid votes cast for unions" panel the Teamsters column heading is set as "Team- sters sters", i.e. the syllable "sters" is repeated on an extra line; I transcribed it as "Teamsters". Typesetting details: New York's AFL-CIO valid votes is printed "23 717" with a space and no comma (transcribed 23,717); South Dakota's AFL-CIO valid votes is printed "1; 114" (transcribed 1,114); Rhode Island's AFL-CIO valid votes has a stray ink dot before "634"; Connecticut's won-elections "Total" has a stray mark after "49" that is ink damage, not a footnote marker (21+22+6+0 = 49 confirms the digits).
