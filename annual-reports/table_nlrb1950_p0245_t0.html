Reader’s notes

p. 245. Panel designator: a centered line "A. ELECTIONS" is printed between the caption and the column headings (this crop is part A of Table 15; the vote-count part B is not on this image). It sits above the table rules and headings, so it is treated as caption material and is not transcribed as a row. Decimal points: this is a 1-bit scan in which many periods fail to reproduce. Most percent cells print as e.g. "21 3", "15 8", "25 5", "100 0", "69 2" with a gap where the decimal point belongs (a few, such as "37. 4", "71.9", "2.1", "8.7", "30.8", retain a visible dot). All percent values are one-decimal figures and have been transcribed with the decimal point restored. The same loss affects periods inside the abbreviations in row labels and headings ("A. F of L.", "C I. O."); these are given in the standard forms "A. F. of L." and "C. I. O." throughout. No digits were altered. Zero percents are typeset inconsistently in the original — some rows print "0" and others print ".0" (no leading zero). These are transcribed exactly as printed, which is why both forms appear in the percent columns. Row 16 label: printed as "A. F. of L. C. I. O. affiliates—unaffiliated unions". Unlike the other combination rows, the word "affiliates" does not appear after "A. F. of L.", and the connector between "L." and "C." is too faint to resolve; I have transcribed it as an em dash to match the pattern of the neighbouring rows, but the exact character there is uncertain. Row 16, "No union / Percent" column: reads "2.1" as printed, but 1 of 49 elections is 2.0 percent, so this is either a misprint in the original or a 0/1 ambiguity in the scan. Transcribed as read, not corrected. Empty cells in the body are printed as dash rules (------) and are given as "". Arithmetic check (not a correction, only a confidence check): in every row the four "Elections won by" Number cells sum exactly to the "Number of elections" cell, including the Total row (2,101 + 1,199 + 886 + 1,433 = 5,619), which confirms that each figure sits in the column shown.
