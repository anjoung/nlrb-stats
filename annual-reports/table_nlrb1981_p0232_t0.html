p. 232. Decimal points: the 1-bit scan drops most decimal points in the percent columns, so they appear on screen as a space ("22 0", "100 0"). The points are printed — one survives legibly in the "60 to 69" Teamsters percent ("2.8") — and the cumulative-percent column only adds up if they are decimals, so every percent cell is transcribed with its decimal point. Verification: all five "Number" columns sum down to the printed Total RC and RM row (1,866 / 829 / 123 / 201 / 3,637), each data row's five numbers sum to its "Total elections" value, the elections column sums to 6,656, "Number eligible to vote" sums to 403,837, and the cumulative-percent column reproduces the running sum of the percent-of-total column exactly. The integer columns are therefore cross-checked and reliable. Doubtful / apparently misprinted percent cells (transcribed as printed, not corrected): "Under 10" Teamsters percent by size class reads 38.9 but 322/829 = 38.8; "20 to 29" Other national unions percent reads 15.5 but 19/123 = 15.4; "200 to 299" Other national unions percent reads 2.5 but 3/123 = 2.4; "40 to 49" Other local unions percent reads 5.9 but 12/201 = 6.0. In each case the last digit is small and slightly broken in the scan, so either a misprint in the source or a one-digit misreading is possible. Blank cells: the Total RC and RM row's "Cumulative percent of total" cell holds only leader dots and is transcribed as "". Where a "Number" cell is 0 the matching "Percent by size class" cell is generally left blank in the source; those are transcribed as "".
