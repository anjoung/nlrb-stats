p. 126. Eleven columns: stub plus Number/Percent pairs for All R, RC, RM, RD and UD cases. Em-dash rules in Percent columns (and the blank percent cells on the "After Board decision and direction of election" row) are transcribed as "". Scan quality: this is a 1-bit scan in which some decimal points in the Percent columns dropped out. I have transcribed them with the decimal point, since the surrounding typesetting makes the intent unambiguous, but the affected cells print as a gap on the image: "Stipulated election" All R Percent (shown as "53 9", read 53.9, with a stray ink tick above the gap); "After hearing closed, before decision" (under Consent election) RC Percent ("0 0" → 0.0); "Expedited election" RM Percent ("0 5" → 0.5); "By withdrawal, total" UD Percent ("37 6" → 37.6); "After Regional Director's decision and direction of election" All R Percent ("1 8" → 1.8); "After hearing closed, before decision" (under By dismissal) All R Percent ("0 0" → 0.0); "By Regional Director's decision" RM Percent ("3 5" → 3.5). In the row "Before notice of hearing" under "Consent election", the RC cases Number cell is printed as ". 6" — a stray dot (most likely a leader-dot artifact) sits to the left of the digit. Transcribed as 6; the column total structure (7 = 6 + 1) supports that reading. Rows under "After:" (Consent election, Stipulated election, Expedited election, Regional Director-directed election, Board-directed election) and the indented sub-rows beneath them are printed at successive indents; indentation is not represented in the grid.
