p. 104. 13 columns: stub, Elections and pay-roll checks (Number, Percent), Valid votes cast (Number, Percent), and Winner split into four two-column pairs (A. F. of L., C. I. O., Unaffiliated, No union). The page ends after "Miscellaneous manufacturing", so the table is incomplete here: the "Total" line (4,153 elections) exceeds the "Manufacturing" line (3,307), and the nonmanufacturing industry groups that make up the difference must continue on the following page. No "Continued" line is printed on this crop. All count columns were cross-checked and are internally consistent: in every row the four Winner numbers sum to the "Elections and pay-roll checks" number, and the manufacturing detail lines (including the Transportation equipment subtotal and Miscellaneous manufacturing) sum exactly to 3,307 elections and 1,020,318 valid votes. Confidence in the count columns is accordingly high. The percent columns are the faint ones in this scan, and the report mixes rounding with truncation (e.g. Rubber products C. I. O. 53.5 and Machinery (except electrical) No union 13.3 are truncated, while Tobacco manufactures A. F. of L. 68.8 is rounded), so arithmetic cannot settle every doubtful digit. Two percent cells are read with lower confidence and are transcribed exactly as they appear: "Chemicals and allied products" / No union Percent, read as 9.1 (19 of 210 implies 9.0); and "Aircraft and parts" / No union Percent, read as 10.0 (18 of 181 implies 9.9). Either could be a misprint or a misread of a worn digit. Minor marks that were not transcribed: a stray ink dot before the C. I. O. number 33 in "Furniture and finished lumber products", before the Unaffiliated number 13 in "Products of petroleum and coal", and before the A. F. of L. number 1 in "Other"; these are printing speckle, not footnote markers. In the "Other" row the No union columns print a bare "0" for both the number and the percent.
