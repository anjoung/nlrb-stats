p. 277. 15 columns: stub, 4 single columns, four two-column union groups under the spanner "Elections in which representation rights were won by", and a final two-column group. The crop shows only panel A; the table continues (panel B etc.) on material outside this crop. Doubtful cell: the "Other local unions / Number" figure in the "Total RC and RM elections" row is a degraded glyph that could read 237 or 287. I transcribed 237 because the column's 30 size-class entries sum to exactly 237, the row's five union/no-representative numbers then sum exactly to the printed 8,472, and 49/237 = 20.7 matches the printed first-row percent (49/287 would be 17.1). The scan drops many decimal points in the percent columns (e.g. "3 8" and "77 1" in the 50 to 59 row, "94 0" in 190 to 199, "99 8" in 1,000 to 1,999, "0 8" in 10,000 and over). Every such cell was transcribed with the decimal point that the column's typography requires; no digits were altered. Cells printed as a dash rule are given as "": the Cumulative percent cell of the total row, and the "Percent by size class" cells wherever the adjacent count is 0 (80 to 89 and 180 to 189 other local; 190 to 199, 800 to 999, 2,000 to 2,999, 3,000 to 9,999 and 10,000 and over Teamsters; 2,000 to 2,999 AFL-CIO; 3,000 to 9,999 and 10,000 and over other national; 500 to 599 other local; 10,000 and over no representative).
