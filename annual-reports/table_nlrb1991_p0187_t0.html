p. 187. The crop ends mid-table: the last printed row, "Compliance with", is the first line of a new major category and its remaining sub-rows (and any footnote block, including the texts for markers 1 and 2) fall on the following page, so `footnotes` is empty. Decimal points: the scan drops or greys out the period in a number of percent cells, which print as e.g. "100 0" (Total number of cases closed, CG percent), "100 0" (Agreement of the parties, All C "Percent of total method"), "18 8" (Agreement of the parties, CB percent), "44 3" (Informal settlement, CC percent), "0 3" (Formal settlement, CA percent), "0 1" and "0 0" (first "Stipulated decision" row, All C "Percent of total method" and CB percent), "2 2" (Compliance with, All C "Percent of total closed") and "6 1" (Compliance with, CE percent). All are restored with the decimal point; each is confirmed by the surrounding column pattern and, where checkable, by the ratio to the column total (e.g. 3/49 = 6.1, 345/778 = 44.3, 16/16 = 100.0). Row "Total number of cases closed", column All C "Percent of total method": printed as "0.0". This looks like a misprint — the cell is normally blank or 100.0 in this table — but it is transcribed as printed. Row "Before issuance of complaint", column CD cases "Number": the cell holds a parenthesised raised 2, i.e. a footnote reference standing in place of a value, transcribed as "(2)". Its adjoining CD percent cell is a dash rule and is "". Arithmetic suggests the underlying count is 0 (the CD informal-settlement total of 1 is fully accounted for by the next row), but nothing is computed here. Dash rules ("——") used in place of a percentage are rendered as "" throughout; printed zeros are kept as "0". Specks of ink appear beside the CD "Number" cell of "After hearing opened, before issuance of administrative law judge's decision" and the CE "Number" cell of "After hearing opened"; these are scanning artifacts, not digits or markers. The caption carries footnote marker 1 and is omitted per instructions.

Running head bleed-through: the word "Appendix" is overprinted across the header band at the bottom-left of the stub heading / "Number" column of All C cases. It is the page running head, not a table heading, and is excluded. Dropped decimal points: the scan loses the period in many one-decimal percent cells, which print as e.g. "0 7", "2 5", "31 8", "33 8", "56 2", "6 1", "0 1", "22 6", "2 0", "0 0", "28 1".
