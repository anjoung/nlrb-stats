(slightly clipped by the bottom of the crop); it carries no marker, so `footnotes` is empty. The table continues onto later pages (the alphabetical list stops mid-F). Parenthesized footnote references standing alone as a cell value are printed as a raised digit inside parentheses; I have transcribed them as "(1)", "(4)" with no caret, since the whole parenthesis is the cell content rather than a marker attached to a value. Raised markers printed to the LEFT of a date in the "Decision issued" column have been moved behind a caret after the date, per the marker rule: Belding Hosiery Co., Bemis Bros. Bag Co. (first line), American White Cross Laboratories, Bradburn Motors Co., Cayuga Linen & Cotton Mills, Colonial Togs Co., Colt-Brady Co., Deere John Harvester Works, Evelyn Coats, Faribault Woolen Mills. The marker on "Evelyn Coats" is clearly a different, taller digit, read as 5. The marker in the "Decision issued" cell for "A. S. Beck Shoe Corporation" is read as (4) but is faint and could be (6). The trailing superscript on the "Closed" cell of "Allied Yarns Corporation" (Sept. 22, 1939) is the only marker printed after a value in that column and is read as 3 with the same uncertainty. Values printed outside the stated fiscal year, transcribed as printed and not corrected: "Decatur Iron and Steel Co." closed July 19, 1940; "Eavenson & Levering Co., Inc." closed July 3, 1940; "Allied Yarns Corporation" decision Aug. 26, 1940; "Evelyn Coats" opened Dec. 1, 1938 (the only 1938 date on the page). "Do" (ditto) rows under American Hat Mfg. Co., Bemis Bros. Bag Co. and Cudahy Packing Co. are indented in the original; the indentation is not reproduced in the stub text. Blank typographic gaps between alphabetical letter groups are spacing only, not printed rows, and are not transcribed.
