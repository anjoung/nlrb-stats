p. 126. The table is cut off by the crop: it ends mid-section after the "Maryland" row (the South Atlantic division block is incomplete and has no subtotal here), and it continues on the following page. No footnote texts are printed on this crop, although footnote markers appear in the caption (Fiscal Year 1997^1) and in the stub heading ("Division and State^2"); the marker on the stub heading is retained in header_rows. 17 columns: stub, "All cases", eight columns under "Unfair labor practice cases" (All C cases, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), four under "Representation cases" (All R cases, RC, RM, RD), then UD, AC and UC. The headings "Union deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases" and "Unit clarification cases" each sit above their one-letter code (UD/AC/UC) and are repeated across the two upper heading levels. The scan has lost the dots on many lowercase i's (e.g. "Maine", "Missouri", "Michigan" print as "Mame", "Missoun", "Michigan"); row labels are given with their intended spelling. Stray ink specks appear next to several figures (e.g. the dot before "6" in New Hampshire RC, before "44" in Ohio RD, before "23" in Wisconsin RD, and the apparent leading mark on Pennsylvania CE "7" and New Jersey CD column); these are read as printing artifacts, not footnote markers or digits. Pennsylvania CE is read as 7, consistent with the Middle Atlantic CE subtotal of 16 (1+8+7). All row and column sums in the body were checked against the printed division subtotals and the "All cases"/"All C cases"/"All R cases" totals, and every one is internally consistent as transcribed.

p. 127. Crop content: the page opens in the middle of the South Atlantic division.
