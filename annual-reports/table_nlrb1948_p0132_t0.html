Reader’s notes

p. 132. Layout: this is a two-column list table (case number in the stub, company name in column 1) printed as two side-by-side panels separated by a vertical rule, under two centered section headings that span the full page width ("I. Unfair Labor Practice Cases" and "II. Representation Cases"). No column headings are printed, so header_rows is empty. Each section has its own left and right panel; I transcribed section I's left panel, then section I's right panel, then the section II heading, then section II's left panel, then section II's right panel, so that each case stays inside its own section. Names broken by end-of-line hyphens were rejoined (Prod-ucts, Manufac-turing, Per-rault, Amer-ica, Pub-lishing). Doubtful cells: "16-C-1935" (Super-Cold Southwest Co.) — the third digit is smudged and could be a 3, giving 16-C-1335, which would fit better with the neighbouring 16-C series numbers; "91-R-1360" (Avco Manufacturing Corp.) and "91-R-1328" (Burnet-Binford Lumber Co., Inc.) — the leading region number reads clearly as 91 in both, unusually high for 1948 regions, but is transcribed as printed. Printed sequence anomaly, not corrected: in section II, "3-R-1585 Air Metal Construction Co." is printed after "8-R-2694 American Steel & Wire Co.", out of the otherwise alphabetical order. The bottom edge of the crop clips the printed page number (apparently 124), which is excluded per instructions; the list itself continues onto the following page.

p. 133. The crop is a continuation page of an alphabetical list of representation cases: no column headings are printed, so header_rows is empty. Two columns per panel: case number (stub) and company name. The page is set as two side-by-side panels separated by a vertical rule; following the instructions, all 43 rows of the left panel are given first, then the 39 rows of the right panel (82 rows total). In the original printing the right panel's rows sit beside the left panel's, not after them. The list is broken by blank lines into alphabetical groups (C/D, D/E, E/F, F/G, G/H, H/I, I; and I/J, J/K, K/L, L/M, M/N, N/O, O/P, P/Q, Q/R). These blank spacers carry no text and are not transcribed as rows. Doubtful cell: the case number for "Indianapolis Power & Light Co." reads "91-R-1346" in the scan; a leading "91-" region code does not exist in this series and it is most likely a misprint or broken type for "11-R-1346" (or "9-R-1346"). Transcribed exactly as it appears. Possible printing omission: the entry at 2-R-7928 reads "Old Town Ribbon & Co., Inc." — the firm is normally "Old Town Ribbon & Carbon Co., Inc.", but no word "Carbon" is visible on the line. Transcribed as printed. Other characters confirmed as printed: "5W-R-90", "5W-R-2678", "15M-R-81", "15M-R-98", "15M-R-106", "16E-R-17", "17D-R-55", and "14-RE-18" (an RE rather than R case). En-dashes in the case numbers are written as plain hyphens. Trailing periods after company names are part of the printed text and are retained; scattered specks and stray marks in the scan (e.g. after "Coca-Cola Bottling Co.", "Lehrolite, Inc.", "Penn Boiler") are ink artifacts, not punctuation, and are not transcribed. The list continues past the bottom of the crop (the "R" group is cut off after "Rich & Morgan, Inc."), and the beginning of the list (letters A-C) is on an earlier page.

p. 134. The crop is a continuation page of an alphabetical case list, printed as two side-by-side panels of two columns each (case number in the stub, company name beside it). Following the instructions, the two panels are stacked: rows 1-21 are the left panel (Royal Palm ... Superior Manufacturing Co.) and rows 22-39 are the right panel (Tennessee Chair ... Wisconsin Telephone Co.). No column headings, caption, totals or footnotes appear on this crop, so header_rows and footnotes are empty; the table clearly continues from the preceding page and onto the next. Doubtful cells: (1) row "8-R-2674", name column — the line reads "S-P Manufacturing Corp" with the terminal punctuation falling against the vertical panel rule, so it is not legible whether a period and comma ("Corp., The.") are present; transcribed as "S-P Manufacturing Corp., The." by analogy with the other "..., The." entries in the list. (2) Several leading ink specks sit just left of the case numbers "2-R-7789" and "15-R-2234", and just left of the name "Weaver Iron Works."; these are page dirt, not footnote markers or digits, and have been dropped. The printed list leaves a blank line between alphabetical groups (after "Superior Manufacturing Co.", after "Tuttle, H. W., & Co., The." and after "United States Time Corp., The."); these are spacing breaks with no printed label, so no rows were created for them. End-of-line hyphens were repaired in "Divi-sion", "Manu-facturing", "Chemi-cals" and "Associa-tion"; the letterspaced "W e n a t c h e e - W e n o k a" was joined as "Wenatchee-Wenoka", whose hyphen is part of the name. Case-number en dashes are transcribed as plain hyphens. Names are given exactly as printed, including "Scovil", "Tobe-Deutchman" and "Stein-Way", which may be misprints of the firms' actual names.
