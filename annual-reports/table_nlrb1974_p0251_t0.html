Reader’s notes

p. 251. 20 printed columns: stub, "Total number of situations" (a tall heading spanning all three heading levels), two columns under "Total", and eight Number/Percent pairs under "Type of situations" (CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CP, CA-CB combinations, Other C combinations). Decimal points: this is a coarse 1-bit scan and the period in the percent columns frequently fails to print, so very many percent cells appear as a digit, a gap, then a digit ("26 1", "100 0", "0 4"). These are decimal values and I have transcribed them with the decimal point restored ("26.1", "100.0", "0.4"), the same repair the instructions prescribe for a space after a digit-grouping comma. No percent cell was ambiguous as to which digits it contained; only the point itself was faint. The caption's footnote marker 1 and the marker on the Total row's situation count are both small raised figures; the latter is slightly blotted but is the 2 that matches the second printed footnote, so it is given as 24,802^2. Cells that are blank or hold only a dash rule are "" — these appear chiefly in the CE, CP, CA-CB and Other C percent columns on rows whose count is 0, and in the Cumulative percent column of the Total row. In rows 140-149 and 190-199 the empty percent cells in the CP and Other C columns are faint enough that I cannot tell blank from dash rule, but either reading maps to "". Stray ink: a comma-like mark trails "1,287" in the 30-39 row and an apostrophe-like mark trails "96.4" in the 4,000-4,999 row; both are treated as scanning artifacts and not transcribed. Row-label dashes are printed as en dashes (e.g. "10–19", "CA–CB combinations"); transcribed with plain hyphens. Column totals were not checked or adjusted — all values are as printed.
