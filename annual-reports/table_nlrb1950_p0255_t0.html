The spanner "Elections won by" covers the four columns A F of L affiliates / C I O affiliates / Unaffiliated unions / No union; the spanner "Valid votes cast for" covers five columns, Total / A F of L affiliates / C I O affiliates / Unaffiliated unions / No union.
