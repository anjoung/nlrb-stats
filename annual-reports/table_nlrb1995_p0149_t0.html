The crop cuts the table off after the "Compliance with" row: the body continues (the sub-stages of "Compliance with", and the remaining disposition methods) on the next image, and no footnote block is printed here, so `footnotes` is empty even though the caption and the "Before issuance of complaint" row carry markers 1 and 2. 18 columns: stub, then "All C cases" with three columns (Number; Percent of total closed; Percent of total method) and seven two-column blocks (CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), each Number + Percent of total closed. Decimal points dropped by the scan: in a number of percent cells the printed decimal point is too faint to appear, leaving a gap (e.g. "33 3", "31 8", "32 0", "37 0", "42 8", "10 9", "27 2", "0 8", "0 0", "0 2", "0 6", "0 1", "0 5", "0 4", "100 0"). Affected cells: "Agreement of the parties" CG % (33.3) and CP % (31.8); "Informal settlement" All C % of total closed (32.0), CA % (37.0), CE % (42.8); "Before issuance of complaint" CB % (10.9); "After issuance of complaint, before opening of hearing" (informal) All C % of method (27.2) and CD % (0.8); "After hearing opened, before issuance of administrative law judge's decision" CB % (0.0); "Formal settlement" All C % of total closed (0.2), All C % of method (0.6), CA % (0.1), CD % (0.4); "After issuance of complaint, before opening of hearing" (formal) All C % of method (0.5); "Consent decree" (first) CB % (0.1); "Consent decree" (second) All C % of total closed (0.0); "Compliance with" All C % of method (100.0). Transcribed as printed; it looks like a typesetting/compilation artifact rather than a meaningful value. "Before issuance of complaint", CD cases Number: the cell holds a parenthesized raised 2, i.e. a reference to footnote 2 standing in place of a value, not the number 2. Transcribed as "(2)"; the adjacent CD percent cell is a dash rule and is "". Dash rules ("———") used in place of percentages are transcribed as "". Printed zeros are kept as 0. The row labels "After issuance of complaint, before opening of hearing", "Stipulated decision" and "Consent decree" each appear twice (once under "Informal settlement"/"Formal settlement — after issuance of complaint" and once under "Formal settlement — after hearing opened"); the duplication is in the original, distinguished only by indentation. Internal arithmetic checks out throughout (e.g. 7,262 + 2,779 + 123 = 10,164; 10,164 + 60 = 10,224), which supports the column assignments.
