Dash cells: the table prints a short dash ("--") in many cells, distinct from a printed "0", which also occurs. Following the convention that a dash is an empty cell, every "--" is transcribed as "" and every printed "0" is kept as "0"; the distinction between the two is therefore not preserved in the grid.
