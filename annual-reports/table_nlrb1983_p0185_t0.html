No footnote texts are printed on this crop; the image ends at the table's closing rule immediately below "Other remedies". Reference marks exist for them (a raised 1 on the caption, excluded per instructions, and a raised 2 on the "By number of cases involved" value), so footnotes 1 and 2 are presumably printed below the cut or on a continuation. Section label "A By number of cases involved": the leading "A" is followed by a wide space; I could not see a period after it in the scan, so none was supplied. Its value is printed as a raised 2 followed by 11,754, transcribed as 11,754^2. The table is cut off after section A — section B (by number of employees/workers involved), which this lettering implies, is not on this crop. Blank cells are as printed: rows "Recognition or other assistance withdrawn" through "Employees placed on preferential hiring list" have no Union entries, and "Hiring hall rights restored" through "Work stoppage ended" have no Employer entries. By contrast "Other conditions of employment improved" and "Other remedies" print explicit 0s across the Employer columns. A small stray tick mark appears in the heading area above "Agreement of parties" on the Union side; it sits between heading levels and reads as a scan speck, not a footnote marker, so it was not transcribed. Every row's components sum to its printed Employer, Union and Total all figures, so no arithmetic anomalies were found. The scan renders the dot on many "i" characters faintly (e.g. "ınvolved", "Pıcketing"); these were read as normal "i" letters.

p. 186. The crop continues a table from the preceding page: no column headings are printed on this image, so header_rows is empty. Column count: 14 (stub + 13 data columns), read off the 13 vertical rules. The data columns are a grand-total column, then a six-column employer block (block total + five subtypes), then a six-column union block (block total + five subtypes). This is confirmed by the internal arithmetic of every valued row, e.g. "From either employer or union" (backpay): 15,851+598+74+896+469 = 17,888 (employer total), 280+23+0+37+1 = 341 (union total), 17,888+341 = 18,229 (grand total). The same checks hold for the reinstatement, preferential-hiring, hiring-hall, objections-withdrawn, reimbursement and all three monetary rows, so no printed total appears to be in error. Rows where the employer block is blank (hiring hall rights restored, objections to employment withdrawn) carry their figures only in the grand-total column and the union block; the blanks between are left as "". In the "C" row and the two rows under it, the eleventh data column holds a printed zero with no dollar sign; transcribed as "0" as printed. The section letters "B" and "C" are set hanging to the left of their labels; the period after each letter is faint in this scan, so it is possible the original prints the bare letter without a period. A short stray dash appears in the stub just after the word "union" in the first "From either employer or union" line under "Employees reimbursed for fees, dues, and fines"; it is a printing/scan artifact and is not transcribed. Terminal periods in both footnotes, and the sentence-break period after "definition of terms" in footnote 1, are lost or very faint in the scan; they are supplied as the sense requires.
