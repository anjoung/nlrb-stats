The same loss affects the footnote (\"Sec 8(a)(3)\" on the page) and the final sentence period, both restored.\n\nDoubtful cells, row \"Teamsters\", columns \"Valid votes cast / Cast for deauthorization / Number\" and \".../ Percent of total eligible\": I read 340 and 41.4, but these two are mutually inconsistent and only one can be right. 340 makes the Number column add to the printed total (2,304 + 340 + 39 + 205 = 2,888), and every other column of this table adds exactly to its total; but 340/734 = 46.3, not 41.4.
