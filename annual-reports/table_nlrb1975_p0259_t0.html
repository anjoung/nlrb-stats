p. 259. 15 columns: stub, 4 summary columns, four Number/Percent pairs under "Elections in which representation rights were won by" (AFL-CIO, Teamsters, Other national, Other local), and a Number/Percent pair under "Elections in which no representative was chosen". Decimal points: this is a 1-bit scan in which many decimal points have dropped out, so most percent cells print as e.g. "46 4", "100 0", "0 6". Every one of these columns carries exactly one decimal place, so I have restored the decimal point ("46.4", "100.0", "0.6"). No digits were changed. Cells holding only a dash rule ("------") are transcribed as "" (they occur in the Cumulative-percent cell of the total row and in the percent columns wherever the paired count is 0). Arithmetic checks: the five count columns (Number eligible to vote, Total elections, AFL-CIO Number, Teamsters Number, Other national Number, Other local Number, No-representative Number) each sum exactly to the printed total row (545,103; 8,061; 2,344; 1,148; 246; 263; 4,060), which supports the digit readings. Suspected source anomaly, transcribed as printed: the "Under 10" row shows 13,108 eligible voters in only 2 elections, which is impossible for units of under 10 employees. It is nevertheless what is printed, and it is required for the column to sum to the printed total 545,103 (the other 28 rows sum to 531,995), so the error appears to be in the source rather than in the reading. Minor rounding oddities in the source, transcribed as printed: in the "Other national unions - Percent by size class" column, 102/246 = 41.5 but 41.7 is printed, 26/246 = 10.6 but 10.7 is printed, and 22/246 = 8.9 but 9.0 is printed; in the "Other local unions - Percent by size class" column, 91/263 = 34.6 but 34.7 is printed and 34/263 = 12.9 but 13.0 is printed. All other percentages in those columns round correctly, so 41.7 / 10.7 / 34.7 are the cells I would re-check at higher resolution (5 vs 7 and 6 vs 7 are the plausible confusions). The section line reads "A Certification elections (RC and RM)"; the period after "A" is not legible in the scan (consistent with the general loss of periods) and I have written it as "A.".
