"July 5, 1935–Sept. 30, 1994" is a tall heading spanning the first two header levels and is repeated there; "Total" sits only in the third level under "Number of proceedings". Em-dash rules (long dashes) fill the percentage cells and the two right-hand columns where no value is printed; these are rendered as "" per the dash-rule convention. They are not blanks in the original — e.g. the entire Percentages block and both 1935–1994 columns are dashed on the "Proceedings decided by U.S. courts of appeals" row. Row label as printed reads "Proceedings decided by U.S Supreme Court" — no period after the second "S", unlike "U.S. courts of appeals" above it. Transcribed as printed; likely a typesetting slip.
