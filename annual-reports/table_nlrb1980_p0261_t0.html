No footnote texts appear on this crop (the image ends at the double rule below "Other remedies"), so `footnotes` is empty; the footnote 2 text and any continuation of the table (a section B, if any) fall outside the crop. All printed rows are internally consistent (Employer and Union component columns sum to their panel totals, and the two totals sum to "Total all"), so no misprints are suspected.
