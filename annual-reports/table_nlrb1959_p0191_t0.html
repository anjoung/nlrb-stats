Reader’s notes

p. 191. Ten columns: the stub (Case No.), Union and company, Date petition filed, Type of petition, a two-column "Temporary restraining order" spanner (Date issued / Date lifted), Date temporary injunction granted, Date injunction denied, Date injunction proceedings dismissed or dissolved, and Date Board decision and/or order. "Type of petition" column: the glyphs are lowercase letters in parentheses, not digits — "(l)" for Sec. 10(l) petitions and "(j)" in the single CA case (2-CA-5169), which is consistent with the statute. At this scan resolution "(l)" and "(1)" are nearly identical, so the reading rests on that case-type logic as much as on the shapes. Standalone raised references: in "Date temporary injunction granted" the cells for 6-CC-145, 22-CC-4, 19-CD-29, 9-CC-124/125, 33-CC-24 and 17-CC-60 hold a parenthesised superscript 1, and "Date injunction denied" for 17-CD-25 holds a parenthesised superscript 2. These are footnote references standing alone in the cell (not attached to any value), transcribed literally as "(1)" and "(2)". Asterisks: eight union names are printed with a leading "*" (the Teamsters entries). Treating it as a footnote marker, it has been moved to the end of the label as "^*" per the marker convention; it is printed before the name in the original. Footnotes are not visible — the crop ends at the bottom of the last transcribed row ("39-CC-44 ... Retail Clerks (Montgomery Ward & Co).") and any footnote block, including the texts for markers 1, 2 and *, falls below the crop. The table itself most likely continues on the following page.
