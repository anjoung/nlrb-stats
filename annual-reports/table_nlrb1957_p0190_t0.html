p. 190. Seven printed columns: stub, then "Number of elections" spanning Total / AFL-CIO affiliates / Unaffiliated unions / In which no representative was chosen, then Eligible voters and Valid votes cast. The "Number of elections" group rule runs across all four of those columns, with the heavier vertical rule falling before "Eligible voters". Cell notes: - "Tobacco manufacturers", Unaffiliated unions: printed as a dash rule (------), transcribed as "". - "Other transportation", Valid votes cast: the digit group separator looks like a period rather than a comma in the scan ("1.791"); transcribed as "1,791" on the assumption of a broken/ink-filled comma. - "Crude petroleum and natural gas production", Valid votes cast: a stray apostrophe-like speck sits above and left of "590"; read as a scan artifact, not a footnote marker. - "Mining", Valid votes cast: a similar stray speck precedes "9,518"; treated as an artifact. - "Construction", AFL-CIO affiliates: the leading 3 is partly broken by the scan; read as "33". Structure: "Manufacturing", "Mining", and "Transportation, communication, and other public utilities" are group totals printed above their indented component rows, each set off by rules; "Fisheries", "Construction", "Wholesale trade", "Retail trade", "Finance, insurance, and real estate", and "Services" are standalone group lines with no components. No arithmetic was checked or corrected.
