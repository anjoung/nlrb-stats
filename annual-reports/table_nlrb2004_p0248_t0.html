The subtotal row is printed as "31-Manufacturing" (with the leading sector-code digits), unlike the other subtotal rows, which are unnumbered; it is transcribed exactly as printed. Section subtotal rows (Agriculture..., Mining, Construction, 31-Manufacturing) are set off by heavier rules and are indented, but are ordinary data rows with values. All internal arithmetic checks I could perform (union + no-union votes = total valid votes; AFL-CIO + other national + other local = union total; component rows summing to each subtotal) are consistent with the figures as transcribed, except the Leather row noted above.
