No printed value appears to be a misprint.

The table continues past the bottom of the crop: "Miscellaneous repair services" is the last fully printed line visible, and the remaining service-industry rows plus any totals and the footnote text (the marker "1" on "Industrial group" has no printed footnote on this crop) fall below the crop. Doubtful cells: in "U S Postal Service" the periods of the abbreviation are not resolvable in the scan (it is almost certainly "U.S. Postal Service" in the original print); transcribed as the characters that are legible. In "Local and suburban transit and interurban highway passenger transportation", the "Valid votes cast for unions / Total" cell is printed with a broken first "1" and reads as 1,197; the row's internal arithmetic (529+511+17+140 = 1,197 and 1,197+846 = 2,043) confirms it. Every data row's internal arithmetic checks out (total elections = won + no representative; union total = sum of four union types; valid votes for unions + votes for no union = total valid votes), so no misprints are suspected on this page.
