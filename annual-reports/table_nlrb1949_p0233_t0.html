p. 233. This is not a numeric statistical table but an appendix list of cases, set in two side-by-side panels separated by a vertical rule. Each panel has two columns: the case number (stub, cell 0) and the company name (cell 1). Following the instructions for multi-panel tables, all 25 rows of the left panel (Abercrombie through Burns Brick) are given first, then the 29 rows of the right panel (Califruit through Gluck Bros.). No column headings are printed anywhere on the page, so header_rows is empty. The three centred lines at the top — "I. Unfair Labor Practice Cases", "A. Cases decided after contest", "1. NLRA—C cases" — are section headings of the appendix listing rather than a "Table N.—" caption, so they are transcribed as label-only section rows at the head of the body. If the project treats them as caption matter they can be dropped. Doubtful / notable cells: - "2-C-5963 American Book—Stratford Press, Inc.": the printed mark between "Book" and "Strat-" looks like a hyphen immediately followed by a longer dash (an em dash or a double hyphen); transcribed as a single em dash. - "13-C-2943 Dearborn Glass Co.": a small raised apostrophe-like speck sits just right of the case number. It is almost certainly a printing speck, not a footnote marker, so it is omitted. - "21-C-2505 Columbia Pictures.": a small ink mark sits inside the number between "21" and "-C-"; read as a plain 21-C-2505. - Region prefixes "15M-C-1217", "15M-C-1220" and "17D-C-1477" carry trailing letters; these are legible and transcribed as seen, but they are unusual compared with the plain numeric prefixes elsewhere. - "15-C-1315 Dorsey Trailers, Inc" is printed without a closing period, unlike neighbouring entries; transcribed as printed. - "17-C-1415 Des Moines Springfield & Southern Route, The." is transcribed exactly as printed; the wording reads oddly for a railway name but no misprint can be confirmed from the image. - "10-C-1893 Atlantic Stages, J. A. Booker d/b/a.": the "d/b/a" is set with small slashes and a lowered "b"; read as d/b/a. - Blank lines separating the alphabetical letter groups (after Auto Stove Works, after Duro Test Corp., etc.) are spacing only and are not transcribed as rows. - The page number at the foot is clipped by the crop and is excluded. The list continues on the following page. - Case-number dashes are printed as en dashes; they are written here as plain hyphens.
