Scan artifacts, all read as faded periods rather than true omissions: in the "Percent won" column several decimal points failed to print ("51 4", "44 8", "55 2", "46 3", and the four "100 0" cells); they are transcribed as 51.4, 44.8, 55.2, 46.3 and 100.0. Each is confirmed by arithmetic (e.g. 57/111 = 51.4, 1,786/3,988 = 44.8, 16/29 = 55.2, 1,921/4,153 = 46.3). Local"; all are transcribed with "v." for consistency with the clearly printed labels. "3 (or more)-union elections." is printed with a trailing period before its leader dots; the period is dropped here. In the "3 (or more)-union elections" row, the Teamsters cell under "In units won by" is printed as "0" followed by a speck that resembles a period ("0."); it is transcribed as 0, which is consistent with the column (499 Teamsters wins total = 479 + 20 + 0) and with the employee total 16,968 = 16,119 + 849 + 0. Em-dash rules (no entry) are rendered as empty cells throughout; printed zeros are kept as 0. Every subtotal and the grand total were checked against their components and all agree, in both the election counts and the employee counts, so no misprints are suspected. The crop covers only panel "A. All representation elections"; the section letter implies at least one further section (normally "B. Elections in units of 500 or more employees" or similar) that continues on a following page and is not shown here. No footnote texts appear beneath the table on this crop, although footnote markers are present (superscript 1 on the caption, superscript 2 on "Total elections"); the footnote block presumably sits below the continuation of the table.
