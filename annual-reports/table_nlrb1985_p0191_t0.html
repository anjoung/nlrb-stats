The caption carries a superscript 1 ("Fiscal Year 1985^1"), but no footnote text is printed within this crop; the footnote presumably appears under the last panel of the table on a later page. All representation elections" is very faint in the scan and could be absent; I transcribed it with the period, consistent with the usual styling of this series. Dash rules ("——") have been rendered as empty cells; printed zeros are kept as 0. Note in particular the "National v Local" row, where the elections-lost "Other local unions" cell is a printed 0, and the "AFL-CIO v Teamsters v Local" and "AFL-CIO v Local v Local" rows, which mix printed zeros with dash rules. All arithmetic cross-checks pass: the four one-union rows sum to the "1-union elections" line, the nine two-union rows to "2-union elections", the three rows to "3 (or more)-union elections", and the three subtotals to "Total representation elections" in every numeric column; within each row the union components sum to the won/lost totals, and won + lost + the two no-union columns equal the total valid votes. No misprints were found. The scan renders many instances of "i" in "unions"/"union" without a dot; these have been transcribed as the intended words.
