The crop ends at the closing rule after the 8(b)(1)(2)(3)(5) row: the table is cut off here and continues on the following page (the Sec. 8(b) recapitulation block is not on this image). The raised "1" on "Recapitulation" is a footnote marker, but no footnote text is printed within this crop, so `footnotes` is empty; the footnote presumably appears on the continuation page. Doubtful/low-resolution cells: in the source scan the parenthesis pairs in some stub labels print with the closing and opening parens merged (e.g. "8(b)(3X5)", "8(b)(1X2)"); these were read as "8(b)(3)(5)", "8(b)(1)(2)", etc. In the 8(a) block the value 2,262 for row 8(a)(1)(3)(5) has a faint comma but the digits are legible. This 1996 volume is a lossy bilevel scan, so single digits (notably the 1/7 and 2/0 shapes in the small "Percent of total cases" column) carry some risk of misreading, though every value here was legible. No arithmetic was checked or corrected; all figures are as printed.

p. 117. Three-column table (stub plus two value columns); the stub column has no printed heading. The page is a continuation ("—Continued"), so it begins mid-table with the section line "Recapitulation1"; the three "Recapitulation" section lines and the lettered section lines (B1, B2, C, D) carry labels only, no values. The section heading printed as "B2. Analysis of 8(b)(7)" shows what may be a comma rather than a period after "B2" in the scan; transcribed as a period to match the other section lines. Internal arithmetic checks out throughout (B1 detail sums to 706, B2 detail sums to 85, and both recapitulation blocks and all percentages are consistent with the printed figures), which supports the digit readings on this lossy 1996 scan.
