Regions I-IV and the caption/headings are on the preceding page. The grid has 16 columns (stub + 15 data columns), read off the vertical column rules. Arithmetic check: I summed every one of the 15 data columns within each of the six regional blocks against its printed "Region" subtotal. All 90 checks agree except one. In the last (15th) column, Region IX is printed as 53 while its member states as I read them (Arizona 3, California 47, Hawaii 8, Guam 0, Nevada 0) sum to 58. The discrepancy is exactly 5, which is consistent with either California's 47 actually being 42 or Hawaii's 8 actually being 3 in the printed source; I could not resolve the glyphs with certainty at this scan resolution and have transcribed them as they appear (47 and 8). Treat the last-column values for California and Hawaii as the doubtful cells. Region IX agrees with its member states in all 14 other columns, including the 14th column, where Hawaii's 11 is confirmed by the printed subtotal 18. Ohio, 13th data column: the value 19 is followed by a small stray ink mark that is not a digit or a footnote marker; the column sum (23+6+10+8+19+2 = 68) confirms the reading 19. A similar stray mark sits at the right end of footnote 2; it is not part of the text. Several state labels show broken dotted i's from the scan (Illinois, Louisiana, Missouri, Wyoming, Arizona, California, Administrative); the spellings are standard and have been written normally.
