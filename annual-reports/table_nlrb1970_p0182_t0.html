They are transcribed as 100.0 ("Total, all", RM Percent), 0.1 ("After hearing closed, before decision" under Stipulated election, RD Percent) and 1.5 ("After regional director's decision and direction of election", All R Percent). Each is confirmed by the column's own printed subtotal: RM percents 47.1+34.3+18.6=100.0; RD stipulated 8.2+8.5+0.1=16.8; All R withdrawal 13.4+8.3+0.5+1.5+0.2=23.9. In every other Percent cell the period is printed but followed by a typesetting space ("100. 0"), normalized here the same way as the digit-grouping commas.
