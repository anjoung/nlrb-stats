Reader’s notes

p. 201. The crop cuts the table off after the Maryland row; the South Atlantic division and the remaining divisions continue on the following page, and no footnote texts are visible on this crop even though markers ^1 and ^2 are used in the headings (so `footnotes` is empty). Doubtful cells: (1) The "All cases" column heading carries a small raised mark printed on its own line below "cases". It is inked heavily and I cannot read it cleanly; I have transcribed it as the footnote marker ^2 by analogy with the other headings, but it may be a 2, another marker, or a scan artifact. (2) The second spanner heading reads to me as "Representative cases"; NLRB tables of this series usually print "Representation cases", so the word ending (-ive vs -ion) is the one heading I am least sure of. Arithmetic check (no values were altered): every row is internally consistent as printed — CA+CB+CC+CD+CE+CP equals All C cases, RC+RM+RD equals All R cases, and All C cases + All R cases equals All cases. Each division line also equals the sum of its listed states (New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central all verified; South Atlantic cannot be checked because its states are cut off). This confirms the column assignment of every number; no misprints were found. The South Atlantic RC value prints with a faint comma that could be mistaken for a period; it is 1,011.

p. 202. Continuation page: no column headings are printed on this crop, so header_rows is empty. The table is cut off at the top — the South Atlantic group subtotal and its first States (and earlier regions) are on the preceding page; the rows from "District of Columbia" through "Florida" are the tail of that group. Grid is 13 columns (stub + 12 numeric columns), read from the printed column rules. Minor print artifacts, all read as not part of the data: a stray ink speck sits just left of "164" in the Pacific row (column 11); a short dash-like mark sits just left of "4" in the Washington row (column 6), apparently a leader/rule fragment; two leader dashes run into the left edge of the "38" cell in the Nevada row (column 1). The second footnote appears to end without a period after "cases"; transcribed as printed. Arithmetic cross-checks all hold as transcribed: in every row columns 3-8 sum to column 2 and columns 10-12 sum to column 9; column 2 + column 9 = column 1; and the East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, Pacific and Outlying areas subtotal rows equal the sum of their member States in all 12 columns. No printed value appears misprinted.
