Dash cells: many cells hold a short double-hyphen dash mark ("--") rather than a number. Per the rule that a dash is an empty cell, these are transcribed as "". The distinction with the printed zeros matters here, because both appear in this table: every "0" in the grid is a literal printed zero (notably the three "3 (or more)-union" detail rows, the "3 (or more)-union elections" subtotal, and the three zeros in the "3 (or more)-union elections" row under "Other national unions", and the elections-lost columns), while every "" in a data column was a printed "--". The crop is cut off at the bottom: panel B ("B. Elections in RC cases") ends with the "1-union elections" subtotal and the rest of panel B (2-union, 3-or-more-union and total rows) and any further panels continue beyond this image. The caption carries a superscript 1 footnote marker, but no footnote text is printed within the crop, so `footnotes` is empty. All internal arithmetic was spot-checked and is consistent as printed: the 1-union, 2-union, 3-or-more-union and total rows all equal the sum of their component rows in every column, in both panels, so no misprint is suspected. No cell was illegible.
