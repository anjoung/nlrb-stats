p. 155. This crop is an alphabetical case list (appendix-style), set as two side-by-side panels separated by a vertical rule; each panel has two columns, case number and company name. Following the panel rule, the left panel's six entries are given first (Alliance Rubber through Atlanta Metallic Casket) and then the right panel's six (Atlantic Towing through Bettcher Manufacturing). Cell 0 (the stub) is the case docket number as printed in the leftmost column of each panel; cell 1 is the company name. No column headings are printed, so header_rows is empty. The two centered lines "I. Unfair Labor Practice Cases" and "A. Cases decided after contest" are section lines spanning the full page width above both panels and are transcribed as label-only rows. Case numbers are printed with en dashes (8–C–2006); they are transcribed with plain hyphens. Wrapped labels were joined and end-of-line hyphens repaired: "Amer-ica" to "America", "Ma-chinery" to "Machinery", "Prod-ucts" to "Products". Doubtful cell: the name at 10-C-1928 reads "Babcock-Wilson Co., The." in the scan; the firm is more likely "Babcock-Wilcox", but it is transcribed as it appears. Stray scan marks, not footnote markers, appear in the image: a short dash below the stub "9-C-2349" in the left panel, a short dash immediately left of "13-C-2761" in the right panel, a mark above "8-C-1818", and a tick before "Manufacturing" in the Bettcher entry; none were transcribed. The list continues beyond this crop (the next alphabetical entries and the running page number 147, partly visible at the bottom, are cut off).
