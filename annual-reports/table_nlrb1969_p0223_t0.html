Reader’s notes

p. 223. 15 columns: stub plus seven case-type panels (All C, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CP), each with a \"Number of cases\" and a \"Percent of cases closed\" column.\n\nFour cells in the narrow CC and CE number columns are printed with heavy ink spread and were hard to resolve at this scan resolution; in each case the reading given is the one confirmed by the table's own arithmetic, which then reconciles exactly in both directions. (a) CC, \"After issuance of complaint, before opening of hearing\": read as 107 (the digits could be taken for 170); 107 is required by the All C figure 1,251 and matches the printed 7.1 percent. (b) CC, \"After Supreme Court action\": read as 3 (could be taken for 8); 3 is required by the All C figure 69 and matches the printed 0.2 percent. (c) CE, \"Total number of cases closed\": read as 98, the leading 9 being faint; 98 is required by the All C total 18,939 and by the CE column's own sum. (d) CE, \"After Board decision, before circuit court decree\": read as 37 (the leading digit could be taken for 8); 37 is required by the All C figure 606 and matches the printed 37.7 percent.\n\nWith those readings every row and every column of the number columns adds exactly to its printed total, and every percent column sums to 100.0, so no printed value appears to be in error.\n\nCells printed as a short dash rule (---) in the percent columns, indicating no cases, are transcribed as empty: CE percent in rows 4, 5, 6 and 9; CD percent in rows 5, 6 and 9; CP percent in row 9.\n\nThe All C percent for \"After Board decision, before circuit court decree\" is printed 3.1 where 606/18,939 would give 3.2; transcribed as printed.\n\nThe caption carries footnote marker 1, but the caption itself is excluded per instructions.
