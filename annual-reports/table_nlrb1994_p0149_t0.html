In the "Ship and boat building and repairing" row a small ink speck sits just left of 311 in the last column ("Eligible employees in units choosing no representation"); it is not a footnote marker or digit, and the value is 311. All fifteen columns were cross-checked arithmetically: every column of the 21 industry rows sums exactly to the printed "Manufacturing" line, and the within-row identities (won + no representative = total elections; union votes + no-union votes = total valid votes cast; AFL-CIO + other national + other local = union totals) hold throughout, so no misprints are suspected.
