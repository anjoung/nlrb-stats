Reader’s notes

p. 176. 11 columns: stub, "All cases", five unfair-labor-practice columns, four representation columns. The stub heading "Industrial group^1" and the tall heading "All cases" span both heading levels and are repeated in each. Typesetting/scan issues, all resolved in favor of the arithmetically consistent reading: several thousands commas print as periods or as blank gaps ("2.015" for Retail trade All cases, "2.860" and "1.890" for the Transportation row, "1 620" and "1 125" for Motor freight); these are transcribed with commas. Loose ink speckles sit just left of a few figures and are not digits or markers — Mining/RC (74), Motor freight/RC (425), Local passenger transportation/RC (127), Services/RD (16); Services/RD is the least clean but reads 16. Every row and panel total cross-checks: each row's All cases = All C + All R, All C = CA+CB+CC+CD, All R = RC+RM+RD; the Mining, Transportation and Manufacturing subgroup columns sum to their group lines; and the nine major group lines sum to the printed Total of 21,586 in all ten numeric columns. No misprints found. Footnote 1 punctuation is partly broken in the scan (the colon after "Source" and the periods in "U.S." are faint or dropped); rendered here with conventional punctuation. Footnote 2's "table 1" is printed in a face where the digit 1 resembles a capital I. Footnote 2 ends without a period, as printed.
