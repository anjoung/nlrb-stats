p. 53. Seven columns: stub plus Number of cases, two "Percentage of—" columns (Total cases closed; Total cases on docket), Number of workers involved, and two further "Percentage of—" columns (Total number of workers involved in cases closed; Total number of workers involved in cases on docket). "Number of cases" and "Number of workers involved" are tall single headings spanning both heading levels, so each is repeated in both; the stub carries no printed heading, so its heading cells are empty. The spanner "Percentage of—" appears twice, over columns 3-4 and over columns 6-7. In the "Total cases closed" and "Total number of workers involved in cases closed" columns, the rows for cases pending/received, total cases on docket, and all the pending rows hold only leader dots; these are transcribed as "". The printed decimals carry a typographic space after the point (e.g. "11. 2", "20. 7"); these are written without the space. Decimals under 1 are printed without a leading zero (".8", ".5") and are kept that way. No row-label, heading or value carries a footnote marker, and no footnotes are printed under the table. Legibility: "2,331" in the "Total cases on docket" row and "1,015,216" in the same row are struck by a horizontal rule artifact in the scan but are legible and confirmed by arithmetic (262 + 2,069 and 210,318 + 804,898). The "Total cases closed" value for "By dismissal before issuance of Board decisions" reads as ".6" (it could be mistaken for ".0") and is confirmed by the column sum.
