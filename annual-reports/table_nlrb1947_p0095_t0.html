Reader’s notes

p. 95. 16 columns: stub, "1936-47^1", "Percent of cases closed", the 12 fiscal years 1936-1947, and "July 1-Aug. 21, 1947". Only one heading level is printed. Doubtful cell: row "Prehearing election" (the one under "Adjusted" in the "Before formal action" block), column 1946. The glyph is ambiguous between 66 and 68 at this scan resolution; transcribed as 68 because the row's 1936-47 total (445 = 68+341+36) and the 1946 "Adjusted" figure (3,877 = 238+3,571+68) both require 68, whereas 66 fails both. Every other cell in the table reconciles exactly with its row and column totals, so no other value is in doubt. Dash rules (long broken rules spanning the early-year columns, marking "not applicable") appear in the "Prehearing election", "Stipulated election and cross check", "Prehearing election and stipulation", and "Prehearing election and Board decision" rows; they are transcribed as empty cells. "Stipulated election and cross check" (both the Certified and the Dismissed instance) has its rule over 1936-1940 and data from 1941 on; the four prehearing rows have their rule over 1936-1945 and data only from 1946 on. The "Percent of cases closed" cells reading "(2)" are a parenthesized raised footnote reference (footnote 2, "Less than 0.1 percent."), printed in place of a percentage; they are given as "(2)". The footnote marker printed beside the second footnote line reproduces as a smudged raised character but is the superscript 2 referenced by those cells. Row labels are indented to show hierarchy; indentation is not preserved in the stub. Several labels repeat at different levels ("Adjusted", "Recognition", "Consent election and cross check", "Prehearing election", "Withdrawn", "Dismissed", "Closed otherwise", "Stipulated election and cross check", "Ordered election", "Prehearing election and Board decision", "Without election"), so the rows must be read in printed order. "Prehearing election and Board decision" under "Certified" is printed with a wide word space; normalized to a single space.
