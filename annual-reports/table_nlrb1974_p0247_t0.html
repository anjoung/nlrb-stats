Reader’s notes

p. 247. The crop is the first page of the table and is cut off at the bottom: it ends with a double rule after the "Measuring, analyzing, and controlling instruments..." row, so the remaining industry rows and the grand total continue on the following page. The raised "1" on the stub heading "Industrial group" refers to a footnote that is not printed within this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Column structure: 17 printed columns. Two five-column spanners ("Number of elections in which representation rights were won by unions" and "Valid votes cast for unions") each break into Total / AFL-CIO / Teamsters / Other national unions / Other local unions; the three tall single headings between them and the two at the right are repeated in both header levels. Heading wording in doubt (scan resolution, not content): the last column reads as "Eligible employee in units choosing representation" (singular "employee"); it may be "employees". Likewise the AFL-CIO sub-heading appears singular ("AFL-CIO union") under the elections spanner but plural ("AFL-CIO unions") under the votes spanner; both are transcribed as they appear. Arithmetic cross-checks pass for all 21 rows (the four union components sum to the spanner Total in both panels, Total won + no representative = Total elections, and union votes + no-union votes = Total valid votes cast), which corroborates the column placement of every figure. No misprints detected. Row-level remark: "Tobacco manufactures" shows only 6 elections but 9,653 employees eligible and 8,732 valid votes cast, with just 19 eligible employees in units choosing representation. The figures are unusual in scale relative to the election count but are internally consistent (2,956 + 5,776 = 8,732) and are transcribed exactly as printed. The scan carries scattered specks and stray tick marks (e.g. before "509" ... no, before the "6" of Tobacco, before "232" of Primary metal industries, and near several votes figures such as "1,637", "402", "4,446", "1,956", "635", "462", "2,155"). These are page noise, not digits or footnote markers, and were excluded; the surrounding digits were confirmed by the row sums.

p. 248. Continuation page: no column headings are printed on this crop, so header_rows is empty. The grid has 17 columns (stub + 16 numeric), counted from the column rules; the Manufacturing section is cut off at the top, where only its last detail row ("Miscellaneous manufacturing industries") and the "Manufacturing" group-total row survive on this page. Column assignment was cross-checked arithmetically and every column balances: the four mining detail rows sum exactly to the "Mining" row in all 16 columns, the six transportation detail rows sum to "Transportation, communication, and other utilities" in all 16, the nine services detail rows sum to "Services" in all 16, and the nine group rows on this page (Manufacturing, Mining, Construction, Wholesale trade, Retail trade, Finance, U S Postal Service, Transportation, Services) sum exactly to "Total, all industrial groups" in all 16 columns. No printed figure appears to be a misprint. Doubtful typography (values unaffected): the stub label read as "U S Postal Service" is almost certainly "U.S. Postal Service" in the original — the periods are not resolvable in this scan, so it is transcribed as the characters are legible. Likewise the footnote's "Source" is probably followed by a colon that the scan has lost. The label "Pipes lines, except natural gas" is transcribed as printed (the standard SIC wording is "Pipe lines"), i.e. a probable misprint in the source, not a reading error. A small ink speck follows "156" in the first column of the "Miscellaneous manufacturing industries" row and another follows "24" in column 14 of that same row; both are dirt, not footnote markers. The footnote marker "1" belongs to a column heading or caption printed on the preceding page, which is outside this crop.
