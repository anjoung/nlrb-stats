Reader’s notes

p. 119. Seven printed columns: the stub, "Cases in which formal actions taken", and five columns under the spanner "Formal actions taken by type of case" (Total formal actions taken^2, RC, RM, RD, UD). A double vertical rule separates RD from UD, consistent with footnote 2 (UD counts are excluded from the "Total formal actions taken" column); in every data row the "Total" column equals RC + RM + RD exactly, which supports the column assignments. "Review of Regional Directors' decisions:", "Regional Directors' decisions:" and "Outcome:" are label-only section lines printed with no values. "Transferred by Regional Directors for initial decision" and "Withdrawn after request granted, before Board review" each wrap onto two printed lines and are joined here. The scan is slightly speckled (stray marks above "Review of Regional Directors' decisions:" and beside some rows) but no digit was ambiguous. The caption "Table 3B.—Formal Actions Taken in Representation and Union Deauthorization Cases, Fiscal Year 1996^1" carries footnote marker 1 and is omitted per instructions.

p. 120. Seven columns: stub, "Cases in which formal actions taken", then five columns under the spanner "Formal actions taken by type of case" (Total formal actions taken^2, RC, RM, RD, UD); a double vertical rule separates RD from UD but UD still sits under the spanner. In the row "Board decision after review, total" the punctuation after "review" prints like a period in this scan; I read it as a comma, matching the other "..., total" rows. Flagging it since it is a scan-quality judgement, not a certain reading. Footnote marker 1 appears only in the caption ("Fiscal Year 1996^1"), which is excluded per instructions, so no row or heading in the grid carries it; marker 2 is on the "Total formal actions taken" heading. All arithmetic checks pass as printed: in every data row RC + RM + RD equals the "Total formal actions taken" cell (UD excluded per footnote 2), and Granted + Denied + Remanded (5 + 24 + 2) equals the 31 in "Board action on request ruled upon, total". No suspected misprints. Minor scan artifacts that are not data: a stray mark at the upper right of the table rule, and a stray dot left of "321" in the "Cases in which formal actions taken" column of the "In stipulated elections" row.
