p. 183. Four columns: stub "Name of case", then "Date opened" and "Date closed" under the spanning head "Date hearing held", then "Date decision issued". The crop is a continuation page of an alphabetical case list; it ends mid-alphabet (last row "East Gulf Stevedoring Co., Inc."), so the table continues on the following page. No numbered footnotes are printed on this page. The only line below the rule is the reference "See footnotes at end of table, p. 179.", which is not itself a footnote, so `footnotes` is empty. In the "Date decision issued" column, four cells contain only a raised numeral inside parentheses, i.e. a footnote reference and not a date: Acme Air Appliance Co. "(1)", Bethlehem Steel Corporation and Bethlehem Steel Co. "(2)", Cudahy Packing Co. "(2)", Eagle Pencil Co. "(3)". I kept the printed parentheses rather than caret notation because the marker is the whole cell, not an attachment to a value; the digit in the Bethlehem Steel and Cudahy cells is blurred and is read as 2 partly from context. Ditto marks: cells printed as leader dots around "do" are given as "do"; the stub and the decision column print the capitalised form "Do.", which is kept as printed. The row "Byco Distributors, Inc." prints lower-case "do" (not "Do.") in the "Date decision issued" column, as transcribed. Doubtful cells, all due to 1-bit scan degradation of the digits 3/8/9: - "Do." (4th Armour & Co. line), Date closed: reads "June 30, 1939"; a 1939 closing date before an Aug. 1938 decision is implausible, but 1939 is what the glyph shows. - "Do." (4th Armour & Co. line), Date decision issued: the final digit of the year is broken and could be read as 3; transcribed "Aug. 29, 1938" since 1933 predates the Board. - "B. F. Sturtevant Co.", Date decision issued: same ambiguity; transcribed "Aug. 6, 1938". - "Coastal Freight Handlers, Inc.", Date opened: reads "June 30, 1939"; the parallel maritime cases on either side open "June 20, 1938", so this is likely a misprint or a damaged glyph, but it is transcribed as it appears. - "Admiar Rubber Co.", "Alfred LeBlanc, Inc." and "Atlantic Gulf Stevedoring, Inc.", Date decision issued: terminal year digits are smudged; read as 1938 in each case. In "Atlantic Gulf Stevedoring, Inc." the comma of "Sept. 29, 1938" prints as a period. Row-label spellings are kept as printed, including apparent typesetting oddities ("Admiar Rubber Co.", "B. G. Hoodley Quarries, Inc.", "Bloedel-Donavan Lumber Mills", "Carl Furst Stove", "Burrel Collins").
