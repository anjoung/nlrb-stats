p. 218. The crop is the first page of a multi-page table and is cut off at the bottom: the Region IV block is incomplete (it ends mid-block at "North Carolina", with no Region IV subtotal), and the remaining regions and the national total continue on the following page(s). No footnote text is printed on this crop; the raised "2" on the stub heading "Standard Federal Regions" is a marker whose text appears elsewhere, so `footnotes` is empty. Seventeen columns: stub, "All cases", eight unfair-labor-practice columns (All C cases, CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CG, CP), four representation columns (All R cases, RC, RM, RD), then UD, AC and UC. The three right-hand headings ("Union deauthorization cases", "Amendment of certification cases", "Unit clarification cases") sit over a second heading level giving their codes UD, AC and UC; the stub heading, "All cases" and the code columns span all three heading levels and are repeated accordingly. Every data row and every region subtotal was checked against the printed figures and is internally consistent (component columns sum to "All C cases" and "All R cases", and those plus UD, AC and UC sum to "All cases"; the state rows sum to each Region line), so no misprints are suspected. The one value that looks anomalous at a glance, West Virginia CC = 263 (far larger than any other CC entry), is confirmed by the row and column totals: 427+261+263+5+3+0+9 = 968, and the Region III CC total of 440 requires it.

Continuation crop: the table starts mid-way through the Region IV block (South Carolina is the first printed row; earlier States of that region are on the previous page) and is cut off at the bottom in the middle of the Region VIII block (Colorado through Wyoming are printed, but that region's total row falls below the crop). No footnote texts are printed on this crop, so `footnotes` is empty. Stub heading marker: "Standard Federal Regions" carries a small raised character that I read as a superscript 2; at this scan resolution it could also be a raised 1 or a raised letter "a". The marker is recorded as "^2" in all three header levels. Suspected misprint, North Dakota row: the Representation-cases subcolumns RC 18 + RM 1 + RD 5 sum to 24, but the printed "All R cases" cell for that row is 23. The 23 is the value consistent with the rest of the row (All C 14 + All R 23 + UD 0 + AC 0 + UC 2 = All cases 39, as printed), so one of the three subcolumn digits is probably a misprint or a scan artifact; all four cells are transcribed exactly as printed. Every other row on the page is internally consistent (All cases = All C + All R + UD + AC + UC; All C = CA+CB+CC+CD+CE+CG+CP; All R = RC+RM+RD). Two faint single digits in the bottom block sit between baselines and were ambiguous on first reading: the UD column for Montana (6) and for North Dakota (0). The assignment given here is the one that makes both rows add to their printed "All cases" totals (Montana 110+71+6+0+4 = 191; North Dakota 14+23+0+0+2 = 39); the alternative pairing fails both checks. The Oklahoma "RC" cell is printed with a broken second digit that reads as "4l"; it is transcribed as 41, which is what the Region VI subtotal supports. A stray ink speck sits just left of the Iowa "All C cases" value (198); it is not a footnote marker and is not transcribed.

p. 220. The crop is cut off at the bottom edge: a line of footnote text is partly visible below the table's closing rule but is sliced through and not legible, so `footnotes` is left empty rather than reconstructed. At least one footnote marker is printed on the table (the raised character after the stub heading "Standard Federal Regions"), so the table does carry footnotes that this crop does not show in full. The raised character on the stub heading is read as a 2 ("Standard Federal Regions^2"); it is small and slightly broken in the scan, so a 1 cannot be entirely excluded. This crop continues the table from the previous page: the "Region VIII" line is a region total whose component States appear on the earlier page, so no State rows precede it here. "Region IX" and "Region X" are likewise total lines printed after their State rows.
