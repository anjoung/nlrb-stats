p. 182. Grid is 11 columns: stub plus five two-column panels (All R, NLRA R, RC, RM, RD), each panel "Number of cases" / "Percent of cases closed". Decimal points: this is a 1-bit scan in which the period in the percent columns is frequently dropped, so most percents image as e.g. "75 9", "100 0", "1 4". The percent columns are uniformly one-decimal (several cells, such as All R "100. 0", RM "Dismissed" 18.1 and NLRA "Dismissed" 27.7, do retain a visible point), so the decimal point has been restored throughout the percent columns. No digits were altered. Zero percent cells: the convention in this table is inconsistent in the scan — some zero percents show a visible point (".0", e.g. NLRA "Recognition" and "Otherwise" before formal action) while others image as a bare "0". Cells read as a bare "0" are transcribed "0": RD "Recognition" and RD "Otherwise" (before formal action); NLRA "Dismissed", RD "Stipulated election" and RD "Recognition" (before hearing); NLRA/RM/RD "Recognition" and RM "Dismissed" (after hearing); RM "Withdrawn" (after Board decision). Some of these may be printed ".0". Footnote-reference cells: "(^2)" is a standalone parenthesised raised 2 (= "Less than 0.1 percent") filling the whole cell, not a value with an attached marker. It occurs at "Recognition" before hearing (All R percent, RC percent) and at "Recognition" and "Dismissed" after hearing (RC percent). Arithmetic verification: every count column reconciles exactly — all five panels' subtotals sum to their section totals and to the grand total (e.g. All R 7,017 + 2,228 = 9,245; 267 + 255 + 1,706 = 2,228; 862 + 717 + 94 + 33 = 1,706), so the number columns are well supported.
