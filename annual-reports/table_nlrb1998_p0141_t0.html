p. 141. Scan quality: the decimal points in the "Percent of total cases" column are not resolved anywhere on this page (every value prints as e.g. "100 0", "17 9", "0 9"). These are one-decimal percentages, so they are transcribed with the decimal point restored ("100.0", "17.9", "0.9"); the digits themselves are legible and unambiguous. Likewise, sentence periods are faint or dropped in several labels: the section head for A reads "A Charges filed against employers under Sec. 8(a)" (period after the "A" not visible, though "B." in the parallel head below is clear), and "Subsections of Sec 8(a)/(b)" and "under Sec 8(b)" show no period after "Sec". Periods have been supplied in these labels to match the clearly printed forms elsewhere in the table. "Recapitulation" carries a raised footnote marker 1, but no footnote text is printed within this crop (the area below the last body row is blank down to the closing rule), so `footnotes` is empty; the note text presumably sits on the facing/next page. There is extra vertical white space between the 8(b)(1)(3) and 8(b)(1)(5) rows, which could suggest a dropped 8(b)(1)(4) line. No text or digits are visible there, and the printed detail lines in section B sum exactly to the printed total of 6,726 (and section A's detail lines sum exactly to 23,630), so no row appears to be missing; the gap is only spacing. The internal arithmetic of both panels and of the Recapitulation block checks out against the printed values. Stray specks/ink marks appear in the stub beside "Subsections of Sec. 8(a)", "Total cases", "8(a)(1)(2)", "8(a)(1)(2)(3)" and "8(a)(1)(2)(4)(5)", and below the last row; they are scan noise, not footnote markers or leader dots, and are not transcribed. The column heading is printed broken as "Number of cases show- / ing-specific allegations" and is joined here with the end-of-line hyphen repaired.

Scan quality: this page is a degraded 1998 scan in which fine punctuation largely drops out. In the Percent column every value is printed with a decimal point that renders as a blank gap (image shows "85 0", "100 0", "0 3", etc.); I have restored the decimal points, since all figures are percentages and every panel's total reads 100.0. Every percent is internally consistent with its base (e.g. 52/669 = 7.8, 37/121 = 30.6, 73/121 = 60.3), which confirms the placement. The same dropout affects sentence periods in the footnote (after "Act" and at the end), which I have likewise restored. Minor stray specks appear in the body (left of "8(b)(5)", in the blank stub area beside 8(b)(4)(B) and 8(b)(7)(C), and in the left margin beside 8(b)(7)(A)); none are footnote markers and none obscure a digit.
