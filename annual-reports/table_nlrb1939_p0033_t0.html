The stub has no printed heading, so cell 0 of both heading levels is empty; "Number of cases" and "Number of workers involved" are tall single headings and are repeated across both levels. Cells shown with dash rules (e.g. the "Total cases closed" percentage column for the pending/received/docket rows, and the "Total cases on docket" percentage columns for the "Total cases closed July 1, 1938 to June 30, 1939" row) are transcribed as "". The four rows with no values at all ("By dismissal before hearing", "By dismissal after hearing", "By intermediate report finding no violation", "By compliance with intermediate report") carry dash rules across all six value columns in the original. Percentages under 1 are printed without a leading zero (".8", ".5", ".2", ".1", ".9", ".4") and are transcribed as printed. "By settlement after hearing" shows 23 cases but only 271 workers involved, far fewer than the 736 workers for the 4 cases settled before hearing.
