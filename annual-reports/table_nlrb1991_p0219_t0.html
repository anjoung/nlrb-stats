p. 219. Grid is 15 columns: stub, Number eligible to vote, Total elections, Percent of total, Cumulative percent of total, then Number/Percent pairs for AFL-CIO unions, Teamsters, Other national unions, Other local unions, and Elections in which no representative was chosen. Single panel, three heading levels. Crop: the page is cut off below the "2,000 to 2,999" row, so section A continues (further size classes) and any later sections and footnotes are not on this image. "A. Certification elections (RC and RM)" is printed as a centered banner across the data columns; it is given here as a label-only row. Dash rules ("——") in percent and cumulative cells are transcribed as "", including the Cumulative percent cell of the "Total RC and RM elections" row and the two trailing cells of "2,000 to 2,999" (the Other local unions percent cell there is a very faint rule, but it carries no value either way). Decimal points lost in the 1-bit scan, restored here (each verified against column arithmetic): Percent of total for "70 to 79" prints as "26" (= 2.6, since 80.9 − 78.3 = 2.6); Percent of total for "500 to 599" prints as "04" (= 0.4); AFL-CIO Percent by size class for "400 to 499" prints as "02" (= 0.2); Cumulative percent for "1,000 to 1,999" prints as "100 0" (= 100.0). Stray ink specks treated as scan noise, not footnote markers or digits: a dot before "47" (100 to 109, Total elections); a short dash before "1.5" (120 to 129, Percent of total); a dot before "1" (2,000 to 2,999, Total elections); dots before "1" in Other national unions Number for "90 to 99" and "600 to 799"; a dot before "4" in Teamsters Number for "170 to 179". Arithmetic checks all pass: every row's five Number cells sum to its Total elections; the five Number columns sum to the printed totals 942, 391, 59, 98 and 1,689; Total elections sums to 3,179; Number eligible to vote sums to 195,025; and the Percent of total column matches the first differences of the Cumulative percent column. The one inconsistency is Other local unions "Percent of size class" for "20 to 29", printed 14.4 where 14/98 = 14.3 — transcribed as printed. Heading wording as printed: the Other local unions percent heading reads "Percent of size class" while the other four read "Percent by size class". "AFL–CIO" is set with an en dash and is written here as "AFL-CIO".

Two percent cells disagree with the arithmetic implied by the counts and are either misprints or digits I could not resolve at this resolution: 'Under 10' / Teamsters Percent by size class reads 11.5 (5 of 43 would round to 11.6), and '60 to 69' / AFL-CIO Percent by size class reads 5.6 (7 of 122 would round to 5.7); both are given as read. '800 and over' / Percent of total reads 0.4 (2 of 573 would round to 0.3), but it is consistent with the printed cumulative column reaching 100.0, so it is as printed. Dash rules ('------') in the Cumulative percent cell of the total row and in the percent cells where the count is 0 are transcribed as empty.
