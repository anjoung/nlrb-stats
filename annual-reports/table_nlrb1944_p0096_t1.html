Reader’s notes

p. 96. Eight columns: stub, a tall "Total^2 number of polls", a two-column "Results of polls" spanner, a tall "Total number eligible to vote", and a three-column "Valid votes cast" spanner. The tall headings and the stub heading are repeated in both header levels, and the spanners are repeated in each column they cover. Footnote marker 3 is printed as a raised 3 to the LEFT of the values 58 (Total row) and 48 (A. F. of L. affiliate row), in the "Voted against interruption of work" column; per instructions it is written after the number as 58^3 and 48^3. The marker is clearly raised and set off from the digits, so there is no ambiguity about the numbers themselves. The last column is printed "Votes against interrup-tion to work" — "to work" rather than the "of work" used in the parallel heading two columns earlier. Transcribed as printed; this looks like an original typesetting inconsistency, not a misreading. All columns foot correctly against the Total row (e.g. 311+27+43=381; 61,244+34,103+34,314=129,661; 10,855+6,612+10,779=28,246), which corroborates the digits read. Nothing is cut off by the crop.
