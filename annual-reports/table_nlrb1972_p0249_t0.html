p. 249. 16 columns: stub + "All C cases" (Number, Percent of total closed, Percent of total method) + Number/Percent-of-total-closed pairs for CA, CB, CC, CD, CE, CP. The crop ends mid-table: the last printed row is "Contested" under "Board decision", and the "Compliance with" block (1,085 for All C vs. 9 + 616 shown) clearly continues onto the next page. No footnote texts are printed on this crop, so `footnotes` is empty even though footnote markers 1 (caption, excluded) and 2 (CD column) are referenced. Decimal points: this 1-bit scan drops many of the small periods in the percent columns, so the same value prints as "100.0" in one column and "100 0" in the next. I have written a decimal point in every percent cell where the scan shows a digit-space-digit pattern (e.g. "22 8" -> "22.8", "0 0" -> "0.0"); no digits were added or changed. Doubtful cells: - "Trial examiner's decision" row, CC cases / Percent of total closed: the scan shows a bare "0" where every neighbouring cell in the row shows two digits. It is likely "0.0" or "0.1" (1/1,928 = 0.05 pct) with a character lost; transcribed as the single "0" that is visible. - "Before issuance of complaint" row, CD cases / Number: the cell holds a parenthesised raised 2, i.e. a footnote reference standing in place of a value, transcribed as "(2)". The column arithmetic (5 = 0 + 5 + 0) implies the suppressed value is 0. - "After issuance of complaint, before opening of hearing" (under Informal settlement), CA cases / Percent of total closed: read as "6.1", but 1,033/16,725 = 6.18 pct, which would normally print as 6.2; the glyph could be a 2. Flagged rather than corrected. - "Informal settlement" row, CE cases / Percent of total closed: read as "17.8", but 14/79 = 17.72 pct, which would print as 17.7. Either a 7/8 misread in this scan or a misprint in the original. Other printed percentages are internally inconsistent in the original (some rounded up, some truncated, e.g. 59/16,725 = 0.35 printed as "0.3" but 2,835/16,725 = 16.95 printed as "17.0"); all are transcribed as printed. All Number columns were checked against their own subtotals and are internally consistent.

p. 250. Continuation page: no column headings are printed on this crop, so header_rows is empty. The crop also begins mid-section — the first two rows ("Circuit court of appeals decree", "Supreme Court action") are the tail of a preceding block (compliance/agreement section) whose heading and earlier rows are on the previous page. Grid is 16 columns: stub plus 15 numeric columns. The numeric columns run as a 3-column total group (number, percent of all cases, percent within the disposition method) followed by six number/percent pairs for the individual case types; the headings identifying those groups are not on this crop. Faded decimal points: the 300-ppi scan drops many periods in the percent columns, printing e.g. "0 2", "0 4", "2 5", "100 0", "41 3", "0 7", "0 8", "2 2", "0 1", "0 0". These are decimal points lost in the scan (every percent column carries one decimal place), and they are transcribed with the point restored (0.2, 0.4, 2.5, 100.0, 41.3, 0.7, 0.8, 2.2, 0.1, 0.0). No value's digits were altered. The cells at "Before issuance of complaint" (both the Withdrawal and the Dismissal sections), column 10 (the first number column of the 5th case-type pair, the CD column per footnote 2), hold a parenthesized raised 2 in place of a value; transcribed as "(2)". The raised marker itself is faint and could be read as a 3, but footnote 2's text about CD cases closed in this stage confirms it is 2. The footnote marker printed at the start of the second footnote line is likewise typeset faintly and reads as "3" on the scan; it is footnote 2. Blank vs. dash-rule cells: in the "Adopting trial examiner's decision (no exceptions filed)" row, columns 11, 13 and 15 appear to be left blank rather than carrying the dash rule used elsewhere; either way they are transcribed as "". Row 6's label is printed "After hearing opened. before trial examiner's decision" — the comma after "opened" has scanned as a period. Transcribed with a comma to match the identically-worded row 12. Row 19 ("10(k) actions"): columns 3 through 9 and 12 through 15 carry dash rules or are blank; only the total number/percent and the CD pair (598, 94.9) are filled. No arithmetic was checked or corrected; all values are as printed.
