Reader’s notes

p. 326. 12 columns: stub, 5 "Number of proceedings" columns and 4 "Percentages" columns under "Fiscal year 1979", then Number and Percent under the cumulative heading. The stub has no printed heading (the cell is blank apart from a stray ink mark), so the stub cells of the header rows are empty. Scan artifact: this page renders with small dots dropped (e.g. "Fiscal" prints with a dotless i), so periods and decimal points are frequently invisible. The percentage cells appear as "100 0", "64 6", etc.; they are one-decimal percentages and I have transcribed them with the decimal point ("100.0", "64.6"). Likewise I restored the periods in "U.S.", "Sept. 30", "Vs." and in the footnote sentences. No digits were altered. Rows 1, 8 and 12 are the unindented/section-level lines; the rows under each are indented sub-items, kept in the order printed. Row 1 ("Proceedings decided by U.S. courts of appeals") has dash rules in all four percentage columns and in both cumulative columns, transcribed as "". Row 8 ("On petitions for contempt") and its three sub-rows have dash rules in the cumulative Number/Percent columns. Two printed percentages differ slightly from what the counts imply (transcribed as printed, not corrected): "Board orders set aside" under petitions for review shows 17.3 in "Vs. employers only" where 57/328 rounds to 17.4; "Board orders set aside" under the Supreme Court section shows 16.0 in the cumulative Percent column where 36/226 rounds to 15.9. Both choices make the respective columns sum to 100.0. The footnote 2 marker on the "Board dismissal" heading belongs only to the heading in the "Number of proceedings" panel; the "Board dismissal" heading in the Percentages panel carries no marker.
