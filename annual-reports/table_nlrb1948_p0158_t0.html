p. 158. This is an alphabetical appendix list, not a numeric table: two printed columns (case number in the stub, company name beside it), set as two side-by-side panels separated by a vertical rule. Following the panel rule, the left panel's 38 entries (Abercrombie through Cascadian Fruit Shippers) are given first and the right panel's 41 entries (Case, J. I., Co. through Gaylord Container Corp.) follow; no left-panel entry is paired with the right-panel entry printed beside it. No column headings are printed. The three centred lines "II. Representation cases", "A. Cases in which elections were directed" and "1. NLRA—R and RE cases" are section lines spanning both panels; they are transcribed as label-only rows at the top. The third uses an em dash as printed (NLRA—R). Doubtful cell: the stub of the "Burnet-Binford Lumber Co., Inc." row reads "91-R-1328" in the scan. There is no Region 91, so this is very likely a misprint (or an ink artifact) for "9-R-1328"; it is transcribed exactly as printed. Other items transcribed as printed, which may be printing errors in the original: "Bethlehems' Globe Publishing Co., The." (apostrophe after Bethlehems), "Electric Autolite Co." (usually Electric Auto-Lite), "Ferguson Steere Motor Co.", "Clarkton Gramwood Products Co., Inc.". Hyphens in the case numbers are typeset as short dashes in the scan and are rendered here as plain hyphens. End-of-line hyphenation has been repaired (Manufac-turing, Amer-ica, Illi-nois, Divi-sion, Pub-lishing, Upholster-ing, Ship-pers, Elec-tric, Prod-ucts, Manu-facturing, Air-craft, Air-plane, La-tex). The list is alphabetical and continues past the bottom of the crop (it breaks off at the letter G); the preceding and following pages are not shown. A stray short dash appears in the top right margin outside the text block, and scattered specks in the left margin are scan artifacts, not footnote markers.
