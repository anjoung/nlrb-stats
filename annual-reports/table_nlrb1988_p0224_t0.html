Reader’s notes

p. 224. Eleven columns: stub plus Number/Percent pairs for All R, RC, RM, RD and UD cases. The stub heading "Method and stage of disposition" spans both heading levels. In this scan many decimal points in the Percent columns failed to print and appear as a blank gap (e.g. "40 5" in Certification issued, total / RM Percent; "53 8" in the same row's UD Percent; "1 0", "0 4", "0 0", "15 2", "21 8", "44 6", "29 2", "23 5", "38 5", "17 2", "1 8", "1 6", "4 1", "15 7", "0 9", "0 1" elsewhere). These are dropped points, not spaces in the typesetting, so they are transcribed with the decimal point restored; every one is confirmed by recomputing the percentage from the printed Number and the corresponding column total. All Number columns reconcile: the three method totals (Certification issued, By withdrawal, By dismissal) sum to "Total, all" in each of the five Number columns, and each method's component rows sum to its total, so the digits are well supported. "After:" is a section line carrying a label and no values; the indented "Before notice of hearing / After notice of hearing, before hearing closed / After hearing closed, before decision" triplets belong to the Consent election and Stipulated election blocks above them. The caption carries footnote marker 1 (excluded from the grid per instructions); footnote 1 is printed below the table.
